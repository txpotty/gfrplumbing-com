    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Centennial, CO

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. Taste improves fast. We install twin-tank setups or dual-stage units.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Centennial

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Centennial

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Softener Loop Installation in Centennial

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We cut it in for you. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Centennial, CO

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Centennial

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one.     Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Centennial, CO

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. GFR Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.
