    What Goes Into a Sewer Lateral Replacement in Centennial, CO

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Centennial, CO

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. Many mid-century houses got it, and by now most of it has blistered and flattened enough to snag waste. Clay holds up better, except at the joints. When the camera shows trouble in three or more places, a full replacement is usually cheaper than chasing spot repairs.

    What Sewer Replacement Costs in Centennial, CO

    Four things set the price: length, depth, access and method. Trenching an open yard tends to be cheapest per foot, and bursting costs more per foot but avoids tearing out concrete. Driveways change the math fast. We write up every workable option with its own total after the camera run.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Centennial, CO

    Concrete is expensive to replace. Bursting lets us run new HDPE under a driveway, patio or mature tree by pulling it through the path of the old pipe from a launch pit to a receiving pit. We fuse the HDPE into one continuous length on the lawn. Most bursts finish in a day. You end up patching two small pits rather than a whole slab.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Centennial, CO

    Two holes instead of one long trench. With bursting, a steel head splits the old pipe into the soil and pulls new HDPE into the same path. Lining is the other trenchless route. Lining puts a resin tube inside the old pipe and cures it hard, but only if the host pipe is still mostly round and has no major sags.

    Sewer Permits and Code in Centennial, CO

    Sewer replacement nearly always needs a permit. Our office files the permit and books the inspection, and our crew meets the inspector so you do not have to. Code covers pipe size, fall, bedding and cleanout placement. Unpermitted sewer work can show up on a home inspection when you sell.

    Emergency Sewer Replacement in Centennial, CO

    Sewage coming up a floor drain is an emergency.     Commercial Sewer Lateral Work in Centennial, CO

    Apartment buildings, offices and restaurants in Centennial, CO need lateral work too. Commercial lines carry more volume and more grease, so we size the pipe, place cleanouts for regular jetting, and coordinate grease interceptor connections with the inspector.     How Pipe Bursting Works in Centennial

    A winch pulls the bursting head. Because the head is a little wider than the old pipe, it cracks it into the surrounding dirt as the new HDPE follows. You get a pit at each end. With no joints in the new pipe, roots have no way back in.
