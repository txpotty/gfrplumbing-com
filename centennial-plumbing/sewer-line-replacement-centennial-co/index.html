    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Centennial, CO

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement swaps out pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is ideal when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Centennial and can often be completed faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Centennial, CO

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the correct grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we plan for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we cover it.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Centennial

    Many older homes in Centennial may have sewer lines made from outdated materials like clay or cast iron, which are prone to cracking, corrosion, and root invasion. We offer upgrades to newer, more durable materials such as PVC or HDPE (high-density polyethylene).     Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Centennial, CO

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive option to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining installs a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods greatly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Centennial, CO while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Clay and Orangeburg Line Replacement in Centennial, CO

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, and Orangeburg fiber pipe flattens and blisters with age. Both are near the end of their life in many older properties.
