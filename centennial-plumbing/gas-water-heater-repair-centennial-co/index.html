    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Centennial

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't detect heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a leading reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a vital safety component.

    Our technicians are experienced in repairing all major gas water heater brands, including but not limited to Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith, State and Noritz. We have access to parts for a wide variety of models and manufacturers.

    Common causes include a weak thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube, a clogged flame arrestor or poor combustion air. Drafts in the garage can also snuff out older pilots.

    Look out for a lack of hot water, inconsistent water temperature, a pilot light that won't stay lit, unusual noises like rumbling or popping, a rotten egg smell, which can point to a gas leak, or visible leaks around the tank. Any of these call for a professional inspection.
