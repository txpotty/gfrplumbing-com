    What Our Gas Line Plumbers Fix in Centennial, CO

    CSST Repair in Centennial

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Nails from a remodel, rodents chewing the jacket and nearby lightning strikes are the usual causes, and a damaged run has to be cut out and spliced with the manufacturer's own fittings. We check bonding on every CSST job. The system should be bonded to the electrical grounding system with a proper clamp and conductor, per the manufacturer's instructions and local code.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Centennial

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines harder, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Centennial

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. Our plumbers reseal or replace the leaking fittings and sections using new pipe and approved sealant. Rusted pipe goes too. Pipe in a garage, crawlspace or exterior wall that shows heavy rust gets replaced before it turns into a leak, and the whole system gets a pressure test after the repair.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Centennial

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. We shut off every appliance and watch the meter's test dial, then close branch valves one at a time to find which section is leaking. An electronic detector does the rest. Soap solution at the suspect fitting confirms it with bubbles, and then we repair the joint, replace the section or swap the connector before testing again.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Centennial

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Centennial building officials. We file the permit, schedule the inspection and are on site when the inspector checks the work and the test gauge. Inspection usually happens within a few days. After it passes, the gas company can restore service, and we can return to relight the pilots.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Centennial, CO

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. We size outdoor lines for the full load. Repairs and new runs are tested, buried at code depth and marked with tracer wire.
