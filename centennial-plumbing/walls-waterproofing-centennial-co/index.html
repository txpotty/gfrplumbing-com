    Foundation, Shower and Block Wall Sealing in Centennial, CO

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Centennial

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most dependable way to stop a basement leak, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines slower, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth. Backfill goes in last.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Centennial

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and fasten it with termination bar. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Centennial, CO

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Homes from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may still have polybutylene supply lines, which are prone to fail at fittings. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. Old mortar joints get repointed first.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Centennial

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Centennial take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. One wet electrical room can close a business for days. We schedule around operating hours, work with property managers on access, and follow the waterproofing specs written into the building plans for below-grade walls. We keep downtime short.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Centennial, CO

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while replacing drywall, cabinets and flooring again and again costs far more, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill.     Basement Wall Sealing in Centennial, CO

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Dry air, intense sun, and big daily temperature swings put constant stress on pipes, fittings, and outdoor plumbing in Colorado. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then seal the face with a masonry waterproofer that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Centennial, CO

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and tie it into a sump pit so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. It just stops puddling on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.
