    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Centennial

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Centennial, CO

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Expansive clay soils are a well-known problem in parts of Colorado, swelling with moisture and lifting slabs, basement floors, and buried pipes. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and reset the slope to the quarter inch per foot most codes call for on residential laterals. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Centennial, CO

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and tell you whether sealing or replacing those joints makes more sense than cleaning every spring. Mature trees in Centennial yards make this a common call.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Centennial, CO

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. So does an extra-green strip of lawn. We confirm it on camera, look for cracks, open joints or a broken cleanout riser, and mark the location so the repair stays small. A small crack can sometimes be fixed with a short sectional liner, with no digging at all. Larger breaks get cut out and replaced.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Centennial

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, so by the time a camera shows widespread damage, spot repairs are a poor investment. We replace these lines with PVC. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Centennial, CO

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. One or two joints is a small job. A line rooted end to end is a lining or replacement job.
