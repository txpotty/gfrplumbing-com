    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Centennial

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. It mounts on the main line after any filter and needs no electricity or drain. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Centennial

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. Depending on how old it is, we swap media or the full unit. Following the install specs keeps the warranty intact.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Centennial

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. A carbon prefilter may go first.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Centennial, CO

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Colorado homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Current Fleck valves fit standard tanks.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Centennial, CO

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. Put the unit in bypass first. Most stuck or leaking systems get replaced in a day or two, sooner if the right size is in stock.

    Urgent Softener Problems in Centennial

    Not every softener failure is urgent. Water on the floor from the valve or an overflowing brine tank does count, and those calls get moved up. Turn the bypass handle and wait for us.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Centennial, CO

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. They do keep scale from sticking inside pipes and water heaters. They need no drain line. Swapping to a salt-based unit is an option too.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Centennial

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. They cost more.
