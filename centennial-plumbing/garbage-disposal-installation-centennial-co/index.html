    Replacing an Old Disposal in Centennial, CO

    Old disposals usually come off with a twist of the mounting ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Old gaskets go stiff.     Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Centennial

    New disposals come with a knockout plug in the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. Forgetting the plug floods the dishwasher. We also install an air gap or high loop, so dirty sink water cannot flow back into the dishwasher while the disposal is draining.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Centennial, CO

    A batch-feed disposal will not start until the lid is in, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. You grind one load at a time. Families with small kids often like the trade.

    Same-Day Disposal Installation in Centennial, CO

    When the old disposal dies and the sink is full of water, we can usually install a new one the same day. Our trucks carry common 1/2 and 3/4 HP models, mounting hardware and cord kits, so we do not need a second trip to the supply house before the sink drains again. Figure on roughly an hour.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Centennial

    Batch-feed disposals only run when the stopper lid is in place, which adds safety for families with kids. They need no wall switch, so they are a good choice for kitchens where adding a switch would mean cutting into finished drywall or tile backsplash. Mounting and drains are standard.

    Disposals in Remodels and New Builds in Centennial

    On new builds and remodels in Centennial, we work with your contractor so the disposal, the sink and the dishwasher are planned together.
