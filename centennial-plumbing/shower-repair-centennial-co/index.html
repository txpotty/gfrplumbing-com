    Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Centennial

    A shower that drips after it is turned off typically has a worn cartridge or seals. Hard water scale scratches cartridge surfaces and breaks down O-rings faster than soft water does. We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn easily again.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Centennial, CO

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This brings back full flow to the showerhead.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Centennial, CO

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints.     Low Shower Pressure Repair in Centennial, CO

    Weak shower pressure is often caused by mineral buildup in the showerhead or flow restrictor. It can also come from a clogged cartridge or a failing pressure regulator. We clean or replace the showerhead, check the cartridge and test whole-home pressure when needed. Most low-pressure issues are fixed quickly.

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Centennial
