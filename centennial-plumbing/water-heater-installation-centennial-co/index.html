    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Centennial, CO

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank.     Expansion Tank Installation in Centennial, CO

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Centennial, CO

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. No venting needed.
