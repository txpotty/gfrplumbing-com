    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Centennial

    PEX and CPVC do not carry sound well. For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, often through grout joints and hairline cracks. It catches leaks the microphone cannot in Centennial homes.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Centennial

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or suggest a reroute that skips the slab.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Centennial

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, wearing against the gravel and concrete until a pinhole opens. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Gas or electric bills may jump too.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Centennial

    Rerouting leaves the failed pipe in the slab. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, so there is no concrete work and the cost is often lower than going through tile. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. Tile and hardwood floors make this the favorite.

    Non-Invasive Slab Leak Locating in Centennial, CO

    We touch the floor last.     Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Centennial, CO

    Hot water leaking under the slab warms the floor above it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. It only works on hot lines. We use it to narrow the search before the acoustic check.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Centennial, CO

    Two slab leaks usually means more are coming. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, ending the cycle of floor openings and surprise water bills. Most repipes wrap up in two to four days.

    Direct Access Slab Leak Repair in Centennial, CO
