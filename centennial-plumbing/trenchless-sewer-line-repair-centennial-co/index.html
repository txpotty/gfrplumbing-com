    Sectional Point Repair in Centennial

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are a good fit on otherwise healthy lines.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Centennial

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot get into. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Centennial, CO

    Tree and shrub roots go after moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause repeat backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every couple of months.

    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Centennial

    Before any trenchless repair, we perform a thorough video inspection of your sewer line. This involves inserting a high-resolution camera into the pipe to accurately diagnose the problem's exact location and nature. This key step makes sure we choose the most appropriate and effective trenchless method for your Centennial property, avoiding guesswork.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Centennial

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.
