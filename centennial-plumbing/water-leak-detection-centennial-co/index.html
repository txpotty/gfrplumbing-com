    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in Centennial, CO

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak silently around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are usually quick and save water right away.

    Water Meter Leak Testing in Centennial

    The water meter is the first and simplest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is leaking.

    Main Service Line Leak Detection in Centennial, CO

    The line from the meter to your house carries full pressure and can leak underground for a long time. Wet soil, soft spots or oddly green patches of yard may be the only clues. We trace the line, listen for the leak and mark it for a targeted repair. Replacing old galvanized or polybutylene service lines is usually recommended.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Centennial, CO

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak protects the pool and the soil around it.

    Slab Leak Detection in Centennial

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often signaled by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. GFR Plumbing uses specialized acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is essential to preventing major foundation damage.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Centennial

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for extended periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include unusually green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. GFR Plumbing employs ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.
