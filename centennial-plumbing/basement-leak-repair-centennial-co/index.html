    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Leak Repair in Centennial

    Basement plumbing leak repairs in Centennial typically cost $200 to $1,500, depending on the pipe and how hard it is to reach. Pipe on an open ceiling is the cheap case.     Plumbing leaks show up no matter the weather. Groundwater leaks track the rain. Turn off every fixture and watch the water meter, and if it still moves, the leak is in the pressurized lines, while water that only appears a few hours after a storm is almost always coming from outside.

    Look for damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell, and white chalky deposits on concrete. That white crust is mineral left after water dries. A rising water bill or rust at the water heater base points toward a plumbing leak. Smell often comes first.

    Most supply and drain repairs are finished in a few hours. A new sump takes most of a day. Stack and slab repairs run one to two days because surfaces get opened and patched.

    For sump pumps we mostly install Zoeller and Liberty, with Wayne for some battery backups. We prefer cast iron pump housings.     Leak repair stops a specific source, like a cracked pipe or a leaking wall crack. Waterproofing covers the whole wall. It manages groundwater around the entire foundation with drains, membranes and a sump, and it makes sense when water enters along many points rather than one obvious spot that a single repair can fix.

    Most plumbing leak repairs in a basement are done in one visit. Expect two to four hours with drywall access. Seepage work ranges from half a day for one crack to several days for a perimeter drain.
