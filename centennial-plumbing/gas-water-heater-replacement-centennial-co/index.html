    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Centennial, CO Home

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Centennial, CO

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    No Hot Water Calls in Centennial

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. We check those first. On a tank past ten years, a new heater usually beats a new valve.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Centennial, CO

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. Vent length and elbows stay inside the manufacturer's limits.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Centennial, CO

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Code is stricter here. Sizing comes from peak demand and recovery rate.

    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Centennial, CO

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Self-cleaning dip tubes help on hard water. Powered anodes outlast standard rods. We price it against how long you plan to stay.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Centennial

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. The old tank gets drained and hauled off, and the new one goes on a pan. Every install gets new gas and water connectors. We soap-test the gas joints and check the draft hood with the burner lit.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Centennial

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. A second blower failure on an older tank is usually the point where a new heater makes more sense. A dedicated outlet gets confirmed first.

    Atmospheric Vent Upgrades in Centennial, CO

    Flue pipes on older atmospheric heaters rust at the joints and lose their upward slope, and a loose connector can spill exhaust into the closet. We replace damaged sections and the draft hood if needed. We check draft with a match or mirror at the hood.
