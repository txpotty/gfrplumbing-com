    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Longmont

    The cost of a sump pump replacement in Longmont, CO can range from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, its horsepower and the complexity of the installation.     Sump pumps usually have a lifespan of 7 to 10 years. Even if they appear to be working, their efficiency can decrease over time. Regular inspection and testing are crucial, and replacement is advisable as they approach the end of their expected service life.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that activates if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many Longmont homes benefit from a backup system due to storm risks.

    Simple replacements typically do not require permits. New discharge lines or electrical work may, depending on your city.

    Yes, in addition to replacement, we offer sump pump maintenance services to keep your system ready to run.     To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by GFR Plumbing can also help lengthen its lifespan.
