    Tank and Tankless Water Heaters in Longmont, CO

    Expansion Tank Installation in Longmont, CO

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Longmont, CO

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Our crews set the gas line, the drip leg and the vent connector, then do a draft test at the hood to prove the exhaust is leaving the house. Then we check for leaks. We add an expansion tank if code calls for it.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Longmont, CO

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Tankless jobs cost more mostly because of gas line and vent work, while attic installs, moves and code updates add labor to any type. Permits are included. We compare operating costs so you can choose with real numbers.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Longmont, CO

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most. Every manufacturer sets its own clearance, venting and relief valve rules, and following them is what keeps the warranty good. Our crews know each one. You can compare warranty length and recovery rate with us before you buy.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Longmont

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien each spell out clearances, vent lengths and relief valve placement, and a warranty claim can be denied if those were ignored. Our crews install to spec. That protects your coverage.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Longmont, CO

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Rebates may apply. We will tell you if your space works.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Longmont, CO

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Longmont, CO

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.
