    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in Longmont

    The cost of toilet replacement in Longmont, CO usually ranges from $400 to $800, including the new toilet fixture and labor. This can vary based on the toilet model you choose, any necessary repairs to the flange or subfloor, and the difficulty of the installation.

    Most standard toilet replacements can be completed by a professional plumber in about 2 to 4 hours. This includes the removal of the old unit, preparation of the flange, installation of the new toilet, and testing for leaks. More complex installations may take a bit longer.

    We recommend reliable brands known for their quality, efficiency, and availability of parts. Popular choices include Kohler, American Standard, Toto, Delta, and Gerber. Each offers a range of styles and features, from basic models to advanced low-flow or comfort height options. We can help you select the right fit for your needs.

    Key signs include persistent leaks that cannot be sealed, a cracked bowl or tank, frequent clogging despite repairs, or an old toilet that uses excessive water. If your toilet is constantly running or has visible damage, replacement is often the more cost-effective long-term solution.

    While it is possible to DIY a toilet replacement, we strongly recommend hiring a professional. Improper installation can lead to leaks, water damage, and expensive repairs. Our plumbers make sure of correct sealing, proper flange connection, and adherence to all plumbing codes.

    A like-for-like toilet replacement in the same location generally does not require a permit. If drains are moved or a new bathroom is added, a permit is usually required.

    We install TOTO, Kohler, American Standard, Gerber, Mansfield and other quality brands. We can also install a toilet you have already purchased.

    Signs of failing internal toilet parts include a toilet that runs constantly, a weak or incomplete flush, a handle that needs jiggling, or visible wear and tear on components like the flapper, fill valve, or flush valve.
