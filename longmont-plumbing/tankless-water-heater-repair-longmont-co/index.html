    Ignition Failure Repair in Longmont, CO

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet. Undersized gas lines are a common hidden cause when a tankless unit replaced a tank. We correct the issue so the unit lights reliably.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Longmont, CO

    Rinnai is a popular tankless brand, and its units can face challenges such as scaling from hard water or issues with their flame rod. Our technicians in Longmont, CO are skilled at Rinnai tankless water heater repair, focusing on descaling services, fan motor replacements and fixing issues with gas valve assemblies. We make sure your Rinnai system continues to provide reliable, on-demand hot water, following manufacturer specifications.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Longmont, CO

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and verify proper drainage. PVC venting is also inspected for slope and sagging.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Longmont

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water faster. Pump failures, check valve problems or wrong schedule settings can cause long waits or lukewarm water. We test the pump, check valves and controls and set the schedule to match your daily routine. A properly tuned system saves both water and gas.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Longmont

    Tankless heaters rely on flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace faulty parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.

    Tankless Water Heater Error Code Diagnostics in Longmont, CO

    Modern tankless water heaters display error codes to indicate specific problems, from flow rate issues to combustion errors. Our experts in Longmont, CO are trained to interpret these codes accurately, using specialized diagnostic tools to pinpoint the exact component failure. This precision allows us to quickly order the correct parts and perform targeted repairs, saving you time and money. We address codes related to burner failures, fan issues and temperature sensor errors, and more.

    Navien units are known for their advanced condensing technology but can sometimes develop issues with their circulation pump or exhaust systems. GFR Plumbing provides specialized Navien tankless water heater repair in Longmont, addressing common problems like ignition failures or condensate drain blockages.
