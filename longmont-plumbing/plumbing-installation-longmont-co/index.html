    PEX Pipe Installation in Longmont, CO

    PEX piping has become a popular choice for new plumbing installations and repipes in Longmont, CO due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion. GFR Plumbing installs PEX piping for water supply lines, offering a cost-effective and long-lasting alternative to copper. Its ability to expand makes it more resistant to freeze damage than rigid pipe. Hard freezes are a normal part of winter in Colorado, and pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, and garages are especially vulnerable. We make sure all connections are secure and leak-free.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Longmont, CO

    When plumbing fixtures are located below the main sewer line, such as in a basement bathroom or laundry room, a sewage ejector pump is needed. GFR Plumbing provides expert sewage ejector pump installation in Longmont, CO. We ensure correct sizing of the pump and basin, proper venting, and secure discharge line connection to the main sewer. This complex installation requires specialized knowledge to prevent backups and ensure reliable waste removal. We use durable pumps from trusted brands like Liberty Pumps or Zoeller to guarantee long-term performance.

    Many larger systems in Colorado draw on mountain snowmelt that runs fairly soft, while plains communities and groundwater sources often deliver more mineral-heavy water. Proper bypass valve installation and connection to your main water line are key for easy maintenance and effective operation.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Longmont

    PEX is flexible, resistant to scale and a popular choice for repiping older homes with failing copper or galvanized pipe. We install PEX with manifold or home-run layouts for balanced pressure.
