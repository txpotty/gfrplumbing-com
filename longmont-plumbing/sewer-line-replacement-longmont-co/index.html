    Sewer Line Replacement Options in Longmont, CO

    Replacing a Collapsed Sewer by Trench in Longmont

    Collapsed pipe cannot be burst or lined. When the camera stops at a pile of broken clay or a flattened section of Orangeburg, the only fix is to dig down, clear the debris and lay new pipe through that stretch. Digging also lets us see the soil around the break. Sometimes we find a washout or a root mat that explains why the pipe failed in the first place.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Longmont, CO

    Concrete is expensive to replace. Bursting lets us run new HDPE under a driveway, patio or mature tree by pulling it through the path of the old pipe from a launch pit to a receiving pit. We fuse the HDPE into one continuous length on the lawn. Most bursts finish in a day. Restoration is two small holes instead of a sawcut slab.

    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Longmont

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Roots get into clay joints, cast iron rusts out along the invert, and Orangeburg fiber pipe squashes flat over the years. PVC and HDPE do none of that. HDPE is fused into one continuous piece with no joints for roots to find.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Longmont

    Cast iron rots from the inside out. Sewer gas and moisture eat away the bottom of the pipe first, so the camera often shows a channel worn through along the invert even when the top still looks solid. Rough scale catches everything. Outside the foundation we replace it with PVC or HDPE. For cast iron under a slab, we talk through lining versus rerouting.

    Emergency Sewer Collapse Repair in Longmont

    Every drain in the house stops when a lateral collapses. We get a camera in, find the failure, mark it, and tell you the same day whether we can dig the next morning or need to wait for utility locates to clear. Locates usually take a couple of business days. We can often set up a bypass so one bathroom stays usable.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Longmont, CO

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. Many mid-century houses got it, and by now most of it has blistered and flattened enough to snag waste. Clay holds up better, except at the joints. Several bad spots on one run usually make full replacement the better buy.

    Sewer Permits and Code in Longmont, CO

    Sewer replacement nearly always needs a permit. We file it, schedule the inspection and meet the inspector on site, so you never have to take a morning off to stand in the yard. Code covers pipe size, fall, bedding and cleanout placement. Skipping the permit can come back to bite you at resale.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Longmont

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. After cutting roots and jetting the line, we pull in a resin-filled liner and inflate it until it cures against the old pipe wall. Collapsed pipe will not work. Lining shines under finished basements and slabs. You will get a straight answer on whether yours is a candidate.
