    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. A fault code on the drive points us in the right direction.     Jet Pump System Repair in Longmont, CO

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. GFR Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. Nonstop running without pressure usually means lost prime or an air leak.

    Jet Pump Repair in Longmont, CO

    Shallow wells and booster systems often use jet pumps. Common problems are loss of prime, a leaking shaft seal and a worn impeller. Our crew in Longmont restores prime, replaces worn parts and checks the suction line for air leaks. Where water levels swing through the year, a falling level can starve the pump and cost it prime.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Repair in Longmont

    Expect $300 for a pressure switch and up to $2,000 or more for a submersible motor replacement. Well depth changes the number most. A deep well means more drop pipe and wire to pull and replace, plus more labor time on the hoist, so the same pump costs more to swap in a deeper hole.

    Power surges and worn parts top the list. Sediment in the well and a failing pressure tank or switch also wear pumps out.

    Standard repairs usually do not need a permit in Longmont, CO. A permit may apply to major replacements or new installs.

    Once a year is a good rule. An annual check catches small problems before they turn into a dead pump.

    No water, sputtering faucets and a pump that runs constantly are the big ones. Pressure that swings up and down or a tank that clicks every few seconds also means it is time for a visit.

    Submersible pumps commonly last 8 to 15 years, depending on run time, sand and water quality. A failing pressure tank that causes short cycling can shorten it badly.

    Check the pressure tank air charge once a year. Keep the well cap sealed and watch for short cycling, since a pump that starts too often wears out fast.

    Simple fixes like a pressure switch take a few hours at most. Replacing a submersible pump can take a full day, especially in a deep well.
