    Why Choose GFR Plumbing for Trenchless Pipe Repair in Longmont, CO?

    Traditional pipe replacement can mean digging up concrete, pavers and landscaping that took years to establish. Trenchless repair avoids most of that and is often completed in a single day.

    • Long-Term Value, Avoiding major excavation and restoration usually makes trenchless the cheaper option over the life of the pipe.
    • New Pipe Inside, The liner forms a strong, corrosion-resistant pipe inside your existing line.
    • Root Resistant, New liners and HDPE pipe hold up to roots and rust better than the clay or cast iron they replace.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Longmont

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Pipe Bursting in Longmont, CO

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. It needs two pits. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Sewer Lateral Lining in Longmont

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Old materials crack and sag. Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots over time, so we jet the line clean, inspect it by camera and choose lining or bursting based on what we see. The new line restores full flow and resists roots.

    Commercial Trenchless Pipe Repair in Longmont

    Commercial properties have sewer and storm lines under parking lots, sidewalks and buildings. Tearing it out costs plenty. Trenchless repair avoids that demolition and the disruption to tenants and customers, and we line larger diameter pipe and multiple laterals on a schedule that limits the impact on business.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Longmont

    Older Longmont homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Clay joints let roots in and cast iron scales and rusts through, and a liner seals both problems with a new jointless pipe that often outlasts the original material by decades. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Longmont

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Picote milling heads clear roots, scale and old fittings out of the pipe before lining, and after the liner cures, they cut open the lateral connections so every drain in your home flows again. A clean pipe holds a liner best.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Longmont, CO

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. We burst or bore. New HDPE or PEX goes in along the old path by bursting, or along a new one by directional boring, leaving lawns and drives alone. The line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Longmont

    Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines. Basements are common in Colorado, and shifting soil can crack floor drains, sewer lines, and foundation walls over time. We fix minor cracks with a spot liner. For longer damage we line the whole run, and for a collapse we burst in new pipe, without trenching the yard or cutting the drive.
