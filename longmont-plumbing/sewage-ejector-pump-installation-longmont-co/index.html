    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Longmont

    Digging the pit starts with a saw cut through the floor, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines slower, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth. The basin sits on gravel with backfill around it so rising groundwater cannot float it, and new concrete seals the floor and locks it down. A floating basin breaks pipe.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Longmont

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.
