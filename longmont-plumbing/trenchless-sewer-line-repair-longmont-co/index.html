    Long-Lasting Trenchless Sewer Line Repair Services in Longmont, CO

    Sectional Point Repair in Longmont

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Technologies in Longmont

    GFR Plumbing uses industry-leading trenchless technologies from trusted brands like Perma-Liner and NuFlow. These systems are known for their durability, effectiveness and adherence to plumbing standards. Using these materials and equipment means your trenchless sewer line repair in Longmont is not only minimally invasive but also a high-quality, long-lasting solution, backed by manufacturer warranties.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Longmont

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.

    Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Longmont

    Before any trenchless repair, a detailed sewer camera inspection is essential. Our high-definition cameras travel through your sewer lines, providing real-time video of the pipe's interior. This allows us to accurately diagnose the problem, identify its exact location and determine the most appropriate trenchless method, whether it is pipe bursting or CIPP. This diagnostic step makes for a precise and effective repair in Longmont.

    Pipe Bursting in Longmont, CO

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is best for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Longmont

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Longmont homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Longmont

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Costs in Longmont, CO

    While the upfront cost of trenchless sewer repair in Longmont, CO can sometimes be comparable to or slightly higher than traditional methods, the overall savings are often substantial. This is because trenchless repairs eliminate the need for extensive excavation, backfilling and landscape restoration. Homeowners save money on repairing driveways, patios and gardens, making it a more economical choice in the long run for many.
