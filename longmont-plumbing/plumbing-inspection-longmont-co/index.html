    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Longmont, CO

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Longmont, CO

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Certain types need a plan. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Longmont

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.

    Water Heater Inspections in Longmont

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. We check tankless units for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Longmont, CO

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Old brass connectors, missing shutoffs or drip legs and rust all get noted, and we sweep joints with a gas detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Older homes are where we find the most issues, usually at appliance connectors that have been there since the house was built.

    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Longmont

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. Our pre-purchase inspection covers the supply pipe type, the sewer condition on video, the water heater's age and every fixture. We send the report to your agent too. Many buyers use our findings to ask for repairs or credits, and the sewer video alone has saved buyers from five-figure surprises.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Longmont, CO

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Commercial Property Plumbing Checks in Longmont
