    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Longmont

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings.     PEX Pipe Installation in Longmont, CO

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Labor runs lower with PEX. We keep PEX out of direct sunlight, away from water heater flues and recessed light housings and support it at the spacing the manufacturer calls for, which protects both the pipe and the warranty.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Longmont

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Leaving some poly keeps the risk.     Copper Pipe Replacement in Longmont

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. It is rigid, handles heat well and has decades of history in homes. Plan on a higher price. We review your water and any history of copper pinholes nearby before recommending it over PEX.

    Why Polybutylene Fails in Longmont

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Many larger systems in Colorado draw on mountain snowmelt that runs fairly soft, while plains communities and groundwater sources often deliver harder water. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.
