    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Longmont

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak without risk or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Emergency Slab Leak Detection in Longmont

    A sudden drop in water pressure, visible dampness or the sound of running water can indicate an emergency slab leak. GFR Plumbing offers emergency detection services in Longmont to address urgent situations quickly. We prioritize rapid response to limit water damage and prevent further structural compromise to your property.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Longmont, CO

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to see temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area quickly without any physical disruption to your property in Longmont.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Longmont

    Our technicians rely on highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the subtle sound of escaping water, even through concrete. It's particularly effective for pressurized water lines, allowing us to find leaks without unnecessary excavation and minimizing disruption to your Longmont home.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Longmont, CO

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of repeat slab leaks. We can usually complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Commercial Slab Leak Detection in Longmont, CO

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Longmont

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the precise location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's particularly effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Longmont homes.

    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Longmont, CO

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most affordable repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring. Accurate detection keeps that opening as small as possible.
