    Common Plumbing Repairs in Longmont, CO

    Slow Drain Repair in Longmont

    Gurgling toilets and a tub that drains slow often trace to a blocked vent or a sagging drain line. We clear the line, camera it and repair the damaged section. Common fixes include replacing corroded cast iron, cutting out failed ABS joints, rebuilding a broken trap arm and resetting toilets on a new wax ring and flange. The goal is a drain you stop thinking about.

    Slab Leak Repair for Longmont, CO Homes

    Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines slower, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth. Lines under a slab take the brunt of that movement, and Colorado homeowners often find out from a warm floor or a spinning meter. GFR Plumbing finds the leak with electronic equipment before any concrete is cut. Then the choice is a spot repair through the floor or a reroute that abandons the old line. Reroutes skip the jackhammer.

    Gas Line Leak Repair in Longmont

    Gas work is the one job no homeowner should try. A sulfur smell or a hissing fitting means leave first and call from outside. GFR Plumbing finds and fixes gas leaks in Longmont with a combustible gas detector, then pressure tests the line to code before the meter is turned back on. Flex connectors fail most often.

    Gas Pipe Repair in Longmont, CO

    Leave the house first if you smell gas, and call the gas utility from outside. After the utility makes it safe, we come in for the repair. The system gets a pressure test, the leak gets traced with a detector and bubble solution, and the failed connector or pipe gets replaced. Nothing is relit until the test holds.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Longmont, CO

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the path of the old one, and cured-in-place lining builds a new pipe inside the existing pipe. Neither one needs a trench across the driveway. Once restoration is counted, the price is often close to a deep dig. A camera decides.

    Gas Leak Repair in Longmont

    Rotten-egg odor near the range or a hiss at the meter means get out of the house first. Phone the gas company from the yard, then call us to fix it. Our plumbers locate the leak with an electronic detector and soap solution, then replace the failed connector, valve or section of pipe to code. No shortcuts on gas.

    Well Pump Repair in Longmont, CO

    No water at the tap on a well system points to a short list of parts. It is usually the pressure switch, the capacitor in the control box, the pump motor or a waterlogged pressure tank. Voltage and amp readings at the control box tell us whether the pump needs to come up. Pulling a submersible pump is a two-person job.

    Well Pump Service in Longmont, CO

    Submersible pumps sit deep in the casing, so diagnosis happens at the surface first. GFR Plumbing checks the pressure switch, the control box capacitor and the tank precharge, then measures amp draw on the motor. High amp draw usually means a worn motor or a seized impeller. Jet pumps are simpler, and most of the time a bad foot valve or a lost prime is to blame.
