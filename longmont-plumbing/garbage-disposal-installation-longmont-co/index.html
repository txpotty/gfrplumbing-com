    Every Kind of Disposal Install, Done Right in Longmont, CO

    Standard Disposal Installation in Longmont

    Most households in Longmont do well with a continuous-feed unit between 1/3 and 1/2 horsepower. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Then we wire it and test it. A smooth spin and a clean drain tell us it is right.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Longmont, CO

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. We lay out the drain so both bowls drain freely and the disposal outlet does not push water into the other bowl, which happens when the tee sits too close to the disposal discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.

    Disposal Upgrades in Longmont, CO

    Stepping up from an old 1/3 HP unit to a quieter model is a common request. We disconnect and remove the old disposal, check the trap and drain arm for corrosion, and install the new unit with a new mount, so the upgrade does not inherit the old unit's worn parts. Corroded drain arms get swapped. Galvanized arms rust from the inside.

    Same-Day Disposal Installation in Longmont, CO

    When the old disposal dies and the sink is full of water, we can usually install a new one the same day. Our trucks carry common 1/2 and 3/4 HP models, mounting hardware and cord kits, so we do not need a second trip to the supply house before the sink drains again. Most swaps take about an hour.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Longmont

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. We also check the circuit. Bigger motors draw more at startup.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Longmont

    Batch-feed disposals only run when the stopper lid is in place, which adds safety for families with kids. Because they need no wall switch, they suit kitchens where a new switch would mean cutting drywall or a tile backsplash. Mounting and drains are standard.

    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Longmont

    With a typical 1/2 or 3/4 HP unit, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink. We connect it to the drain, set the P-trap and wire it, then check the clearance under the sink, which can be tight in older Longmont homes with shallow cabinets or a water filter mounted on the back wall. Tight cabinets need planning. Sometimes a compact model is the better fit.

    InSinkErator Model Selection in Longmont, CO

    Choosing between Badger and Evolution is where we start based on how you cook, how much noise bothers you and how much cabinet room you have. Badger units are compact and simple, while Evolution units are taller and heavier, with more grind stages and better sound insulation. The cabinet usually settles it. We measure before you buy.
