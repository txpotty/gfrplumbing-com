    Budget Tub Faucet Options in Longmont, CO

    Not every tub faucet needs a full valve. A cartridge plus a same-brand trim kit can refresh an old faucet for far less than a new valve. That option comes up first. Three-handle faucets are the exception.

    Upgrading Tub Faucets in Longmont, CO

    Plenty of people replace a working tub faucet for looks. A dated chrome cross-handle set can become a brushed nickel or matte black lever trim, and if the valve is a newer universal rough-in, the swap happens without opening the wall at all. Matching the sink faucet is easy. Delta and Moen make this simplest.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Longmont

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. That cartridge wears out and causes drips or a handle that will not reach full hot, and swapping it with a cartridge puller takes about an hour when the valve body is in good shape. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.

    Tub Valves in Older Houses in Longmont, CO

    Cast iron tubs and plaster walls change the plan. Plaster cracks wider than drywall when you cut it, so we open plaster carefully with a multi-tool and often go in from an adjacent closet, and the heavy tub stays put while we work on the valve above it. Patching plaster is slow. Access panels avoid it.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Longmont, CO

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. They adjust flow when a toilet flushes so the mix holds, and thermostatic valves hold an exact temperature. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Longmont

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Longmont, CO

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Seat wrenches come out often. About an hour, usually.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Longmont, CO

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.
