    Channel and Trench Drains in Longmont, CO

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send storm runoff straight into a garage or under a door. A channel drain with a grate catches that sheet flow before it reaches the building. We use durable polymer or concrete channels from brands like NDS and set them flush with the surface. The channel ties into solid pipe that carries the water to a proper outlet.

    Kitchen, Bath and Laundry Drain Clearing in Longmont

    Slow sinks, gurgling tubs and overflowing laundry standpipes usually come from grease, hair, soap scum and mineral scale. Many larger systems in Colorado draw on mountain snowmelt that runs fairly soft, while plains communities and groundwater sources often deliver more mineral-heavy water.     Downspout and Roof Drain Solutions in Longmont

    Gutters and scuppers concentrate a lot of roof water into a few points, often right next to the foundation.
