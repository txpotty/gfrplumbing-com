    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Longmont

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. Each install gets a new gas connector and drip leg, a vent connector pitched upward and a relief line piped where code allows. We strap it if code calls for it. Your household sets the size.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Longmont

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. Most conversions need a bigger gas line from the meter, a new vent through the wall or roof, a condensate drain and isolation valves for future descaling. It takes most of a day. Our licensed plumbers handle all of it to code.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in Longmont, CO

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. GFR Plumbing handles every Rheem gas model.     Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Longmont

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Most run without electricity. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Longmont, CO

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. They need an outlet. We terminate the vent to spec.
