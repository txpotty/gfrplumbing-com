    Leaking P-Trap Repair in Longmont

    Most puddles under a bathroom sink start at the P-trap. Slip-joint washers flatten over time, plastic traps crack, and old chrome traps rust through from the inside. We replace the trap with a correctly sized 1-1/4 inch assembly, align the tailpiece and wall arm, and run water to confirm a dry seal. This is one of the fastest and least expensive fixes we do.

    Sink Water Pressure Issues in Longmont, CO

    Low water pressure at your sink can be frustrating and indicate underlying plumbing problems such as mineral buildup in the aerator, faulty faucet cartridges, or even larger issues within your home's water lines. Our technicians in Longmont, CO can accurately diagnose the cause of low pressure and make the necessary repairs to restore full water flow to your sink.

    Pop-Up Drain and Stopper Repair in Longmont, CO

    When the stopper will not close, will not open or lets water slowly escape, the pop-up linkage is typically to blame. The pivot rod nut can leak, the clevis strap can slip, and hair wraps around the stopper body. We clean or replace the assembly and adjust it so the lift rod works smoothly. If the drain body itself is corroded, we replace it with a matching finish.

    Overflow and Drain Body Leak Repair in Longmont, CO

    Water that appears only when the sink is full often comes from the overflow gasket or the drain flange putty. We pull the drain, clean the old putty or sealant, and reset the flange with fresh plumber's putty or silicone based on the basin material. Then we fill the bowl past the overflow and check for drips. This kind of leak is easy to miss and easy to fix once we find it.

    Fixing Loose or Wobbly Sinks in Longmont

    Over time, sinks can become loose or wobbly, especially if they are undermounted or pedestal styles. This can be due to failing caulk, loose mounting clips, or even issues with the countertop. Our plumbers in Longmont can re-secure your sink, making sure it's stable and properly sealed to prevent water infiltration and further damage to your cabinetry or flooring.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Longmont, CO

    Sometimes, a full sink replacement isn't necessary; only specific parts need attention. GFR Plumbing offers precise sink parts replacement services in Longmont, CO, including P-traps, supply lines, aerators, strainers, and pop-up stoppers. We use high-quality, durable components that match your existing fixtures and meet all local plumbing codes. This focused approach saves you money and extends the life of your current sink, making it a cost-effective repair option.

    Slow Drain and Clog Clearing in Longmont

    Bathroom sinks clog with hair, soap scum and toothpaste mixed with mineral scale. We pull the stopper, clean the overflow channel, and clear the trap and branch line with a hand auger instead of caustic chemicals. If the clog sits past the wall, we check whether a shared line with the tub or shower is the actual problem. You end up with a sink that drains at full speed.

    Sink Repair vs Replacement Costs in Longmont, CO

Replacement starts to make sense when the basin is cracked, the finish is badly etched, or the drain body is corroded into the bowl. We give you both prices when it is close so you can decide.
