    Sewer Line Slab Leak Repair in Longmont, CO

    Slab leaks aren't always about clean water pipes, since sewer lines can also leak under your foundation. These leaks pose significant health risks and can cause extensive damage. We specialize in detecting and repairing sewer line slab leaks, using camera inspections to identify the exact problem area. Our methods ensure proper waste removal and prevent contamination in your Longmont, CO property, following health and safety codes.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Longmont

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is simplest. Reroutes eliminate future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Longmont, CO

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most practical repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. Our team makes sure the repair is fully compliant with Colorado plumbing codes and the slab is properly restored, preventing future issues. We take great care to contain dust and debris during this process in your Longmont home.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Longmont

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Longmont with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Longmont, CO

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to limit wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.

    Slab Leak Detection in Longmont

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to confirm a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to find out which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.

    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Longmont, CO

    A large slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. We help you shut off the water and send a technician to isolate the leaking line. Often we can restore water to the rest of the house while we plan the repair. Quick action limits flooring damage and soil saturation.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Longmont, CO

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a sensible option. We cut a small opening, dig down to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.
