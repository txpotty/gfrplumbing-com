    Propane Gas Line Service in Fort Collins, CO

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Fort Collins, CO

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. We install the branch line, add an accessible shutoff valve and a sediment trap where the code calls for one, then connect the appliance with a new coated flexible connector. Leaking existing lines get repaired during the same trip.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Fort Collins, CO

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. Once the coating cracks, the pipe starts to corrode, and wet soil from sprinklers or a low spot in the yard speeds that up a great deal. Utilities get marked first. We lay polyethylene with tracer wire or coated steel where exposed, then backfill and clean up after the test passes.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Fort Collins, CO

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. CSST can be damaged by nails, rodents or lightning, and older yellow-jacket CSST needs proper bonding to the electrical grounding system to reduce the risk of a lightning arc. It takes about an hour. Damaged runs get spliced with the manufacturer's fittings, and we confirm the bond on the way out. Spring snowmelt raises groundwater in Colorado, which can push water into basements and overwork sump pumps.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Fort Collins

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. Usually under an hour per appliance. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.
