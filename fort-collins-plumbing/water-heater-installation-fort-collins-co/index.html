    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Fort Collins, CO

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Rebates may apply. We help you decide if one fits.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Fort Collins

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. A licensed plumber who handles permits keeps the work inspected, which protects your warranty, insurance coverage, and resale value. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Fort Collins, CO

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Fort Collins

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. We size the tank by first-hour rating, not just gallons, because that number tells you how much hot water it delivers in a busy morning. Gas or electric both work. The fuel hookup, the venting and haul-away are all part of the job.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Fort Collins

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien each spell out clearances, vent lengths and relief valve placement, and a warranty claim can be denied if those were ignored. Our crews install to spec. That protects your coverage.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Fort Collins, CO

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Fort Collins

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. We schedule around your hours. Night installs are available.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Fort Collins, CO

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Leak checks follow. We add an expansion tank if code calls for it.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Fort Collins

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank typically runs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless jobs run about $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and vent needs.

    Our crews install Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai, all brands with solid parts support and decent tank warranties. We recommend based on price and use.

    Tank heaters usually last 10 to 15 years, and tankless units can go 20 or more. Hard water and skipped maintenance shorten that, while yearly flushing and anode checks stretch it a good deal past the average. Flush it once a year.

    Doing it yourself means handling gas, 240-volt power and pressurized water at once. Mistakes can cause gas leaks, CO exposure or electrical fires, and a DIY install can void the warranty. We do not recommend it.

    Permits are common for water heater work. A licensed plumber who handles permits keeps the work inspected, which protects your warranty, insurance coverage, and resale value. GFR Plumbing takes care of permits and inspections in Fort Collins.

    Our crews install Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, Noritz and Navien. If you want to stick with your current brand, we can usually get it.

    Age, repair cost and efficiency decide it. Past ten years, with frequent breakdowns or a repair quote over half the price of a new unit, replacement is usually the better spend. A leaking tank is always a replacement.
