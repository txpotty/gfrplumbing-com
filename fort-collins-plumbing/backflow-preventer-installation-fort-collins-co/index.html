    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Fort Collins, CO

    Pressure vacuum breakers are the low-cost answer for lawn irrigation in Fort Collins, CO. They let air in when supply pressure drops, which breaks the siphon, but they only protect against back-siphonage, so they must sit above every downstream outlet and cannot be used where back pressure is possible. We check clearance and drainage too.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Fort Collins

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. If the zone between the checks loses its pressure margin, the relief valve opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Fort Collins. Brands include Watts and Febco.
