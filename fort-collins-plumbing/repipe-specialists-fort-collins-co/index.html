    Slab Leak Reroutes in Fort Collins

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save money.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Fort Collins, CO

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. GFR Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Fort Collins, CO, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also noticeably dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Fort Collins

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Expansive clay soils are a well-known problem in parts of Colorado, swelling with moisture and heaving slabs, basement floors, and buried pipes. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Fort Collins

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell later. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us which type you have.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Fort Collins, CO

    Many older homes in Fort Collins, CO still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. Our team specializes in the complete removal and replacement of these outdated systems with modern PEX or copper piping. This upgrade not only resolves existing issues but also significantly improves water quality and flow, boosting your home's value and plumbing reliability for decades to come.
