    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Cleaning in Fort Collins

    In most cases, sewer line cleaning methods like snaking and hydro jetting are non-invasive and do not require digging. We access the line through existing cleanouts. Digging is usually only necessary if there is a severe structural issue like a collapsed pipe requiring replacement, which we would confirm with a camera inspection first.

    Root treatment products can slow regrowth after a mechanical cleaning, but they do not remove a root mass. Cleaning first is the essential step.

    We use industry-leading equipment from trusted brands such as Spartan Tool, RIDGID, and Gorlitz for our sewer cameras, drain snakes, and hydro jetting machines. These brands are known for their reliability and effectiveness in tackling tough sewer line blockages.

    Common causes include tree root intrusion, buildup of grease and food particles, flushing non-biodegradable items like wet wipes or feminine hygiene products, and structural issues like cracked or collapsed pipes. Older homes frequently have clay pipes that are prone to root invasion.

    Hydro jetting is usually safe for most pipe materials, but it is crucial to have a professional assess your sewer line's condition first. We always perform a camera inspection to confirm your pipes can withstand the high-pressure water without damage, especially for very old or severely corroded lines.

    We firmly advise against chemical drain cleaners for sewer line clogs.
