    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Replacement in Fort Collins

    The cost for gas water heater replacement in Fort Collins, CO usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the unit size, brand, efficiency and any needed plumbing changes. Factors like gas line upgrades or ventilation improvements can affect the final price.

    Most standard gas water heater replacements in Fort Collins can be finished within 3 to 5 hours. More complex installations, such as converting to a different type of unit or significant plumbing changes, may take longer.

    Regular maintenance, such as annual flushing to remove sediment and checking the anode rod, can significantly extend your gas water heater's lifespan. Proper sizing and professional installation also play a crucial role.

    Atmospheric vent water heaters use natural convection to vent combustion gases up a chimney or flue. Power vent models use an electric fan to force gases out, allowing for more flexible venting options and often higher efficiency due to cleaner combustion.

    Most gas tanks last 8 to 12 years. Sediment from hard water and extreme garage temperatures can cut that. Annual flushing and anode checks help them last longer.

    GFR Plumbing installs and services all major brands of gas water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A. O. Smith, State and more. We help you pick the best brand and model to suit your needs and budget.

    If your home has a pressure regulator or backflow device, heated water has nowhere to expand. An expansion tank absorbs that pressure and helps protect the heater and fixtures.

    Tank warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We add our own labor warranty on the installation.
