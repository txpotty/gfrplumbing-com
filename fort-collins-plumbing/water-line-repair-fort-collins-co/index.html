    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Fort Collins, CO

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a quick spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with actual information.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Fort Collins, CO

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Many larger systems in Colorado draw on mountain snowmelt that runs fairly soft, while plains communities and groundwater sources often deliver harder water. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and ends repeat leaks.

    Underground Leak Detection in Fort Collins, CO

    Many water line leaks occur underground, making them difficult to detect without specialized equipment. Our technicians use advanced acoustic and thermal imaging tools to accurately locate hidden leaks without unnecessary digging. Early detection saves you money on water bills and prevents expensive damage to your foundation. Basements are common in Colorado, and shifting soil can crack floor drains, sewer lines, and foundation walls over time. Once located, we provide a clear repair plan, so the problem is resolved efficiently for your property in Fort Collins.
