    We strongly advise against using a home pressure washer for drain jetting. These machines lack the power, specialized nozzles, and safety features of professional equipment.     In most cases, a permit is not required for routine drain jetting because it is maintenance within existing plumbing. However, if the jetting reveals serious pipe damage requiring excavation and repair or replacement, a plumbing permit from Fort Collins may be necessary. Our team will let you know if that happens.

    Yes, drain jetting is very effective at removing tree roots that have gotten into sewer lines. We use root-cutting nozzles that break up the roots with high-pressure water, flushing them out of the system. This provides a more thorough and lasting solution than snaking for root intrusions.
