    Battery Backup Sump Pump System Installation in Fort Collins

    Sudden summer thunderstorms bring flash flooding and hail, and heavy runoff can overwhelm storm drains and sewer lines. Power outages during severe storms can leave standard sump pumps useless. Our battery backup sump pump system installation in Fort Collins provides an essential layer of protection. We add a secondary, battery-powered pump that instantly activates if the primary power fails, keeping flood protection running even without electricity. This is a valuable upgrade for many homes.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Fort Collins, CO

    Some properties use pumps in yard drainage pits or below-grade window wells to handle storm runoff. These pumps may sit idle for months, then fail when they are needed most. We replace them with corrosion-resistant models and clear debris from the pit. We can route discharge away from foundations and onto approved areas. Regular testing before storm season is a smart habit.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Fort Collins

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Fort Collins, CO

    For added protection, many homeowners in Fort Collins choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump quits. GFR Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are well matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Fort Collins

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide a backup. We schedule work to avoid disrupting operations.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Fort Collins

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation stops sewer gas odors.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods can fail earlier due to stuck switches.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that kicks on if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow.
