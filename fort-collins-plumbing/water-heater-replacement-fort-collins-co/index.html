    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Fort Collins, CO

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Fort Collins, CO homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Fort Collins, CO

    Ruud and Rheem are built by the same maker and share many parts. Our technicians install both, and since the parts overlap, a Ruud thermostat or element is easy to source when a repair makes more sense than a full replacement on a newer tank. Repair stays an option.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Fort Collins

    Homes from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may still have polybutylene supply lines, which are prone to fail at fittings. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. We bring the help.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Fort Collins, CO

    A leaking tank can put 40 or 50 gallons on the floor faster than most people expect. If water is coming from the heater, shut off the cold supply valve on top, turn the gas valve to off or flip the breaker, and open a hot tap to relieve pressure, then call and we will try to have a plumber and a new tank at your home that day. Do not wait it out.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Fort Collins

    We use Bradford White often on our jobs because the tanks hold up and the parts are easy to find. When replacing one, we match fuel, size and venting, check whether the old warranty still applies, and register the new heater right away. We file the warranty.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Fort Collins

    Businesses in Fort Collins run their water heaters harder than homes do, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons, laundromats and offices each have a different demand curve, and we size the new unit by peak gallons per hour and the temperature health code requires, which can mean a larger tank, a booster or a rack of tankless units.     Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Fort Collins, CO

    Rheem and AO Smith cover nearly every size and fuel, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. We lay out the options.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Fort Collins, CO

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.
