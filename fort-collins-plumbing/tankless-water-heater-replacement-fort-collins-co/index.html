    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Fort Collins

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Dry air, intense sun, and big daily temperature swings put constant stress on pipes, fittings, and outdoor plumbing in Colorado. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Fort Collins

    Many homes in Fort Collins rely on natural gas for water heating, and gas tankless units are a very efficient option. Our gas tankless water heater replacement service includes careful inspection of your existing gas lines, proper sizing of new gas lines if needed, and secure venting to the outdoors. We put a priority on safety. All gas connections and combustion air requirements meet Colorado and local codes, heading off potential hazards and maximizing efficiency.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Fort Collins, CO

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can break down plastic covers and wiring over time. Hard freezes are a normal part of winter in Colorado, and pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, and garages are especially vulnerable. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to guard the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Fort Collins

    The cost for tankless water heater replacement in Fort Collins, CO typically ranges from $3,000 to $6,000, depending on the unit's brand, capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed changes to gas lines, electrical or venting. This estimate includes both the new unit and professional installation. We will provide a detailed quote upfront after assessing your needs.

    With proper installation and regular maintenance, a new tankless water heater usually lasts 20 to 25 years. This is significantly longer than traditional tank water heaters, which usually last 10 to 15 years. Regular flushing and descaling can help extend the life of your tankless unit, especially in hard water areas.

    Well-maintained tankless units can last 15 to 20 years. In hard water areas without regular descaling, heat exchangers can fail far sooner. Annual flushing makes the largest difference.

    A direct swap usually takes four to six hours. A tank to tankless conversion with new gas and venting usually takes a full day.

    To protect the life and efficiency of your new tankless water heater, regular maintenance is crucial, especially in areas with hard water. This typically involves annual flushing to remove mineral buildup, checking the air filter, and inspecting gas and water connections. GFR Plumbing offers maintenance plans to help keep your unit in top condition.

    Most manufacturers offer 10 to 15 years on the heat exchanger and about 5 years on parts for residential use. We add our own labor warranty and register the unit for you.

    Repeated error codes, fluctuating temperature, water leaking from inside the cabinet and a unit older than 15 years are common signs. If the heat exchanger is leaking, replacement is usually the better value.
