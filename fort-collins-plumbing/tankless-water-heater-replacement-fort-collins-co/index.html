    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Fort Collins

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. They vent through PVC. Acidic condensate goes through a neutralizer and then to an approved drain. Higher elevations lower the boiling point of water, which affects water heater settings and pressure relief performance in mountain homes. The savings carry through every season because hot water use does.

    Commercial Tankless Racks in Fort Collins, CO

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. A rack of four units can draw more than 600,000 BTU, so we confirm gas pressure at the far unit with every burner firing. We work after hours when needed.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Fort Collins, CO

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. We replace failed units without shutting down the rest. Hand sinks and dish stations have to hit the temperatures health code requires.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Fort Collins

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. Most need at least a 3/4-inch line. Pipe length and meter capacity get checked against the sizing tables, and we confirm pressure at full fire. An undersized line causes ignition faults.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Fort Collins

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. The replacement gets matched to the existing gas, vent and mount where possible. Most swaps take four to six hours.
