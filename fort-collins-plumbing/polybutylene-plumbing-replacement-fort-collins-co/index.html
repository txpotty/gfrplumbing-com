    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Fort Collins

    Most poly replacements in Fort Collins fall between $4,000 and $15,000 or more. Size drives the price. Small homes with one bath and an open attic cost the least, and big multi-bath houses or copper jobs cost the most. The quote comes in writing at the visit.

    Around Fort Collins, a yearly water heater flush helps clear the sediment that collects at the bottom of the tank. Hard water does not cause poly to fail the way chlorine does. It adds scale.     Replacing an entire supply system requires routing, pressure testing and permits. A licensed crew finishes faster and provides warranties and documentation.

    For PEX, we use trusted brands like Uponor, Viega and Zurn. Uponor is our usual pick. For copper, we install Type L tubing from established mills rather than the thinner Type M found in many older homes, because the thicker wall holds up better over time.

    Most full poly repipes take three to five days, including drywall patching. Big houses need more time. Fixture count, attic access and pipe choice set the schedule, and you will usually have water each evening.

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water break down the plastic from the inside, making it brittle. Fittings and hot water lines often fail first.
