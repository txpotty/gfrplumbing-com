    Most water softener installations cost $1,500 to $4,000 including equipment. Adding a new softener loop typically adds $500 to $1,500 depending on access.

    Most households add salt every one to two months depending on water use and hardness. Metered systems use salt more efficiently.

    Common signs include white, chalky residue or scale buildup on faucets, showerheads and dishes, dry skin and hair after showering, dull-looking laundry, decreased lather from soap and reduced water pressure due to pipe buildup. If you notice these issues in your Fort Collins home, you probably have hard water.

    While some homeowners attempt DIY installation, it's generally recommended to hire a professional.     In many areas, a plumbing permit is required for major plumbing alterations like water softener installation to ensure compliance with local codes. GFR Plumbing will advise you on any required permits for your Fort Collins installation and can assist with the application process.

    Installing a water softener offers numerous benefits, including preventing scale buildup in pipes and appliances, extending appliance lifespan, reducing soap and detergent usage and improving the feel of your skin and hair. It also makes cleaning easier and keeps clothes looking brighter.
