    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Fort Collins, CO

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Newer valves meter actual water use, some connect to Wi-Fi for salt alerts and higher-capacity tanks regenerate less often for larger households. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Fort Collins, CO

    Building a home in Fort Collins, CO? New construction is the cheapest time to add a softener loop, because the walls are open and the main can be routed past the garage or utility room without cutting finished drywall. Builders call us in early. Hose bibs can stay on hard water.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Fort Collins

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. We follow Culligan's requirements for the drain, bypass and electrical, and we program the system for your tested hardness so it does not over-regenerate. Connections and setup included.     High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Fort Collins

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. That pays back over time. We install several models.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Fort Collins, CO

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. Our crew pulls the old system, checks the loop and drain line and installs a model matched to your water today. Disposal is included. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Fort Collins

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Fort Collins

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron adds to the setting. You get a walkthrough of the settings.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Fort Collins, CO

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.
