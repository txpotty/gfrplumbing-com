    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Fort Collins

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet typically costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout generally runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    Rental machines are available, but they can kink, grab and injure users who are not trained. They can also get stuck or damage older pipe. Professional rooter service is safer and generally more effective.

    Snaking uses an auger to break through and pull out blockages, while hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire inside of the pipe, removing buildup like grease and mineral deposits. Hydro-jetting provides a far more thorough cleaning, often recommended for recurring clogs or main sewer lines.

    Most standard rooter services for isolated clogs can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex main sewer line blockages, especially those involving extensive root removal or hydro-jetting, might take longer, sometimes up to a full day. We aim for efficient service with lasting results.

    Homes with tree roots or older clay pipe often need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Homes with newer PVC lines may go many years without it. A camera inspection can help set the right schedule.

    Our technicians take every precaution to keep your home clean during rooter service. We use drop cloths and protective gear to contain any mess, so the work area is tidy after the job is done. We respect your property and aim for a clean, efficient service.

    GFR Plumbing stands by the quality of our work. We offer a warranty on our rooter services, the specifics of which will be provided with your estimate. This gives you confidence knowing that our solutions are designed to last.

    Generally, simple rooter services to clear clogs do not require a permit in Fort Collins. However, if the service reveals a need for pipe repair or replacement, especially for main sewer lines, a plumbing permit may be required. We will handle all necessary permitting if that situation arises.
