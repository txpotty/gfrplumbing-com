    Gas Water Heater Repair in Fort Collins, CO

    Gas units add a burner, a pilot or igniter and a gas control valve to the list of parts that can fail. Most no-heat calls trace to the thermocouple or the gas control. We leak test each gas connection after the repair. Draft at the vent gets checked too.

    Gas Control and Thermocouple Repair in Fort Collins

    Thermocouples, flame sensors, igniters and gas control valves cause most gas heater failures. Each part gets tested, the burner gets cleaned and the vent gets a draft check. Pilots that light and quit usually mean a weak thermocouple or a plugged arrestor screen. Most are done in under two hours.

    Sediment Flush and Noise Repair in Fort Collins

    Popping, rumbling or crackling usually means a thick layer of sediment is trapping water and overheating the steel at the tank bottom. The tank gets drained and refilled several times to break up scale, and the drain valve gets cleared. Flushing restores capacity. Homes on hard water benefit from a yearly flush.

    Electric Tank Repair in Fort Collins, CO

    Four parts can fail on an electric tank, two elements and two thermostats. Hard water piles scale on the lower element until it burns out. After testing resistance and voltage, we replace what failed, reset the high limit and watch it heat up. A breaker that keeps tripping gets traced first.
