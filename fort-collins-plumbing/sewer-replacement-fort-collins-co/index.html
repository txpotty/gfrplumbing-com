    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Fort Collins, CO

    When appropriate, trenchless sewer replacement offers a less invasive alternative to traditional digging. Methods like pipe bursting or pipe lining allow us to replace or repair your sewer line with minimal excavation, often only requiring two access points. This can save your lawn, driveway, and landscaping from extensive damage, making it a common choice for homeowners in Fort Collins who want to preserve their property's appearance. We'll determine if your sewer line is a good candidate for this advanced technique.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Fort Collins, CO

    Where applicable, we offer trenchless sewer repair methods like pipe bursting. This technique involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, at the same time breaking the existing pipe apart. It's a less disruptive option that requires only two small access points, saving your landscaping and driveway from extensive excavation. Trenchless methods are ideal for certain types of pipe damage and property layouts in Fort Collins.

    Foundation Protection during Sewer Replacement in Fort Collins, CO

    Basements are common in Colorado, and shifting soil can crack floor drains, sewer lines, and foundation walls over time. Soil movement can cause foundation shifts, which in turn can damage sewer lines. Our replacement process in Fort Collins, CO takes foundation integrity into consideration. We plan excavations carefully and ensure proper soil compaction and backfill to prevent future issues that could impact both your new sewer line and your home's foundation.     Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Fort Collins

    Many older homes in Fort Collins still have original clay or cast iron sewer lines, which are vulnerable to root intrusion, corrosion, and cracking. GFR Plumbing can upgrade your system to modern PVC (polyvinyl chloride) piping. PVC is durable, resistant to root penetration, and has a smooth interior that reduces the likelihood of blockages, providing a long-lasting solution for your home's drainage.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Fort Collins

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It requires only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are finished in one day.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Fort Collins, CO

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Roots can penetrate tiny cracks, grow inside the pipe, and eventually cause blockages or even burst the line. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to stop future intrusions. We also advise on tree placement to protect your new sewer system.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Fort Collins, CO

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the right option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench after inspection. This method allows full correction of slope issues.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Fort Collins

    Many homes are built on slab foundations with drain lines underneath. When these lines fail, replacement requires tunneling or rerouting. We evaluate options to limit impact on your home. Rerouting through walls or attic space is sometimes possible for branch lines. We explain the cost and disruption of each option.
