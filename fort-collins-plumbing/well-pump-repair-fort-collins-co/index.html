    Pressure Tank Problems in Fort Collins

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. A waterlogged tank or a ruptured bladder causes most of it. We check the precharge with the tank drained. It should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure, so a 40/60 switch wants roughly 38 psi in the tank.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Fort Collins, CO

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Spring snowmelt raises groundwater in Colorado, which can push water into basements and overwork sump pumps. Lightning and power surges damage those parts more than anything else. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. An arrestor costs little.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Fort Collins, CO

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. Breaker trips usually come first. With the power locked out, we test and replace the failed parts and tighten every terminal. Wiring meets Colorado code when the job is done.

    Well Pump Maintenance and Repair Cost in Fort Collins, CO

    Maintenance costs far less than an emergency pull. A typical well pump repair in Fort Collins runs $400 to $1,500, while a yearly checkup costs a fraction of that. GFR Plumbing prices both up front. The checkup covers tank precharge, switch contacts, amp draw and the well cap seal, which catches most problems before the pump fails in the middle of a hot week.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Fort Collins, CO

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. The control box capacitor and relay are next on the list. We lock out the power before testing anything. All wiring and splices meet Colorado electrical code before the pump restarts.

    Low Water Pressure Diagnosis in Fort Collins

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. A clogged sediment filter, a pressure switch set too low or a leak in the yard line can all cause it. In some cases the pump is fine and the well just cannot keep up with demand. We test each one.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Fort Collins, CO

    Switch and control repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. A submersible pull and replacement runs about $1,800 to $5,000 and up, based on depth and horsepower. Replacing an aging pump during a pull saves paying for a second pull later. You see both numbers.

    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Fort Collins, CO

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. A weak motor draws high amps. Low flow with normal amp draw points to a plugged intake or a falling water level, which is a different repair. Testing first saves a pull.
