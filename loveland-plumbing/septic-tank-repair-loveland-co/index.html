    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Loveland, CO

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. Once the outlet baffle is gone, solids head straight for the field. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Emergency Septic Repair in Loveland

    Sewage in the shower pan on a Sunday is an emergency, and we treat it like one. The first thing we ask is whether the tank has a riser we can reach, because uncovering a buried lid can add an hour or more. Stop using water until the tech arrives. Then we find the blockage, clear it and tell you what caused it.

    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Loveland

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Press the silence button and cut back on water. We check whether the pump runs and whether a float is hung up on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Loveland, CO

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. Humming with no flow points to a jammed impeller or a failed capacitor. We pull it and test the discharge line before setting a new one. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Septic Repair for Businesses in Loveland, CO

    Daycares and small shops on septic put more flow through the tank, more often, than a typical three-bedroom house with four people in it. Kitchen grease and restroom paper towels cause most of the trouble. We add a larger effluent filter and set a pump-out schedule based on use. Repairs get scheduled around your hours.

    Leach Field Restoration in Loveland, CO

    Before we condemn a leach field, we open the distribution box and look at how evenly it splits the flow. A tilted or crushed D-box can send everything to one trench and drown it. Leveling or replacing the box often brings the field back. Some fields are past saving. For those, we explain the design and permit steps.

    Septic Maintenance and Small Repairs in Loveland

    A sludge judge, a long clear tube, tells us how full the tank really is. Each maintenance call includes a sludge and scum reading, and we pump when the solids reach about a third of the tank depth. Filters get rinsed. Small cracks around the riser get sealed before groundwater finds them.
