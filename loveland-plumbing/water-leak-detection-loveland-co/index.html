    Water Meter Leak Testing in Loveland

    The water meter is the first and easiest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is losing water.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Loveland

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for long periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include oddly green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. GFR Plumbing uses ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Loveland, CO

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak safeguards the pool and the soil around it.

    Slab Leak Detection in Loveland

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often signaled by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. GFR Plumbing uses dedicated acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is key to preventing major foundation damage.
