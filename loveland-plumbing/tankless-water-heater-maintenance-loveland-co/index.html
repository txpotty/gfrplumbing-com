    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Loveland

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. Solution goes from a bucket into the cold port, out the hot port and back to the bucket. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Loveland, CO

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. We add a pair of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.

    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Loveland, CO

    We service Bosch, Rheem, Takagi and Navien heaters around Loveland, and each one has its own filter locations and code list in the manual. We follow the manufacturer's steps, not a generic checklist. Warranty claims later can hinge on it.

    Noritz Descaling in Loveland, CO

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. We use a descaler that the manufacturer approves and keep the cycle inside the time limit in the manual, because too strong a mix can etch the metal. While the cycle runs, we check the fan motor and exhaust path.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Loveland, CO

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. On a Rinnai an LC code means scale, on a Navien E003 means no ignition, and on a Noritz a 90 usually means an air or exhaust problem. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.
