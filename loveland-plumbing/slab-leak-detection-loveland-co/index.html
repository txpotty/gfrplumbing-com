    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Loveland

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, finding where the sound is loudest and confirming it from another position, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.

    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Loveland

    Plastic pipe muffles leak noise. For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, even through grout lines and small cracks. It finds leaks acoustic gear misses in Loveland homes.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Loveland, CO

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Loveland

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, rubbing against concrete and gravel until it wears through. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Energy bills often rise.

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Loveland, CO

    A supply leak and a drain leak show different signs. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, showing up as smells, sluggish drains or wet spots near bathrooms. Each needs its own test in your Loveland, CO home.

    Slab Leaks in Commercial Buildings in Loveland
