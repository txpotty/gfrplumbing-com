    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Loveland, CO

    Gray polybutylene went into millions of homes from the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorine in treated water attacks the plastic and the acetal fittings from the inside, so it can look fine from outside right up to the day a joint lets go. Insurers ask about it. Swapping it for PEX can help with insurance and with resale.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Loveland

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Water chemistry, high flow velocity and contact with soil under a slab can all pit the pipe wall from the inside, and once a few pinholes appear, more are usually on the way. Around Loveland, a yearly water heater flush helps clear the scale that collects at the bottom of the tank. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Loveland

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. Give it four or five decades and the inside is so packed with rust that a half-inch pipe barely flows, and the first draw in the morning runs brown. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. Pressure comes back at the fixtures and the rust stops.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Loveland, CO

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. Every one is listed for potable water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Loveland

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Solutions in Loveland, CO

    Apartment buildings, small offices and churches in Loveland, CO can benefit from PEX as much as houses can. Each unit can get its own manifold and shutoff, so a leak in one apartment shuts down one apartment instead of the whole stack. Rated walls need firestopping. Every penetration through a fire-rated wall gets a listed firestop sleeve or sealant.
