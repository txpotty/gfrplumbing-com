    Proven Walls Waterproofing Solutions in Loveland, CO

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Loveland

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra attention before new tile is set.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Loveland

    Many homes in Loveland have crawl spaces, which are very prone to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting prevents ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Loveland, CO

    Sump pumps are a critical part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall. We install high-capacity sump pumps designed to handle large volumes of water, keeping your basement dry even during flash floods. Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units. We make sure the discharge line is routed properly so water does not circulate back toward the foundation.

    Drainage System Integration in Loveland, CO

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a robust drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Loveland, CO properties, so water is efficiently managed and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Loveland

    Restaurants, gyms, salons and offices often have restrooms, locker rooms and kitchens with constant water exposure. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with durable membranes. Work can be scheduled after hours to limit downtime. We also repair the plumbing behind those walls when leaks are found.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Loveland, CO

    Basement wall sealing is a key part of walls waterproofing. Dry air, intense sun, and big daily temperature swings put steady stress on pipes, fittings, and outdoor plumbing in Colorado. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier, healthier environment in your Loveland, CO basement.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Loveland, CO

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry for good.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Loveland

    Housing in Colorado ranges from older homes with galvanized and cast iron pipe to fast-growing suburbs built with copper and PEX. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks. We seal cracks with injection products, apply interior or exterior waterproofing and install drainage where needed. A sump pump may be added if groundwater builds up.
