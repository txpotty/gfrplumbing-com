    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Loveland

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. When pressure between the checks gets too close to supply pressure, the relief opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Loveland. We install Watts and Febco units.
