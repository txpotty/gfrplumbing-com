    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Loveland

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Loveland. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. GFR Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Loveland

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Loveland

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Loveland, CO

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Loveland, CO, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.
