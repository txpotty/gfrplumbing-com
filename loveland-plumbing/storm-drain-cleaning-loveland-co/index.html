    Catch Basin Cleaning in Loveland, CO

    Catch basins are key parts of any storm drainage system in Loveland, CO, designed to collect runoff and trap debris before it enters the main drain lines. Regular cleaning of catch basins is important to prevent them from becoming overloaded and causing blockages further down the system. Our service includes removing built-up leaves, sediment, trash, and other debris from catch basins, so they work correctly and protect your entire drainage system. This preventative maintenance is key to avoiding larger issues.

    Root Removal from Storm Drains in Loveland

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in Loveland, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Roots seek out moisture and can infiltrate drain pipes through tiny cracks or loose joints, growing and eventually completely blocking water flow. GFR Plumbing uses specialized root-cutting attachments on our drain snakes and hydro jetting equipment to cut through and remove root intrusions. After removal, we can recommend further steps to prevent future root problems.

    Channel and Trench Drain Cleaning in Loveland

    Channel drains across driveways and garage entries catch water before it reaches the house. Their narrow channels fill quickly with sand, leaves and debris. We remove the grates, clean the channel and jet the outlet pipe. Cracked or loose grates can be replaced while we are there. A clean trench drain keeps water out of your garage during a downpour.
