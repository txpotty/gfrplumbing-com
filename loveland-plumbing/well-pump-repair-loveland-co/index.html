    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Loveland

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a frustrating problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts thorough diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Loveland home, ensuring a targeted and effective repair solution.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Loveland

    The pressure tank works with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. Issues like a waterlogged tank, sudden pressure drops, or continuous pump operation often point to a faulty pressure tank. We inspect the air bladder, pressure switch, and tank connections to identify the problem. In Loveland, we repair or replace tanks to optimize your well system's performance and extend pump life.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Loveland

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Loveland, CO

If your pump is older and already needs pulling, replacing it at the same time can save a second pull later. We lay out both options with clear numbers.
