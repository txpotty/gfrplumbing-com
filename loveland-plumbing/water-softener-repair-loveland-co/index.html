    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Loveland, CO

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Loveland, CO

    Leaks in a water softener can range from minor drips around fittings to significant puddles from a cracked tank or faulty valve. These leaks can cause water damage and waste water. Our plumbers promptly pinpoint the source of leaks, whether from seals, O-rings, hoses, or internal components, and perform lasting repairs. We use appropriate sealing compounds and replacement parts for a lasting fix in Loveland, CO.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Loveland, CO

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500. If the tank and valve are sound, repair generally wins. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Loveland

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a common issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. GFR Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, carefully removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in Loveland.

    Control Valve Repair in Loveland

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, directing the flow of water and initiating regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water or waste salt. GFR Plumbing technicians are experts at diagnosing and repairing common control valve issues, whether mechanical or electronic, ensuring precise timing and efficient operation. We stock common replacement parts to speed up your repair in Loveland.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Loveland

    A frequent issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to inefficient regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and making sure the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Loveland, CO

    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. Our team can safely break up and remove salt bridges, clean the brine tank, and advise on best practices to prevent future occurrences. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, common in older systems.
