    Disposal Jams and Hums in Loveland, CO

    A hum without grinding tells us the motor has power and the plate will not turn. Usually a ten minute fix. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before we test it with water. Silence with no hum points to the switch, the outlet or the breaker instead.

    Burst Supply Lines in Loveland, CO

    Plastic supply lines with plastic nuts crack more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings.     Grease Trap Repair in Loveland, CO

    Disposal Repair and Replacement in Loveland, CO
