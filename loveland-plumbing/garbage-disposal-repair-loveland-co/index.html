    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Loveland, CO

    Leak Detection and Repair in Loveland, CO

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Preventive Care and Longevity in Loveland, CO

    Keep cold water running before, during and after use, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Grease poured in with hot water coats the chamber and the pipe, while cold water keeps it solid so the impellers can break it up. Stringy peels cause most jams. Throw out celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Loveland

    If a disposal is completely dead, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. The reset, breaker, wall switch and wiring all get tested, along with air switches and the magnetic lids on batch-feed units. Most are simple fixes.

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Loveland, CO

    Food buildup and grease on the chamber walls and under the splash guard cause most disposal smells. Around Loveland, a yearly water heater flush helps clear the sediment that collects at the bottom of the tank. Mineral scale traps the food. Cleaning the guard and chamber, checking the drain and swapping a torn guard often solves a smell that lemon peels never fixed.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Loveland

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes. We check the breaker, the reset button and the switch for silent units, and the flywheel and motor windings for humming ones, then decide if a repair makes sense.

    Cost-Effective Disposal Repair in Loveland, CO

    Paying half the price of a new disposal for a repair seldom pays off, and we will say so. Plenty of repairs cost little. Resetting an overload, freeing a jam, replacing a switch or resealing a flange usually costs well under the price of a replacement and keeps a younger unit running for years.

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Loveland

    A failed disposal can push water back into the sink and dishwasher. If water is rising or reaching the floor, shut off the disposal and the dishwasher and call us, since running either one just adds more water to the cabinet. We move quickly on these.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Loveland

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Failing bearings are not worth fixing.
