    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Loveland

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation stops sewer gas odors.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Loveland

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    A battery backup sump pump system is a secondary pump powered by a battery, designed to activate automatically if your primary sump pump loses power during an outage or fails. This is critical for maintaining flood protection during power outages caused by severe weather.

    While it might seem like a simple task, sump pump replacement involves electrical connections, proper plumbing and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding.     To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by GFR Plumbing can also help lengthen its lifespan.
