    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Loveland, CO

    Gas water heater maintenance involves crucial safety inspections in Loveland, CO. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are essential to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and keep your gas-fired appliance running safely.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Loveland

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures steady.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Loveland, CO

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Loveland

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for any leaks.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Loveland

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Loveland, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.

    Thermostat and Heating Element Checks in Loveland

    For electric water heaters, we test the thermostat and heating elements to make sure they work correctly and hold an steady temperature. Faulty elements or thermostats can lead to too little hot water or wasted energy use. For gas water heaters, we inspect the burner assembly and pilot light. Higher elevations lower the boiling point of water, which affects water heater settings and relief valve performance in mountain homes.
