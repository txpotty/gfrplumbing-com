    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Loveland

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. We replace these lines with PVC. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Loveland

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. Dirt falls in through the crack. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then run water through it and check it on camera before backfill. Figure on a single day for a typical spot repair.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Loveland, CO

    Replacement makes sense when the camera shows failures every few feet. Patching that line is wasted money. We price open trench replacement, pipe bursting and full-length lining side by side for your Colorado property, with the footage, depth and surface restoration spelled out for each one. The right pick depends on depth and access.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Loveland

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Loveland, CO

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. We tell you which one your pipe can take.
