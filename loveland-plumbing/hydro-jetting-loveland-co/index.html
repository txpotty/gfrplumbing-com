    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Loveland

    The cost of hydro jetting in Loveland, CO typically ranges from $300 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length and diameter of the pipe, and ease of access.     Most residential jobs take 1 to 3 hours including inspection. Long commercial lines or heavy grease can add time.

    Hydro jetting is very effective against a wide range of stubborn clogs that traditional snaking cannot fully clear.     Usually, a permit is not required for standard hydro jetting aimed at clearing blockages in existing lines. However, if the hydro jetting is part of a larger repair or replacement project that alters the plumbing system, a permit from the local building department may be necessary.     While hydro jetting fully removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can discuss during our assessment.
