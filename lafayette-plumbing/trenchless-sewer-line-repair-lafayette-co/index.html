    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Lafayette

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line at high pressure to strip roots, grease and loose scale, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. A poor prep job means a wrinkled liner. Lining works best on cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their round shape.

    Pipe Bursting in Lafayette, CO

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. The new HDPE pipe is butt-fused into one length first. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. It goes quickly.

    Decades. Quality liners and HDPE pipe carry a 50-year design life from most manufacturers when the host pipe was cleaned and prepped right.

    Pipe bursting uses high-density polyethylene, HDPE, fused into one continuous length with no joints. For lining, it is a felt or fiberglass tube saturated with epoxy resin. Both resist roots and corrosion, and both are rated by their makers for a service life of around fifty years when installed on properly prepared pipe.

    Roots cannot get into the lined section. A properly installed liner has no joints, so they have nowhere to get in.
