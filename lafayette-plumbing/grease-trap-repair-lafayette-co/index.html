    Simple repairs like gasket or tee replacements usually cost far less than a new trap.     Flow Control Device Repair in Lafayette

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is essential for a trap to pass inspection.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Lafayette

    The baffles inside a grease trap are essential for separating FOGs from wastewater. If these baffles are corroded, broken, or dislodged, the trap's efficiency plummets, allowing grease into the sewer system. Our service includes inspecting the condition of all baffles and repairing or replacing them as needed, often using durable, corrosion-resistant materials. Proper baffle function is key to compliance with wastewater regulations in Lafayette.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Lafayette

    When traditional snaking isn't enough, hydro-jetting is our go-to method for severe grease trap blockages. This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to thoroughly scour the interior of pipes and the grease trap itself, removing years of FOG buildup, scale, and debris. It's a very effective, non-invasive method that not only clears clogs but also cleans the lines, greatly reducing the likelihood of future blockages in your Lafayette business.
