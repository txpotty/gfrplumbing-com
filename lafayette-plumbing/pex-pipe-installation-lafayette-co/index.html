    Full Home PEX Repiping in Lafayette

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. Give it four or five decades and the inside is so packed with rust that a half-inch pipe barely flows, and the first draw in the morning runs brown. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. Pressure comes back at the fixtures and the rust stops.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Lafayette

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Both last decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Lafayette

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. A shower valve set two inches too low or a vanity stub that misses the cabinet back means opening the wall again after the tile is up, which is expensive and slow. We confirm heights with your contractor first. Then we pressure test with the walls open.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Lafayette, CO

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to replace old supply lines.     PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Lafayette, CO

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. Every one is listed for potable water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.
