    Water Line Materials We Install in Lafayette, CO

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each has a place.     Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Lafayette

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? We will usually just fix that spot. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Lafayette

    Galvanized steel was common in homes built before the 1960s. The zinc coating wears off and the steel rusts from the inside.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Lafayette, CO

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Plumbing code caps house pressure at 80 psi. If your new line comes in above that, we add a pressure reducing valve right after the main shutoff, set it around 55 to 65 psi, and replace the gate valve with a quarter-turn ball valve. A quarter turn and the water is off.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Lafayette

    Your service line runs from the meter or curb stop to the main shutoff inside the house, usually as 3/4-inch or 1-inch pipe. Once it has leaked in two different places, patching stops making sense. We dig at both ends, pull or cut out the old pipe, and run new line at the depth local code calls for, with fresh connections at the meter and at the shutoff. Most homes are done in a day.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Lafayette, CO

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Frost-free hose bibs are standard here, but they still burst if a hose is left attached when temperatures drop. PEX can swell a little in a freeze and go back to shape, where copper splits. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and keep it covered from sunlight at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.
