    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Lafayette, CO

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. They need a 120-volt outlet nearby. Vent length and elbows stay inside the manufacturer's limits.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Lafayette

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Colorado homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Newer tanks can run taller than the old one. We check clearance before we order. Most of these swaps take two to three hours.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Lafayette, CO

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Code is stricter here. We size by peak demand and recovery, not tank volume alone.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Lafayette, CO

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. Either one works. We set them up per the factory manual and test draft.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Lafayette

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. The 1/2-inch line feeding a tank is rarely enough. The venting changes too. We check meter capacity and pipe length before quoting so there are no surprises halfway through the job.

    No Hot Water Calls in Lafayette

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Lafayette, CO

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. You will need a condensate drain and a nearby outlet. Payback depends on usage.

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Lafayette

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. A standard tank sits around 0.60 to 0.65. High-efficiency condensing tanks reach 0.80 or more and can qualify for rebates.
