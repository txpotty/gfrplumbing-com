    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydrojet Plumbing in Lafayette

    The cost of hydrojet plumbing in Lafayette, CO can range from $300 to $800 or more, depending on the severity and location of the clog, as well as the length and diameter of the pipe.     We use professional-grade hydrojetting equipment from reputable manufacturers like Spartan Tool, US Jetting, and Gorlitz. These brands are known for their reliability, power, and effectiveness in clearing even the toughest pipe blockages, delivering a thorough clean every time.

    When performed by a trained professional, hydrojetting is usually safe for most types of pipes, including older cast iron or PVC.     Most homes only need jetting every few years or when clogs recur. Restaurants and busy commercial kitchens often benefit from jetting every three to six months. We recommend a schedule based on what the camera reveals.

    Jetting removes the roots inside the pipe, but roots will grow back if the joint or crack remains open. Regular jetting can control them for years. Pipe lining or repair is the lasting fix.

    The duration of hydrojet plumbing depends on the complexity of the clog and the length of the pipe being cleaned.
