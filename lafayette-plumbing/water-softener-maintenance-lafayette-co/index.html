    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Maintenance in Lafayette

    The cost of water softener maintenance in Lafayette, CO usually ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the system's condition and the services required. Factors like cleaning a heavily fouled brine tank or replacing minor worn parts can affect the final price. We provide a detailed quote before starting any work.

    While you can perform basic tasks like checking salt levels and refilling the brine tank, professional maintenance involves inspecting internal components, testing resin efficiency and diagnosing control valve issues. These tasks require professional tools and experience to ensure the system functions correctly and safely.

    GFR Plumbing services all major brands of water softeners, including but not limited to Fleck, Culligan, Kenmore, GE, EcoPure, Morton and Whirlpool. Our technicians are trained on a wide variety of models and control systems, providing expert care for your specific unit.

    Maintenance usually includes inspecting the brine tank for salt bridges, cleaning the tank, checking and cleaning the resin bed, lubricating moving parts, sanitizing the system and running diagnostic checks on the control valve. We also test water hardness to verify effectiveness.

    Resin often lasts 10 to 15 years, but chlorinated city water and iron can shorten that. Fine resin beads in your faucet aerators can point to resin breakdown.

    For the best performance and longevity, we recommend professional water softener maintenance annually. In areas with extremely hard water, or for older systems, a twice-yearly check-up might be worthwhile to prevent issues before they become serious.
