    InSinkErator Models We Service in Lafayette

    Badger units are the basic line, with a single grind stage and a 1/3 to 3/4 horsepower motor. Evolution models add grind stages and sound insulation, which matters in an open kitchen, and some carry longer in-home warranties than the basic line does. Common models are on the truck. Older InSinkErator units with a good motor can usually be repaired.

    Disposal Replacement in Lafayette, CO

    Disposals last about eight to twelve years. Water dripping from the bottom of the housing means it is finished. We drop the old unit, check the mounting ring, install the new one and reconnect the dishwasher hose with a new clamp so it does not leak at the inlet. We haul the old one away.

    Replacing an Old Kitchen Sink in Lafayette

    Pulling an old cast iron sink takes two people and a putty knife, because they weigh a lot and are usually sealed hard to the counter. The counter cutout gets measured first. A new sink has to match that opening or the counter has to be trimmed, and we tell you which before you buy. The drain gets rebuilt to suit the new bowl depth.

    InSinkErator Installation and Service in Lafayette

    InSinkErator units use the Quick Lock mount, which makes trading one InSinkErator for another a quick job. We install the Badger line for basic use and the Evolution series when you want more grinding stages and less noise. We replace worn mounts. The sink flange gets fresh plumber's putty, the dishwasher knockout gets punched if needed, and the cord is checked against the outlet.

    Grease Trap Installation and Cleaning in Lafayette

    Grease traps are sized by flow, and a three-compartment sink usually needs at least a 20 gallon per minute unit. We set the trap level, install the flow control fitting and vent it so it does not siphon. Cleaning schedules depend on volume. Busy fryer kitchens may need it every few weeks.

    Grease Interceptors in Lafayette

    In-ground interceptors are much bigger than under-sink traps and are sized by the sewer authority's formula for your fixtures, not by guesswork. We coordinate with your general contractor and the inspector about placement, venting and the sampling port. Access matters most. Lids the truck cannot reach make every pump-out more expensive.
