    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Lafayette, CO

    Higher elevations lower the boiling point of water, which affects water heater settings and relief valve performance in mountain homes.     Sewer Camera Inspection in Lafayette, CO

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. The camera starts at a cleanout. Every offset, belly, root intrusion and break shows up with its depth and location, and the recording is yours to keep.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Lafayette

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Lafayette, CO

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too.     Preventive Maintenance Checks in Lafayette, CO

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. It takes about an hour. Doing this every year or two adds years to a water heater and keeps the shutoffs working for the day you really need them.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Lafayette, CO

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Some need replacing soon. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in Lafayette, CO
