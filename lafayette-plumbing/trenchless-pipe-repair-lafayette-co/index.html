    Lateral and Spot Lining in Lafayette

    Not every pipe needs lining end to end. Short damage gets a short liner. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, a sectional liner costs less than a full-length one and goes in quickly, but we confirm the rest of the line is sound by camera before recommending it.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Lafayette

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. Then the liner goes in. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Lafayette, CO

    A broken water service line wastes water and soaks the yard. It also drops pressure. We locate the break, then replace the line by bursting new HDPE through the old path or boring a new path, so the lawn and walkway stay put and you are back in water quickly. We pressure test before turning water on.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Lafayette, CO

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. Bursting or boring works. Our crew pulls new HDPE or PEX through the old path with a bursting head, or drills a fresh path with a directional boring tool, so lawns, driveways and walkways stay untouched. The line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Lafayette, CO

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It also upsizes lines. With an entry and exit pit dug, the winch pulls the head and new HDPE through, replacing the run in a day or two in most homes. GFR Plumbing fuses the HDPE into one length.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Costs in Lafayette, CO

    On paper, the two methods can price out close. Then add restoration. A trench under a driveway or through an established garden means paying to replace concrete, plants and irrigation after the plumbing is done, and trenchless avoids most of those costs. We price both side by side.

    Sectional Point Repair in Lafayette

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. That calls for a sectional liner. Guided by camera, a short liner is placed over the bad section and cured, so you pay for a few feet instead of the full line. It suits isolated damage in Lafayette sewer lines.

    Sewer Lateral Lining in Lafayette

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Old materials crack and sag. Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots over time, so we jet the line clean, inspect it by camera and choose lining or bursting based on what we see. The new line restores full flow and resists roots.
