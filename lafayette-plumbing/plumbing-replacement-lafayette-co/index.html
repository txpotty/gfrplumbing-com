    Sewer Line Replacement in Lafayette

    Persistent sewer backups, foul odors or lush spots in your yard can signal a failing sewer line. Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines slower, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth. We offer complete sewer line replacement using trenchless technology whenever possible, minimizing disruption to your landscaping. We make sure new lines are properly graded, connected and compliant with local Lafayette plumbing codes, preventing future blockages and costly repairs.

    Water Line Replacement in Lafayette, CO

    A leaking main water line can lead to major water loss, high bills and foundation damage, which is a serious concern for homes on slab foundations. Our water line replacement services address issues like corroded pipes, leaks or low water pressure. We locate the leak, excavate the area if necessary and replace the damaged section or the entire line with durable PEX or copper piping. We make sure of a secure, leak-free connection from the meter to your home, bringing back reliable water flow.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Replacement in Lafayette

    The cost of plumbing replacement in Lafayette, CO varies widely based on the type of service. A water heater replacement can range from $1,200 to $3,500, while a sewer line replacement might be $3,000 to $15,000 or more. Whole-home repiping can cost between $4,000 and $15,000. We provide detailed, transparent estimates after an on-site assessment.

    For water heater replacements, we mainly recommend and install reliable brands such as Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien. These manufacturers are known for their durability, energy efficiency and excellent warranty coverage, providing long-term value for your Lafayette home.

    Replacing old pipes with PEX or copper offers many benefits. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant and less prone to bursting in freezes, while copper is durable and long-lasting. Both improve water quality, eliminate leaks from old pipes and can significantly boost water pressure and flow throughout your Lafayette home.

    Many sewer line replacements take one to three days depending on length, depth and method. Trenchless options can speed up the timeline.

    Consider the age of the equipment, the cost of repair compared with replacement and how often it has needed service. We give you both options with pricing so you can decide.

    The timeline depends on the complexity of the job. A water heater replacement usually takes 2 to 4 hours. A major project like a full house repipe or sewer line replacement can take 1 to 5 days. We will provide an estimated timeframe during your consultation.

    Key signs include frequent, recurring leaks in the same area, discolored water, very low water pressure throughout the house, heavy corrosion visible on pipes or a major component like a water heater being over 10 to 15 years old and failing. If repair costs approach the cost of replacement, replacement is often the smarter long-term investment.
