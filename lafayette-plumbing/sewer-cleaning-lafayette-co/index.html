    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Lafayette

    Trees and shrubs send roots toward any moisture they can find, and a sewer joint is a steady source during dry months. Thirsty yard trees and shrubs are common causes in local yards. We use a sectional cable with a root saw sized to the pipe to cut the mass free and pull it out. Heavy root growth may need a follow-up with a jetter to clear fine root hairs.     Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Lafayette

    Older properties may still have vitrified clay or even Orangeburg fiber pipe running to the street. Clay joints are open invitations for roots, and Orangeburg can flatten under soil weight. We clean these lines carefully and use the camera to map joints and soft spots. If the pipe is failing, we will tell you plainly and show the footage. Cleaning keeps you running while you budget for a replacement.
