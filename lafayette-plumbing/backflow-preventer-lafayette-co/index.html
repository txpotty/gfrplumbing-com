    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Service in Lafayette, CO

    RPZ assemblies protect against high-hazard cross-connections at restaurants, medical offices, car washes and buildings with chemical feed. They have two independent check valves with a relief port between them that opens and dumps water if either check starts to fail. That discharge needs a drain. We install, test and rebuild RPZ units from Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.
