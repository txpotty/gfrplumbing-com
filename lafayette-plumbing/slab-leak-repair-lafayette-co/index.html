    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Lafayette

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. Rerouting ends that. We leave the leaking run in the ground and pull new PEX through the attic and down the walls, cutting openings where they are easiest to patch, often behind a vanity or in a closet.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Lafayette

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it. The bad run stays in the ground unused while a new line goes overhead or in the walls, where it can be reached later. Lafayette homes with original plumbing see this often.
