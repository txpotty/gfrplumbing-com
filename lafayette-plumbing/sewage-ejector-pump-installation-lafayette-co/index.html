    What Goes Into an Ejector System in Lafayette, CO

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Lafayette

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. Then we patch the concrete.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Lafayette, CO

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for decades, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. They work well for new systems and for drop-in replacements, since many Zoeller models fit existing pits and discharge piping as is. Parts are easy to find.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Lafayette

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Lafayette

    Residential ejector systems in Lafayette serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. Each system needs a sealed basin, a pump with a float, a check valve and a vent. The parts are simple. Most problems come from the install, not the hardware, so we take our time on the vent and the lid seal.

    Zoeller Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Lafayette

    Zoeller's sewage line covers home and heavier-duty use, in cast iron with tethered or vertical float switches. The model comes from the pump curve for your lift, and most basement baths need a four-tenths or half horsepower unit that passes two-inch solids. Tall lifts need bigger pumps.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Lafayette, CO

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars send gray water down that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A compact gray water pump handles it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer drops at once, with a lint filter to keep fibers off the impeller. Filters save impellers.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Lafayette, CO

    Businesses in Lafayette, CO with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. Commercial setups often run duplex pumps with an alternating panel, so one pump can fail and the other keeps the restrooms open. Closed restrooms cost business. We size both pumps to carry the full load.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Lafayette, CO

    Upgrading an old ejector does not always mean a new basin. If the basin is sound, we can swap in a better pump, replace the old swing check with a new one, add a full-port shutoff and install a high water alarm, which covers most of what a new system offers for a fraction of the cost. Cracked basins get replaced.
