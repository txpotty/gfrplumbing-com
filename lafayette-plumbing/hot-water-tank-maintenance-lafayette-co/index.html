    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Lafayette

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Lafayette, CO

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is essential for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, saving you from a full tank replacement.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Lafayette

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure spike every heating cycle. High street pressure is common in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Lafayette

    Sediment buildup is a frequent issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, giving you clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is key for extending the life of your unit.

    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Lafayette, CO

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial defense against corrosion. Many larger systems in Colorado draw on mountain snowmelt that runs fairly soft, while plains communities and groundwater sources often deliver harder water. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's badly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Lafayette, CO, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Lafayette

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will suggest immediate replacement to keep your Lafayette home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

Our detailed checks confirm all energy sources are operating correctly and safely.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Lafayette

    A standard flush and inspection usually runs $125 to $250 depending on tank size and access. Anode replacement typically adds $75 to $200 in parts and labor, and powered anodes cost more.

    Routine hot water tank maintenance, such as flushing and anode rod replacement, generally does not require a permit in Lafayette, CO. However, if major repairs or a full replacement are needed, a permit may be required to ensure compliance with local building codes.

    Neglecting maintenance can lead to poor operation, higher energy bills, premature tank failure from corrosion or sediment buildup, and potential safety hazards from a malfunctioning pressure relief valve. Regular service heads off these costly and dangerous issues.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which counts most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it extends the lifespan of your unit.

    Common signs include reduced hot water supply, strange noises like rumbling or popping, rust-colored water, a sulfur or rotten egg smell, or water leaking from the tank. These indicate issues that maintenance can often resolve.

    Our technicians are trained to service all major hot water tank brands, including Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, State, Kenmore and more. We have the know-how to handle various models, whether gas or electric.

    Most flushes and inspections take 45 minutes to 90 minutes. Heavily scaled tanks or anode replacements in tight closets can take a little longer.
