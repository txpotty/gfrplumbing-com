    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Lafayette

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Many need no power. The vent goes through a wall with proper clearances.

    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Lafayette

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Lafayette

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They cost the least, but the vent has to be the right size, slope upward and keep its clearances. Old vents get checked. We replace bad sections.
