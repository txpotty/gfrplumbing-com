    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Lafayette

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. Sometimes a heat pump tank serves the house better, and we will say so.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Lafayette, CO

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. We handle that conversion. Gas and water connections usually line up.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Lafayette, CO

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. Gas is the catch, since tankless units usually need 3/4-inch pipe or larger and a new vent. Before quoting, we check the meter and pipe run, and afterward we cap the old flue and seal the roof. Plan on a full day.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Lafayette

    No hot water from a tankless usually shows up as an error code on the controller, and the code tells us half of what we need to know. Send us a photo of the display. A leaking heat exchanger means replacement, usually done same day or next day.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Lafayette, CO

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Lafayette

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Swaps take an hour or two.

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Lafayette

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Shut the gas and cold isolation valve now. We then find out whether it is a fitting or the heat exchanger, since one is cheap and the other is replacement.
