    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Arvada, CO

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Many larger systems in Colorado draw on mountain snowmelt that runs fairly soft, while plains communities and groundwater sources often deliver more mineral-heavy water. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Arvada, CO

    A full break can flood a yard, wash out soil under walkways and leave your home without water.     Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Arvada, CO

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a quick spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with real information.

    Common signs include a wet or unusually green patch of yard, a hissing sound near the meter, low pressure at every fixture and a water bill that climbs without explanation. Dirty or rusty water can also signal a damaged line. If your meter moves with all fixtures off, you likely have a leak.
