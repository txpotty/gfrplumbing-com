    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Arvada, CO

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Arvada, CO

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Battery backups and alarms add to the total. Your written quote shows each option and its price.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Arvada

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Lots that slope toward the house need this most. The outlet sits well clear of the foundation and the property line.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Arvada, CO

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. We carry common pumps on the truck. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Arvada, CO

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Access is often tight. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Arvada

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Arvada

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Arvada

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. The slab gets patched with new concrete. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Local Sump Pump Installers in Arvada, CO

    Local soil and the way Arvada, CO homes were built both change how a sump system should go in. GFR Plumbing has set pumps in clay, sand and rock. In clay the water stays against the foundation longer, so the pump runs more often and a larger basin helps the motor last. We size for that.
