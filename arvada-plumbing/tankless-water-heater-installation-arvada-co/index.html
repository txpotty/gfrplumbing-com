    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Arvada, CO

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Mountain homes in Colorado frequently rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, manganese, or natural minerals. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Arvada, CO

    Making the switch from a traditional tank water heater to a tankless system involves more than simply swapping units. It often requires changes to gas lines, electrical wiring and venting. GFR Plumbing manages all aspects of this conversion, making sure all upgrades meet current codes and your new tankless unit performs optimally, giving you a modern hot water solution in Arvada, CO.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Arvada

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units. A 40 gallon tank heater uses roughly 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need up to 199,000 BTU, so the gas line often needs to be upsized. Venting changes from a metal flue to PVC or stainless, and the unit moves to a wall location. We remove and dispose of your old tank and patch the old flue penetration.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Arvada

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to deliver hot water to distant fixtures. A recirculation pump or dedicated return line keeps hot water close to faucets, cutting wait time and water waste. Some units have built-in pumps with smart scheduling. We design the system to fit your plumbing layout and usage patterns.
