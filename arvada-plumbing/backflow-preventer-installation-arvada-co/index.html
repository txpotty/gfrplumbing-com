    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Arvada

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Arvada. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. GFR Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties.     Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Arvada

    The cost for backflow preventer installation in Arvada, CO usually ranges from $400 to $1,500, depending on the type of device, its size and the complexity of the installation. A basic residential PVB for an irrigation system is usually on the lower end, while a commercial-grade RPZ assembly costs more because of material and labor requirements.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for new backflow assemblies, especially on commercial properties. We check local rules and pull the permit when required.

    Backflow is when water flows the wrong way through a pipe and carries contaminants into the clean water supply. It can happen from back siphonage during a pressure drop or back pressure from pumps and other equipment.

    An RPZ, or reduced pressure zone device, provides the highest level of protection and is used for high-hazard applications where contaminants could be toxic. A DCVA, or double check valve assembly, is used for low-hazard applications where contaminants are non-toxic. The choice comes down to the risk of contamination at the cross-connection point.

    We install backflow preventers from leading manufacturers known for quality and reliability. Our preferred brands include Watts, Febco, Zurn and Apollo, so you receive a durable, effective device that meets all industry standards.

    It depends on the hazard and local rules. Irrigation often uses a PVB or double check, while high hazard connections like commercial kitchens or chemical injection require an RPZ.

    We install Watts, Febco, Wilkins by Zurn and Apollo assemblies. Each has easy to find repair kits, which keeps future service affordable.

    In Arvada, most backflow preventers must be tested once a year by a certified backflow tester to confirm they are working correctly and providing adequate protection. This is a separate service from installation but is essential for ongoing compliance and safety.
