    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Arvada, CO

    Every brand, from InSinkErator to Waste King and Moen, mounts differently, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit.     Jammed Disposal Repair in Arvada

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. We press the reset after clearing it. Check the trap arm if it jams again.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Arvada, CO

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Most repairs use parts from the truck. Motors on these units are not sold separately.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Arvada

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Arvada

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. We start at the red reset button on the bottom, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. It is wet under a sink. Corroded wire nuts and loose cord connections are common in that damp space, and we replace them rather than just tightening them back down.

    Leaking Disposal Fix in Arvada, CO

    When the drip starts at the top, the putty under the sink flange is usually dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. That fixes most top leaks. Side leaks are usually the discharge gasket or the dishwasher hose clamp.
