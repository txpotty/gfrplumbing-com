    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Arvada

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Tune-Up in Arvada

    GFR Plumbing offers a complete tune-up for conventional hot water tanks, focusing on critical components. This includes draining the tank to remove sediment buildup, which is essential wherever water carries minerals, and inspecting the anode rod for signs of corrosion. We also check heating elements, thermostat settings and all pipe connections for leaks. Our goal is to improve your tank's performance and prevent future issues.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Arvada, CO

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Arvada

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to confirm they are functioning correctly and safely. This preventive check can identify failing elements before they stop heating altogether, avoiding unexpected cold showers for your family in Arvada.

    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Arvada

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Many families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which matters most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it lengthens the lifespan of your unit.

    Common signs include reduced hot water supply, strange noises like rumbling or popping, rust-colored water, a sulfur or rotten egg smell, or water leaking from the tank. These point to issues that maintenance can often resolve.
