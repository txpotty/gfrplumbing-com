    The cost for water heater expansion tank installation in Arvada typically ranges from $250 to $500, including parts and labor.     An expansion tank is essential in closed plumbing systems to absorb the increased water volume and pressure that occur when water is heated. Without it, thermal expansion can damage your water heater, pipes and fixtures, leading to leaks and premature system failure. Many local codes in Colorado call for them.

    While it might seem like a straightforward task, installing a water heater expansion tank involves working with pressurized water lines and getting the sizing and code compliance right.     Usually, installing a water heater expansion tank takes about 1 to 2 hours. This includes assessment, preparation, installation and testing. Our experienced plumbers work efficiently to minimize any disruption to your hot water supply.

    Signs of a failing expansion tank include water dripping from the tank's air valve, rust or corrosion on the tank's exterior or a water heater that frequently cycles on and off.
