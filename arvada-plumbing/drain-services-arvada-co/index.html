    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Services in Arvada

    The cost of drain services in Arvada, CO can vary widely, typically ranging from $150 for a simple clog removal to over $5,000 for a major sewer line replacement. Factors like the type of blockage, its location, and the method required (for example, snaking versus hydro jetting) all influence the final price. We provide clear, upfront pricing after an on-site assessment.

    Hair, grease, soap scum and hard water scale cause most fixture clogs. Main line clogs are often caused by wipes, tree roots or a sag in the pipe.

    A camera inspection is worth it if clogs keep coming back, you are buying an older home or roots are suspected. It shows the exact location and source of the problem.

    A typical drain cleaning for a single clogged fixture can take anywhere from 30 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the severity and location of the clog. More complex issues, like main sewer line clearing or hydro jetting, may require several hours. We provide an expected timeline after our initial assessment.

    While readily available, we usually advise against using chemical drain cleaners. They can be corrosive, potentially damaging your pipes, especially older ones, and are often ineffective against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease. They also pose a risk to your health and the environment. Professional methods are safer for your pipes and more effective.

    Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the interior surfaces of your pipes, thoroughly removing grease, scale, and even tree roots. It is safe for most modern plumbing systems. Our technicians will perform a camera inspection first to ensure your pipes are structurally sound enough to handle the process.

    Minor drain cleaning usually does not require a permit. However, major work like sewer line repair or replacement, especially if it involves excavation or connecting to the city's main sewer, typically requires permits from the local building department. We will advise you and handle the necessary permitting.

    Hydro jetting is safe for pipe in sound condition, including PVC and most cast iron. We camera the line first so we do not jet a pipe that is already cracked or collapsing.
