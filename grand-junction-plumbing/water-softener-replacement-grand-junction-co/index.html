    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Grand Junction

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. It mounts on the main line after any filter and needs no electricity or drain. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.

    Residential Water Softener Replacement in Grand Junction

    Scale in the water heater is the expensive part of hard water. A tank water heater on hard water can lose a noticeable share of its efficiency as scale builds on the bottom, and tankless units scale up even faster. A working softener stops that.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Grand Junction, CO

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. We set it before we leave.

    Long-Term Softener Costs in Grand Junction, CO

    Long-term cost on a softener is mostly salt and water. Compared with a timer unit, a metered valve set efficiently often uses a third less salt, which matters over a fifteen-year life. That is how we compare models.

    Urgent Softener Problems in Grand Junction

    Not every softener failure is urgent. A unit leaking from the valve body or the brine tank overflowing onto the floor is, and we move those calls up the schedule. Bypass it until we arrive.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Grand Junction

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. We replace the media or the whole system, depending on age. Following the install specs keeps the warranty intact.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Grand Junction

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. A sound tank can keep its resin and get a new valve. Or the whole system.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Grand Junction

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. We replace timer units with metered valves that regenerate on gallons used. You will buy less salt. We set the brine refill to your hardness so the system is not dumping salt down the drain every few days.
