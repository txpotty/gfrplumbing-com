    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Grand Junction

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all failed for different reasons. PVC avoids all three of those problems. Schedule 40 or SDR 35 PVC is what most codes call for on a lateral, choosing the wall thickness based on how deep it sits and what drives over it. Glued joints stay sealed.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Grand Junction

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. We dig with a mini excavator and hand dig near utility marks. The old pipe comes out, the trench bottom gets a bed of gravel or sand, and new 4-inch PVC goes in at a steady quarter inch of fall per foot, confirmed with a level on every length. Then the camera.

    Sewer Scope Inspections for Replacement in Grand Junction

    A sewer camera on a push cable goes in through the cleanout. It records every joint, crack and root on the way to the main.
