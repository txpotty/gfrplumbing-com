    The cost of foundation leak repair in Grand Junction, CO can vary widely based on the leak's location, severity, and the repair method required.     You can check your water meter with all fixtures off to verify a leak. Locating the exact spot under a foundation requires specialized equipment and training.

    For extensive foundation leak repairs, especially those involving structural changes, re-piping, or significant excavation, a permit may be required by local building codes. GFR Plumbing will help figure out if a permit is needed for your specific repair and assist with the application process to stay in compliance.

    Many homeowner's insurance policies can cover sudden and accidental foundation leaks, especially the damage caused by the leak, rather than the pipe repair itself. However, coverage varies, so it's best to check with your own insurance provider.

    Common signs of a foundation leak include unexplained increases in water bills, wet spots on floors or walls, mildew odors, visible cracks in the foundation or interior walls, and standing water around the foundation perimeter. If you notice any of these, it is crucial to have your foundation inspected promptly.

    The duration of foundation leak repair depends on the complexity of the leak and the chosen repair method.
