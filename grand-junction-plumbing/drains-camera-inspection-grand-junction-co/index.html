    Frequently Asked Questions About Drains Camera Inspection in Grand Junction

    The cost of a drains camera inspection in Grand Junction, CO usually ranges from $250 to $600. This price can vary depending on the length and accessibility of the pipes needing inspection, as well as the complexity of the issue. We provide a transparent quote before any work begins.

    We use professional-grade drain inspection cameras from leading brands like RIDGID and Vivax-Metrotech. These cameras feature high-resolution imaging, self-leveling heads, and powerful LED lighting to provide clear visuals even in dark, wet conditions. They are also flexible enough to navigate different pipe diameters and bends.

    While primarily for inspecting the inside of pipes, many of our drain cameras are equipped with sondes (transmitters) that allow us to precisely locate the camera's location underground. This can be very useful for locating septic tanks, leach fields, or hidden cleanouts when their exact position is unknown on your property in Grand Junction.

    Inexpensive consumer cameras exist, but they lack the range, locating ability and image quality needed to judge pipe condition. Interpreting the footage also takes experience. A professional inspection gives reliable results.

    Most residential drain camera inspections take between 30 minutes and 1 hour, depending on the length and complexity of the pipe system. More extensive sewer line inspections or those involving multiple access points might take a bit longer, up to 2 hours.

    While small, consumer-grade drain cameras are available, they lack the quality, reach, and diagnostic capabilities of professional equipment.     Most inspections take 45 minutes to 90 minutes. Lines without a cleanout or with heavy buildup can take longer. We give you an estimate before we start.
