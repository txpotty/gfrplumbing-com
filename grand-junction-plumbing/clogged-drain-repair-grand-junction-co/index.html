    Kitchen Sink Drain Clogs in Grand Junction

We remove the trap, run a cable through the trap arm and branch line and clear the buildup back to the main stack. For heavy grease, we may follow with a small jetter to wash the walls clean.

    Laundry and Utility Drain Clogs in Grand Junction, CO

    Washing machines discharge a lot of water quickly, and lint, fabric fibers and detergent residue build up in the standpipe and branch line. A clogged laundry drain often overflows at the standpipe mid-cycle. We clear the line with a cable and recommend a lint trap for the discharge hose. In some homes the laundry line ties into the kitchen line, so we check both.

    Floor Drain and Garage Drain Clearing in Grand Junction

    Floor drains in garages, utility rooms and commercial kitchens collect dirt, sand and debris, especially after storms track grit indoors. When their traps dry out from sitting unused, sewer gas can also escape. We clear the drain, flush the trap and can install trap primers or trap seals to keep odors out.

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Grand Junction, CO

    Bathroom sinks clog from hair, toothpaste and soap that bind together on the pop-up stopper and in the P-trap. Hard water can add a mineral crust that makes the pipe narrower over time. We pull and clean the stopper, clear the trap and run a small cable through the wall line. We can also replace worn pop-up assemblies and corroded tailpieces while we are there.
