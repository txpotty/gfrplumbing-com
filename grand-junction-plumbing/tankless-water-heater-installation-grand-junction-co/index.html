    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Installation in Grand Junction

    Most gas tankless installations cost $3,000 to $6,500 including the unit, labor, venting and typical gas line upgrades. Electric units can be less, though panel upgrades can add $1,000 to $3,000.

    Size depends on how many fixtures run at once and your incoming water temperature. Most homes need a unit rated around 7 to 10 gallons per minute.

    Tankless units eliminate standby heat loss, which can reduce water heating energy use. Savings vary based on household size and usage habits.

    Signs you might need a new water heater, tankless or otherwise, include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, strange noises from the unit or frequent leaks. For tankless systems in particular, frequent error codes or a significant drop in hot water flow could point to a need for service or replacement.

    We install leading tankless water heater brands known for their reliability and efficiency, including Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem. Our plumbers are trained and experienced with the particular requirements of each manufacturer to deliver strong performance.

    A standard tankless water heater installation, replacing an existing tank, typically takes 4 to 8 hours. If extensive plumbing or gas line changes are required, it may take a full day. Our team works efficiently to restore your hot water as quickly as possible.

    With annual descaling, tankless units often last 15 to 20 years. Skipping maintenance in hard water areas can shorten that significantly.

    To maintain efficiency and extend the lifespan of your tankless unit, annual flushing to remove mineral buildup is recommended, especially in areas with hard water. Checking and cleaning the air filter (for gas models) and inspecting connections are also part of routine maintenance. GFR Plumbing offers comprehensive maintenance services.
