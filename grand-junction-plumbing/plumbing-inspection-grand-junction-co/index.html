    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Grand Junction

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Grand Junction, CO

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. Plan on an hour or so. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Grand Junction, CO

    Higher elevations lower the boiling point of water, which affects water heater settings and pressure relief performance in mountain homes. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. We look at pipe insulation. Fixing a cracked hose bibb or adding insulation is a small job compared to a burst pipe in the wall behind it.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Grand Junction

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Colorado houses. That list starts with old supply pipe, worn angle stops, aging water heaters, slow drains and sewer lines with roots or sags. Each gets tested, not guessed at. A corroded fitting or partial blockage found now will not become a ceiling stain or a holiday backup later.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in Grand Junction, CO

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. With all fixtures off, we watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera to trace the leak behind walls, under floors or in the slab. Catching it early keeps it small. Basements are common in Colorado, and shifting soil can crack floor drains, sewer lines, and foundation walls over time.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Grand Junction, CO

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Grand Junction, CO

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. We check for old uncoated brass connectors, missing shutoffs, missing sediment traps and corrosion, and we test joints with an electronic detector. Leaks get reported immediately. Older homes are where we find the most issues, usually at appliance connectors that have been there since the house was built.
