    Why Polybutylene Fails in Grand Junction

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Around Grand Junction, a yearly water heater flush helps clear the scale that collects at the bottom of the tank. Heat makes it worse. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Grand Junction

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. Copper is stiff, heat tolerant and long proven. It costs more. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Grand Junction

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Partial jobs leave risk behind. We run new lines from the main shutoff to every sink, toilet, tub, washer, ice maker and water heater, add new quarter-turn stops and test the whole system before patching.

    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Grand Junction

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. Watch the water meter. With every fixture off, a spinning leak indicator on the meter means water is going somewhere, and we use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to find it without opening walls at random. Once we find it, a temporary fix buys time to schedule the repipe.

    Emergency Polybutylene Pipe Repair in Grand Junction, CO

    A burst poly fitting can push water across a floor or through a ceiling in minutes. Shut the main first. We stop the water, splice in a temporary repair to restore service and then plan the full repipe on a calm schedule. We take photos for the adjuster.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Grand Junction

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. A burst fitting in an attic or wall can cause major damage in minutes. We respond to emergency leaks, make temporary repairs and then schedule a full replacement, because stopping the problem for good means taking the poly system out of service entirely.

    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Grand Junction, CO

We check underneath. Frost-free hose bibs are standard here, but they still crack if a hose is left attached when temperatures drop.
