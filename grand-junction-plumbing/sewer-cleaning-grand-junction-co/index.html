    Cast iron rots from the inside. Homes from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may still have polybutylene supply lines, which are known to fail at fittings.     Dealing with Grease Buildup in Grand Junction, CO

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Grand Junction

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Grand Junction, CO

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust.
