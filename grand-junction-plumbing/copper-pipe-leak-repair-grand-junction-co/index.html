    Preventive Copper Pipe Inspection in Grand Junction, CO

    An inspection catches thin spots before they leak. Plan on an hour.     Thin-Wall Copper Pinhole Repair in Grand Junction

    Older copper, especially Type M, pinholes more than heavier pipe. Thin walls wear through first. We locate the leak precisely and either solder in a new section or use a press fitting near framing, with new pipe that is heavier than the original. Nearby pipe gets a look.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Grand Junction, CO

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Temperature drives it. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, which is why we often add a timer to the pump after fixing the leak. Less run time, less wear.

    Copper Repair vs Repipe Cost in Grand Junction, CO

    A single copper leak repair is affordable and fast. Repeated leaks change the math. If you have had two or more pinhole leaks in a year, the copper is likely thinning throughout the system, and a PEX or Type L copper repipe can cost less over a few years than repeated repairs and drywall patches. We price both.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Grand Junction, CO

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Before leaving a home for a winter trip, keep the heat at a safe setting and consider shutting off the main water supply. Close the main valve. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many bursts are found hours after the cold. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Then we add insulation.

    Commercial Copper Pipe Leak Services in Grand Junction, CO

    Split Copper Line Repair in Grand Junction, CO

    A copper line can split from a freeze, a settling foundation or a hit from a nail. Splits leak hard. Our plumber isolates the water source, cuts out the split and solders in new pipe, while checking nearby pipe for damage from the same cause. Insulation goes on exposed runs.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Grand Junction, CO

The joint gets tested.
