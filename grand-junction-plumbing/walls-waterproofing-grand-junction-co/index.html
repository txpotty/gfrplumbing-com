    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Grand Junction

    Housing in Colorado ranges from older homes with galvanized and cast iron pipe to fast-growing suburbs built with copper and PEX. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks. We seal cracks with injection products, apply interior or exterior waterproofing and install drainage where needed. A sump pump may be added if groundwater builds up.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Grand Junction, CO

    Sump pumps are a critical part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall. We install high-capacity sump pumps designed to handle large volumes of water, keeping your basement dry even during flash floods. Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units.     Drainage System Integration in Grand Junction, CO

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a solid drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Grand Junction, CO properties, so water is managed well and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Grand Junction, CO

This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier, healthier environment in your Grand Junction, CO basement.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Grand Junction

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Grand Junction

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Basements are common in Colorado, and shifting soil can crack floor drains, sewer lines, and basement walls over time.
