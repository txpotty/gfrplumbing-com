    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Grand Junction, CO

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Basements are common in Colorado, and shifting soil can crack floor drains, sewer lines, and basement walls over time.     Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Grand Junction
