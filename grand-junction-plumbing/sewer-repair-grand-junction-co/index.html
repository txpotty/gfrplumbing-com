    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Grand Junction

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Grand Junction

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, so any excavation stays small and targeted. Older homes in Grand Junction often hide two or three problems in one line, and the footage shows every one.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Grand Junction

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. Our phones stay on. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. Daylight digs are safer around buried utilities.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Grand Junction

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. Crushed pipe cannot be lined. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    Emergency Sewer Repair in Grand Junction

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. Our phone is answered after hours. The real repair may be scheduled for the next morning. Until then, we tell you which fixtures are safe to use.
