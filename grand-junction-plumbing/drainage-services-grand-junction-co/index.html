    Most basic drain clearing in Grand Junction, CO runs $150 to $500. A main line costs more than a sink. Jetting costs more than cabling, and sewer repairs run from a few thousand for a spot fix to much more for replacement.

    Most homes never need scheduled drain cleaning. A slow drain is the signal. Root-prone sewers and grease-heavy kitchens benefit from cleaning every one to two years.

    Watch for water pooling near the house after storms. Washed-out mulch tells you too. Inside, a damp crawlspace, a musty basement or efflorescence on the foundation wall all mean water is reaching the house instead of being carried away, and the problem usually starts at a downspout or a low spot.

    Slow sinks and gurgling drains are the early signs. A sewer smell in the yard or basement comes next. In the yard, standing water a day after rain or washed-out mulch means the water has nowhere to go.

    Most sink and toilet clogs clear in under an hour. Main lines take longer. A main line with camera work usually takes about two hours, more if the cleanout is hard to reach.

    Store-bought cleaners rarely clear a real clog. They often make it worse. The chemicals sit in the trap, heat up and can damage older pipe or soften glue joints, and if the clog does not clear, the plumber now has to open a trap full of caustic liquid. A cable is safer.

    Clearing a clog does not need a permit. Pipe repairs often require one. Replacing a section of sewer, adding a cleanout, or connecting a new drain to the sewer generally requires a permit and an inspection, and we handle that paperwork and schedule the inspector as part of the job.
