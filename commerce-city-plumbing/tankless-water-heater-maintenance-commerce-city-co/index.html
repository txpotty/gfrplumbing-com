    Commercial Tankless System Checks in Commerce City, CO

    Commercial tankless water heaters face higher demand and require more frequent, detailed maintenance. Our service for businesses in Commerce City includes detailed inspections of multiple units, checking for proper gas pressure, water flow rates and exhaust venting. We look for any signs of wear on components like igniters, sensors and heat exchangers, which is crucial for businesses that rely on consistent hot water, such as restaurants or salons.

    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Commerce City

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is shielded where it runs outdoors.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Commerce City

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means steadier temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Tankless Water Heater Venting System Check in Commerce City

    Proper ventilation is essential for the safe operation of gas-fired tankless water heaters. Our maintenance service in Commerce City includes a thorough inspection of the venting system for blockages, proper slope and secure connections. We confirm that exhaust gases are safely directed outside and that no carbon monoxide leaks are present, which is vital for the health and safety of your household.

    Preventative Maintenance for All Tankless Brands in Commerce City, CO

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Commerce City

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading accurately and the burner breathing properly.

    Error Code Diagnostics in Commerce City, CO

    Codes like Rinnai LC, Navien E003 or Noritz 90 tell us a lot about what is going wrong. We read the code history, test flame rods, flow sensors and igniters and check gas pressure at the inlet. Many codes trace back to scale or a dirty filter and clear after maintenance. When a part has failed we explain the repair and the cost before doing any work.

    Maintenance Cost Versus Repair Cost in Commerce City, CO

Some manufacturers deny exchanger claims when scale damage is found. Regular maintenance helps protect a unit that can last 20 years when cared for.
