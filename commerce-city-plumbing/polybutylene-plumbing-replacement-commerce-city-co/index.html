    Polybutylene Leak Repair in Commerce City

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. We still make them.     Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Commerce City

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. Watch the water meter. If the meter's leak dial moves with everything off, we track the leak down with acoustic equipment and thermal imaging instead of guessing.     Insurance and Home Sale Documentation in Commerce City

    Some insurers decline or surcharge policies on homes with polybutylene. Lenders may ask as well. After replacement, we provide the permit record, the invoice and photos of the new system, which is the paperwork insurance agents and buyers want when they ask whether the poly is gone.

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Leaving some poly keeps the risk.
