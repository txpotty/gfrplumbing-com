    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Commerce City

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and effective solution for hard water in Commerce City. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are notorious for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water. Our plumbers confirm proper brine tank installation and regeneration settings for strong performance.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Commerce City, CO

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes better sense.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Commerce City, CO

    For homeowners in Commerce City, CO looking for an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they reliably protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you transition from a salt-based unit.

    Replacing Pelican Water Softeners in Commerce City

    Pelican Water Systems are popular for their whole-house filtration and softening solutions, including both salt-based and salt-free options. If your Pelican system in Commerce City is nearing the end of its lifespan or needs a significant upgrade, our technicians are experienced in their replacement. We make sure your new Pelican system or an equivalent quality brand is installed to factory specifications for reliable performance and warranty validity.
