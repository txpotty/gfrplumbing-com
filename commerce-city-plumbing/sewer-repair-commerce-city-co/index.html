    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Commerce City, CO

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. Expansive clay soils are a well-known problem in parts of Colorado, swelling with moisture and heaving slabs, basement floors, and buried pipes. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Commerce City

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Commerce City, CO

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. The joint is the real problem. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.
