    Resin Bed Replacement & Regeneration Issues in Commerce City

    Over time, the resin beads inside your water softener can become fouled, damaged, or depleted, reducing their ability to remove hardness minerals.     Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Commerce City, CO

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed usually costs $1,500 to $3,500.
