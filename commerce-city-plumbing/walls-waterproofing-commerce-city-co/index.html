    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Commerce City, CO

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Commerce City

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Expansive clay soils are a well-known problem in parts of Colorado, swelling with moisture and heaving slabs, basement floors, and buried pipes. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.
