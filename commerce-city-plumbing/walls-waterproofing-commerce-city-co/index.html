    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Commerce City, CO

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first.     Basement Wall Sealing in Commerce City, CO

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Dry air, intense sun, and big daily temperature swings put steady stress on pipes, fittings, and outdoor plumbing in Colorado. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then seal the face with a masonry waterproofer that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Commerce City

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines harder, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Commerce City

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Commerce City, CO

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Homes from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may still have polybutylene supply lines, which are known to fail at fittings. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. A rigid coating on soft brick traps moisture and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with interior drain tile. Old mortar joints get repointed first.
