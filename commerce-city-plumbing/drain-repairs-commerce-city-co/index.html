    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Commerce City

    Simple trap or slip-joint repairs usually cost $150 to $350. Replacing a section of drain pipe usually runs $400 to $1,200, and cast iron stack replacement can range from $2,000 to $6,000 depending on access. We provide a written price before starting.

    We work with all standard drain pipe materials found in Colorado homes, including PVC, ABS, cast iron and copper. Our repairs and replacements use high-quality, code-compliant materials from reputable manufacturers for durability and longevity.

    Minor clogs can sometimes be resolved with plungers or enzymatic drain cleaners. However, for stubborn blockages, recurring issues or suspected pipe damage, it is best to call a professional. DIY attempts with harsh chemical cleaners can sometimes damage pipes or hide a larger problem.

    A dry trap, a cracked drain fitting, a failed wax ring or a blocked vent can all let sewer gas into a room. We locate the source with a smoke test or visual inspection and fix it.
