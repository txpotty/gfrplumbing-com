    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Commerce City

    Older homes often have narrow pits that will not fit a submersible.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Commerce City, CO

    Battery backups earn their keep during outages. Sudden summer thunderstorms bring flash flooding and hail, and heavy runoff can back up storm drains and sewer lines. We add a second pump in the same pit with its own float, wired to a sealed AGM battery on a smart charger that tests itself and beeps when the battery gets weak. The main pump keeps doing the daily work.

    Same-day emergency installs cover pumps that quit in the middle of a storm.     Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Commerce City

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually runs $500 to $1,200 including the pump and check valve. A brand new pit through the slab falls between $1,500 and $3,500, and a battery backup adds roughly $600 to $1,500. Most people add the backup.

    Liberty Pumps, Zoeller and Wayne are our regulars. All three use cast iron housings and reliable switches, and all three have solid parts support through local supply houses, which matters more than a fancy feature list when something breaks. The pit size picks the model.

    Zoeller, Wayne, Little Giant and Liberty Pumps cover most of our installs. We pick the model by horsepower and lift, then by features like a vertical float or a built-in alarm, so the pump fits your pit, your budget and the amount of water your Commerce City basement actually takes on. Cast iron is our default.

    Permit rules vary by town, including Commerce City. A straight pump replacement usually does not, but a new pit, a new outlet or a new exit through the foundation wall often does, and we sort that out before any concrete gets cut. The paperwork is on us.

    Our installs carry a written warranty on both parts and labor. You will see the exact length in your service agreement, and the pump manufacturer adds its own coverage, which on a quality cast iron pump usually runs three to five years from the date of purchase. We handle the registration.

    Grinding or rattling during a cycle is the first warning. Rust on the housing and constant on-off cycling are others, along with a pump that does not start during a heavy rain even though the pit is full to the float. After about ten years, plan on replacing it.

    Swapping an identical plug-in pump is doable for a handy homeowner.
