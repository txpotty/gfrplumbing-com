    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Commerce City, CO

    The flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube and body are where leaks start. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. That seal cannot be replaced.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Commerce City

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A steady whine is different. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. We pull it, clean the chamber walls and replace the guard if it is torn.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Commerce City

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Next we pull the object with pliers. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Commerce City

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Bad bearings mean replacement.
