    Sink Parts Replacement in Commerce City, CO

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. We match finish where we can. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Commerce City

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. It usually means worn O-rings on a swivel spout or a failed base gasket, and on some faucets it means the body has cracked and the whole faucet needs replacing. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Commerce City

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Basket strainers loosen and leak around the putty, pull-down sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and the disposal flange works loose from years of vibration. We fix the part that is wet. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Commerce City, CO

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. Lines and stops get replaced together. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Commerce City, CO

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. Replace the sink only if the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain has rusted into the bowl. When it is close, you get both prices. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Commerce City, CO

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. We replace bad traps with a matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Commerce City

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. When the epoxy under the counter fails, the bowl drops a quarter inch, the caulk line splits, and water from the counter runs straight down into the cabinet. New clips go in and the bowl gets pulled tight. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Commerce City

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Frost-free hose bibs are standard here, but they still burst if a hose is left attached when temperatures drop. Sun breaks down plastic traps and supply lines left outside, and yard dirt and grass clippings clog the drain faster than kitchen scraps ever do. We fix leaks at the hose bib too. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.
