    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Commerce City, CO

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Uncoated brass connectors from decades ago crack at the flare fittings and should be replaced on sight. We replace them with coated stainless connectors. Each appliance also gets a shutoff you can reach and a sediment trap where required, and we check that the connector length and diameter match the appliance rating.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Commerce City, CO

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. We size outdoor lines for the full load. Repairs and new runs are tested, buried at code depth and marked with tracer wire.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Commerce City, CO

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Commerce City, CO. The inspector checks pipe sizing, material, support spacing, shutoff locations, sediment traps and the pressure test result before signing off. We handle the paperwork. A permitted repair also gives you a paper trail for insurance and for any future sale.

    Gas Leak Detection in Commerce City

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose. We isolate sections of the system and use electronic combustible gas detectors to narrow the source, then confirm with soap solution at fittings, valves and unions. After we locate the leak, we price the repair in writing.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Commerce City, CO

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Gas Shut Off by the Utility in Commerce City

    When your utility shuts off gas for a leak, you may be without hot water, heat or cooking until a licensed plumber fixes it. Our plumber finds and fixes the leak, then runs the test your gas company requires before unlocking the meter. The truck carries what we need. We do not leave until the gauge holds steady and the system is ready for the utility.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Commerce City, CO

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. We install the branch line, add an accessible shutoff valve and a sediment trap where the code calls for one, then connect the appliance with a new coated flexible connector. If the existing appliance line is leaking or damaged, we fix it on the same visit.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Commerce City

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Expansive clay soils are a well-known problem in parts of Colorado, swelling with moisture and heaving slabs, basement floors, and buried pipes.
