    Cast Iron and Clay Pipe Condition Assessment in Commerce City

    Many older homes have cast iron drain lines under the floor and clay sewer laterals outside. Cast iron can corrode and develop scale, cracks and channeling along the bottom of the pipe. Clay joints shift and let roots in. Our camera inspection shows how much life the pipe has left so you can plan repairs on your schedule.

    Belly and Offset Detection in Commerce City, CO

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds water and waste. Offsets happen when pipe sections shift out of alignment, often because of soil movement. Expansive clay soils are a well-known problem in parts of Colorado, swelling with moisture and lifting slabs, basement floors, and buried pipes. The camera shows these defects clearly, and we mark their location for targeted repair.

    Sewer Scope for Slow Drains in Commerce City

    If you are experiencing consistently slow drains or recurring clogs, a sewer scope inspection can pinpoint the exact cause. Instead of guessing, our camera can show whether the issue is a simple blockage, a pipe belly or more serious damage like a collapse. This precise diagnosis in Commerce City allows us to recommend the most effective and least invasive repair, saving you from unnecessary digging or repeated efforts.

    Pre-Purchase Sewer Line Inspection in Commerce City

    Standard home inspections typically do not include a camera look inside the sewer line. A pre-purchase inspection shows whether the line has roots, cracks, bellies or other problems before you close. That information can help you negotiate repairs or price with the seller. We provide a video and written report that fits most real estate timelines.

    Post-Clearing Sewer Camera Inspection in Commerce City

    After a backup is cleared, a camera inspection reveals why it happened. It might be grease, roots, a broken section or an object stuck in the line. Knowing the cause helps you avoid repeat backups. We often recommend an inspection after any main line cleaning.

    Locating Lost Items and Pipe Mapping in Commerce City, CO

    Beyond diagnosing damage, our sewer cameras are also invaluable for locating lost items dropped down drains, such as jewelry or small tools.
