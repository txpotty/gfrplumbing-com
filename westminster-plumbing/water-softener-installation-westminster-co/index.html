    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Westminster

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. Sizing comes first. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Westminster, CO

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. GFR Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Westminster, CO

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Westminster

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. Each install follows the brand's specs for drain, bypass and power, then we program it to your water test. Connections and setup included. A loop saves time.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Westminster, CO

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. An existing loop keeps the price down, and adding a loop adds labor and materials. Carbon tanks cost extra. Your written quote lists equipment, labor and setup.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Westminster

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. Pairs well with a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Westminster

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Little upkeep is needed. They work best on moderate water.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Westminster

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Then we program it. Regeneration time is usually set for 2 a.m.
