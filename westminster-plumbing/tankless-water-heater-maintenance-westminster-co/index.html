    Annual Tankless Checkup in Westminster, CO

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. Gas pressure at the inlet gets read with a manometer at full fire and checked against the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. You get a written report with the readings, so next year's visit has something to compare against.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Westminster

    Around Westminster, a yearly water heater flush helps clear the scale that collects at the bottom of the tank. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. Flow comes back. Temperatures stop swinging, and the unit quits shutting down on high-limit.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Westminster, CO

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. Gas pressure gets read with every unit firing together. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.
