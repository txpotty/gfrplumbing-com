    PEX Water Line Installation in Westminster, CO

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Westminster, CO

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Code says no more than 80 psi inside the house. A quarter turn and the water is off.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Westminster, CO

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorine in the water breaks it down from the inside. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some home insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Worth replacing.

    Type L vs. Type K Copper in Westminster, CO

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is lighter and mostly goes indoors. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against stone contact and soil chemistry. Same fittings, different pipe.
