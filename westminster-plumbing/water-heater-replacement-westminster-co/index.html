    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Westminster

    Electric water heaters in Westminster usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. Tank leaks are the end, so we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to your existing circuit. Hot water is back the same day.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in Westminster, CO

    A standard tank replacement is usually the most affordable option in Westminster, CO. Tankless and hybrid units cost more up front but can lower operating costs, and tankless often lasts 15 to 20 years versus about 8 to 12 for a tank. Ask for each option.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Westminster

    Businesses in Westminster put far more hours on a heater than a house, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons and offices all draw differently, so we size by peak gallons per hour and required temperature, which may mean a larger tank, a booster or a tankless rack. We schedule around your hours.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Westminster

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Westminster, CO

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Westminster, CO

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on most tank jobs. Bradford White is trade-only and durable, Rheem offers range and parts, and AO Smith brings plenty of sizes, so the pick comes down to fit, warranty and stock. Any of the three works.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Westminster

    Homes from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may still have polybutylene supply lines, which are known to fail at fittings. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. We bring the help.
