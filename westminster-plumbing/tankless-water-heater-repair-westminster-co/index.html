    Emergency Tankless Repair in Westminster

    A tankless unit that leaks internally or shows a fault you cannot clear needs prompt attention. We prioritize no-hot-water calls and arrive with common sensors, igniters and filters. If a major component like a heat exchanger has failed, we check warranty coverage and order the part. We can often restore partial operation in the meanwhile.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Westminster, CO

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and confirm proper drainage. PVC venting is also inspected for slope and sagging.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Repair in Westminster

    Commercial tankless water heaters in Westminster face heavier usage and require durable repair solutions. Our commercial tankless water heater repair services address larger capacity units, complex manifold systems and often more serious scaling issues. We prioritize fast, efficient service to minimize disruption to your business operations.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Westminster, CO

    Rinnai is a popular tankless brand, and its units can face challenges such as scaling from hard water or issues with their flame rod. Our technicians in Westminster, CO are adept at Rinnai tankless water heater repair, focusing on descaling services, fan motor replacements and fixing issues with gas valve assemblies. We make sure your Rinnai system continues to provide reliable, on-demand hot water, following manufacturer specifications.

    Ignition Failure Repair in Westminster, CO

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet. Undersized gas lines are a frequent hidden cause when a tankless unit replaced a tank. We correct the issue so the unit lights reliably.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Westminster

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water faster. Pump failures, check valve problems or wrong schedule settings can cause long waits or lukewarm water. We test the pump, check valves and controls and set the schedule to match your daily routine. A properly tuned system saves both water and gas.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Westminster

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, limiting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is wise for most homes on hard water.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Westminster

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace failed parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.
