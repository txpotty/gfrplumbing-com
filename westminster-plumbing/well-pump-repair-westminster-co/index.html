    Well Pump Problems We Fix in Westminster, CO

    Low Water Pressure Diagnosis in Westminster

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. A clogged sediment filter, a pressure switch set too low or a leak in the yard line can all cause it. In some cases the pump is fine and the well just cannot keep up with demand. We test each one.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Westminster, CO

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Spring snowmelt raises groundwater in Colorado, which can push water into basements and strain sump pumps. Lightning and power surges damage those parts more than anything else. After testing the capacitor, relay and overload, we replace the bad part and suggest a surge arrestor if the box lacks one. An arrestor costs little.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Westminster

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Sensor failures and drive errors are the usual problems. GFR Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Westminster, CO well. Usually the electronics are fine.

    Jet Pump Repair in Westminster, CO

    Shallow wells and booster systems often use jet pumps. Common problems are loss of prime, a leaking shaft seal and a worn impeller. Our crew in Westminster restores prime, replaces worn parts and checks the suction line for leaks that let air in. That matters most where water levels rise and fall through the year, since a dropping level can starve the pump and cost it prime.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Westminster

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up, run quietly and last, but motors, impellers and splices still wear out. We pull the pump with the right equipment, test it and replace only what failed. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Westminster, CO well.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Westminster, CO

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. A fault code on the drive points us in the right direction. We troubleshoot the drive, the pressure sensor and the wiring, then repair or replace the failed part and verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Westminster, CO

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Most failures start with an air leak somewhere in the suction line. GFR Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. Nonstop running without pressure usually means lost prime or an air leak.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Westminster, CO

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. The air charge gets checked and the bladder inspected, then the tank is recharged or replaced so the pump runs a full minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.
