    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Maintenance in Westminster

    The cost of water heater maintenance in Westminster, CO can range from $120 to $250, based on the type of water heater (tank or tankless) and the services required.     Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend annual maintenance for your water heater. In homes with harder water, flushing every 6 to 12 months can be beneficial to prevent sediment buildup.

    During a water heater flush, we drain the tank completely and then refill it, letting water flow out through the drain valve until it runs clear. This process removes mineral deposits and sediment that collect at the bottom of the tank, which can hurt efficiency and cause damage.
