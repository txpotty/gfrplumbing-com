    Descaling Tankless Units in Westminster

    Error codes often mean scale. Scale in the exchanger cuts flow and trips the unit's safety shutoff. A pump kit handles it. Most makers recommend descaling at least yearly with hard water.

    Water Heater Problem Diagnosis in Westminster, CO

    Something sounds or feels wrong. Rumbling, short hot water or a wet pan get diagnosed on the spot, with a straight answer on repair or replace. We lay out choices. No pressure either way.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Westminster, CO

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. Leak checks, flame inspection, burner cleaning and a draft test are part of every gas visit. Carbon monoxide is the concern. We also test the thermocouple.

    Yearly Service Plans in Westminster, CO

    Forgetting is the main reason tanks go unflushed. A yearly plan puts the visit on the calendar, sends a reminder a week ahead, and gives you priority scheduling and repair discounts if the heater ever acts up between visits. We track it for you. Long-term savings add up.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Westminster

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. We flush until it runs clear. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.
