    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Westminster

    Many larger systems in Colorado draw on mountain snowmelt that runs fairly soft, while plains communities and groundwater sources often deliver harder water. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Westminster, CO

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Westminster

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. Expansion tanks, drain pans and softeners get a look as well, and a quick hardness test tells us whether the softener is actually doing its job or just using salt.
