    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Westminster, CO

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and reset the slope to the quarter inch per foot most codes call for on residential laterals.     Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Westminster, CO

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Two access holes, sometimes one. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. We tell you which one your pipe can take.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Westminster
