    Sewer Lateral Lining in Westminster

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Clay and Orangeburg fail often. Over time, clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots, so we jet, scope and then pick lining or bursting. The new line restores full flow and resists roots.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Westminster

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. We inflate an epoxy-soaked liner against the wall and cure it with heat or UV, leaving a smooth pipe with no joints. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Commercial Trenchless Pipe Repair in Westminster

    Commercial properties have sewer and storm lines under parking lots, sidewalks and buildings. Concrete is costly to replace. Trenchless repair avoids that demolition and the disruption to tenants and customers, and we line larger diameter pipe and multiple laterals on a schedule that limits the impact on business.

    Cost-Effective Underground Pipe Repair in Westminster, CO

    People are often surprised at the final number. Trenchless can cost less. Per foot, lining can look pricey, but once you add the cost of tearing out and replacing a driveway, a patio and the landscaping above an open trench, the trenchless bid usually comes out ahead.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Westminster, CO

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. Bursting or boring works. New HDPE or PEX goes in along the old path by bursting, or along a new one by directional boring, leaving lawns and drives alone. The line is pressure tested before service is turned back on.

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Prep is half the job. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. A clean pipe holds a liner best.
