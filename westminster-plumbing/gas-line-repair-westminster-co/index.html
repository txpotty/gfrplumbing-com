    Emergency Gas Line Repair in Westminster

    If you smell gas, leave the building, skip the light switches and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Then call us. Once the utility has shut off the meter and made the area safe, we locate the leak, make the repair and run the pressure test needed before service can be restored. Pilots get relit once gas is back. Most emergency repairs are done in a single visit with parts from the truck.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Westminster, CO

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Gas Leak Detection in Westminster

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose. Our plumbers isolate the system section by section, sweep it with an electronic detector and confirm the exact spot with soap solution. Once we find it, you get a written repair price before work starts.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Westminster

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. Usually under an hour per appliance. Ranges, dryers, water heaters and furnaces all need proper connections, and most also need a sediment trap ahead of the appliance gas valve.

    Honest Gas Line Repair Pricing in Westminster, CO

    Gas leaks are usually a surprise expense, so the price should not be another surprise. You get a written estimate covering the work, the parts and any permit fees before anything begins.     Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Westminster, CO

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. Many tankless heaters are rated at 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, which can be more than double what an old tank heater used, so the line feeding it often has to be upsized back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Westminster

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Westminster building officials. Once it passes, the utility can restore service and we come back to relight appliances if needed.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Westminster

An electronic detector does the rest. Soap solution at the suspect fitting confirms it with bubbles, and then we repair the joint, replace the section or swap the connector before testing again.
