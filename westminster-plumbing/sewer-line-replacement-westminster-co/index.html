    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Westminster

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows close behind. This usually needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no seams for roots to enter.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Westminster, CO

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the correct grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we account for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we cover it.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Westminster, CO

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement replaces pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is a great fit when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Westminster and can often be finished faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Westminster

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is pulled in and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a good option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you plainly if your line is a candidate.
