    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Westminster, CO

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Sometimes the vent is blocked. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Westminster, CO

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Westminster

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. We carry Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler cartridges. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Westminster, CO

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. Pop-up linkage parts sit in a damp cabinet all day, and the pivot nut is the leak we find most often on bathroom sinks. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Emergency Sink Repair in Westminster, CO

    Shut the angle stops first. Turn both oval handles under the sink clockwise until they stop, and if they spin freely or keep weeping, go to the main shutoff and close it before calling us.     Sink Parts Replacement in Westminster, CO

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Westminster, CO

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.
