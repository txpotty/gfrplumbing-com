    Slab Leak Detection in Parker

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often indicated by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. GFR Plumbing uses dedicated acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is essential to preventing major foundation damage.

    Main Service Line Leak Detection in Parker, CO

    The line from the meter to your house carries full pressure and can leak underground for a long time. Wet soil, soft spots or oddly green patches of yard may be the only clues.
