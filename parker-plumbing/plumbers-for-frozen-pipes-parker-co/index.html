    Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Parker, CO

    Post-Thaw Pipe Inspection & Repair in Parker, CO

    A pipe that froze and thawed without leaking can still have a hidden crack. We pressure test it to find out. With the line isolated and a gauge on it, even a hairline split shows up as a pressure drop within minutes, and finding it now beats finding it later as a stain on the ceiling.

    Residential Frozen Pipe Thawing in Parker

    A faucet that trickles or goes dry on a cold morning usually means ice in the line feeding it. A thermal camera shows the frozen section. We thaw it with the faucet open so melting water can escape.

    Hose Bib and Outdoor Faucet Repair in Parker, CO

    Outdoor faucets freeze easily, especially with a hose left connected. The crack is often inside the wall. You will not see it until the next time you turn on the faucet in spring and water pours into the basement or crawlspace, so we replace split hose bibs with frost-free models that shut off inside the heated wall.

    Thawing Drain Lines and Sewer Pipes in Parker

    Drain lines can freeze too, usually where a line runs through an unheated crawlspace or an exterior wall. You will see the backup first. We thaw drains with a steam or hot-water jetter fed through the cleanout, then check the slope, because a drain that holds water in a sag is the kind that freezes again next time.

    Frozen Water Line Diagnosis in Parker, CO
