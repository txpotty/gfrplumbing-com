    Leaking Water Heater Repair in Parker, CO

    Not every leak means a dead tank. Drips from the relief valve, the drain valve or a supply connection are repairable for a modest cost. A leak from the tank body or the bottom seam usually means the inner lining has failed, and that tank needs to be replaced soon. We protect your floors either way.

    Electric Tank Repair in Parker, CO

    Four parts can fail on an electric tank, two elements and two thermostats. Scale from hard water buries the lower element and burns it out. We test resistance and voltage, replace the failed part and reset the high limit, then watch a full heating cycle before we call it done. A breaker that keeps tripping gets traced first.

    Gas Control and Thermocouple Repair in Parker

    Thermocouples, flame sensors, igniters and gas control valves cause most gas heater failures. Each part gets tested, the burner gets cleaned and the vent gets a draft check. Pilots that light and quit usually mean a weak thermocouple or a plugged arrestor screen. Most are done in under two hours.

    Gas Water Heater Repair in Parker, CO

    Gas units add a burner, a pilot or igniter and a gas control valve to the list of parts that can fail. The thermocouple and the control valve cause most no-heat calls. We leak test each gas connection after the repair. Draft at the vent gets checked too.

    Electric Water Heater Element Repair in Parker

    Lukewarm water or hot water that runs out in five minutes usually means a burned-out element or a stuck thermostat. We check each element with an ohmmeter. A shorted element will trip the breaker, while an open one just sits there cold, and the readings tell us which one is which. Common elements and thermostats are stocked.

    Relief Valve and Fitting Leak Repair in Parker, CO

    T&P valves drip when the tank is overheating or the house pressure spikes, so replacing the valve alone may not stop it. We check the thermostat setting and the house pressure first. A missing expansion tank is a common cause on homes with a check valve or pressure regulator. Leaking fittings get redone.
