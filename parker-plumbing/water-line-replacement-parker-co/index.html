    Service Line Replacement Options in Parker, CO

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Parker

    Galvanized steel was common in homes built before the 1960s. The zinc coating wears off and the steel rusts from the inside. After a few decades the opening can be half its original size, which is why pressure drops and the water runs orange after a shutoff, and no amount of cleaning brings it back. It has to come out.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Parker

    Your service line runs from the meter or curb stop to the main shutoff inside the house, usually as 3/4-inch or 1-inch pipe. After a second leak in a new spot, we stop recommending patches. We dig at both ends, pull or cut out the old pipe, and run new line at the depth local code calls for, with fresh connections at the meter and at the shutoff. Most homes are done in a day.

    Damage Assessment Before Replacement in Parker

    We start at the meter. If the little dial on the meter is spinning with every fixture off, water is leaving somewhere. From there we listen along the line with an acoustic ground mic, check the house side for a toilet or softener running, and sometimes push a camera into the line when we suspect root damage or a crushed section, so the hole goes in the right spot.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Parker

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Parker

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? We will usually just fix that spot. Three leaks on a galvanized line from the 1950s is different, because the pipe is thin everywhere and the next leak is already forming, so replacing the whole run is cheaper over five years. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Parker, CO

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Before leaving a home for a winter trip, keep the heat at a safe setting and consider shutting off the main water valve. PEX can swell a little in a freeze and go back to shape, where copper splits. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and we keep it out of direct sun at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Emergency Water Line Replacement in Parker

    Water bubbling up through the lawn or the street side of the sidewalk means a break, not a drip. First step, shut off the main valve. If that does not stop it, the break is on your side of the meter but before the valve, and we will shut it at the curb stop with a meter key and plan the replacement from there, usually starting the same or next day.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Parker, CO

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some home insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Worth replacing.

    Being local means we know how service lines in Parker are usually laid out. Some run straight from the street, others jog around old additions.
