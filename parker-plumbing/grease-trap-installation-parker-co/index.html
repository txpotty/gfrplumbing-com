    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Parker

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, typically under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. GFR Plumbing installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Parker for maximum efficiency.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Parker, CO

    Hydro-mechanical grease traps, also known as automatic grease removal units, offer an active solution for FOG management in Parker, CO. These units often include internal baffles and flow control mechanisms to enhance separation efficiency. We provide professional installation of various hydro-mechanical models, ensuring all electrical and plumbing connections are correctly made for reliable performance and automated FOG removal.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Parker, CO

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the involved excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, ensuring compliance with all local Colorado ordinances for waste management.
