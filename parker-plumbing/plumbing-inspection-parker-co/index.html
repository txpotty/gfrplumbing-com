    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots.     Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Parker

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked.     Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Parker, CO

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Water Heater Inspections in Parker

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.
