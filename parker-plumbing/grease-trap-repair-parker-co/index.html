    Flow Control Device Repair in Parker

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is essential for a trap to pass inspection.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Parker, CO

    The internal baffles of a grease trap are crucial for separating FOG from wastewater. If these are damaged or dislodged, the trap becomes inefficient, allowing grease to escape into the sewer system. We repair or replace broken baffles and cracked lids in Parker, CO, ensuring your trap functions as designed. A secure lid also prevents odors and keeps pests out, maintaining hygiene around the unit.

    Emergency Grease Trap Repair in Parker

    A failed trap during a busy shift can force you to stop service. We respond quickly to grease trap emergencies and work to get you running the same day when possible. Our crews carry common replacement parts and pumping equipment. We can pump, repair and test the trap in one visit in many cases. Fast service limits lost revenue and health code concerns.

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Parker, CO

    Damaged lids and failed gaskets let odors escape and create safety hazards. Outdoor risers can crack from vehicle traffic or ground movement. We replace lids, install new gaskets and repair or replace risers. Traffic-rated lids are installed where trucks or cars drive over the interceptor. These repairs keep odors in and keep people protected.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Parker, CO

    Grease that hardens in the lines before or after the trap can cause backups even when the trap itself is clean. We use hydro jetting to clear the pipe and remove buildup. A camera inspection helps us confirm the line is clear and check for damage. If we find broken or sagging pipe, we can repair the section. This keeps water and waste moving freely.

    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in Parker

    Persistent foul odors coming from your grease trap are a sign of inefficiency, excessive FOG accumulation, or bacterial growth. Beyond cleaning, our repair services address the root cause, whether it's poor ventilation, sludge buildup, or structural issues preventing proper flow. We ensure all components are working correctly to contain and process waste effectively, eliminating unpleasant smells that can drive away customers in Parker.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Parker, CO

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and important grease trap repairs.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Parker

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay up to code.
