    Sensor Urinal Installation in Parker, CO

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. Battery or hardwired valves get a tuned sensing range and an optional sanitary flush for idle periods. Batteries last a few years. Hardwired units need an electrician first.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Parker

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. We install Sloan Royal or Zurn AquaFlush valves on a 3/4 inch supply, adjust the control stop for proper flow, and set the vacuum breaker the right distance above the bowl so it protects the drinking water. Handles can go left or right. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    Commercial Urinal Installation in Parker

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. Each unit goes on a carrier with new gaskets and stainless bolts, paired with a matched Sloan or Zurn valve. We check partition spacing too. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Parker, CO

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Low-flow and waterless units leave mineral buildup in the line, so proper slope and a cleanout matter. Venting stops gurgle and odor. We tie in within code distance.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Parker

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. A hairline crack in vitreous china will eventually leak, and a bowl stained by years of hard scale often cannot be cleaned back to white, so a new fixture on the old carrier is usually the fastest fix. Carrier bolts get inspected first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Parker

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Rusted bolts and seized valves often have to be cut, so we protect the tile while working. New gaskets always go in. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.
