    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Parker, CO

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Parker

    Standard tank heaters remain the most common type, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. Hot water by evening.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Parker

    Running out of hot water often means the family has outgrown it. A bigger tank or a higher first-hour rating handles more back-to-back showers, and sometimes a 50-gallon high-recovery unit matches a 75 in the same footprint. Bigger is not always needed.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Parker, CO

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on most tank jobs.     Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Parker

    Businesses in Parker put far more hours on a heater than a house, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons and offices all draw differently, so we size by peak gallons per hour and required temperature, which may mean a larger tank, a booster or a tankless rack. Downtime costs money.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Parker, CO

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Parker, CO homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Every joint gets checked.
