    Efficient PEX Repiping in Parker, CO

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in Parker, CO. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Parker

    Copper piping offers exceptional durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Parker. Copper also inherently inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Parker

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Expansive clay soils are a well-known problem in parts of Colorado, swelling with moisture and lifting slabs, basement floors, and buried pipes. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Parker

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first-time leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save money.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Parker

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics.
