    Slab Leak Repiping in Parker

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Basements are common in Colorado, and shifting soil can crack floor drains, sewer lines, and basement walls over time. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Parker

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history.     PEX Repiping in Parker, CO

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. It resists scale and chlorine, handles a freeze better than copper and costs less to install, which is why most of our repipes use it. We use PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe open. Flow stays strong at the last shower.
