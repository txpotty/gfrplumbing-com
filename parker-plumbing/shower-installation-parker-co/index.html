    Budget Shower Upgrades in Parker, CO

    Budget showers can still be built right. A fiberglass or acrylic unit with a quality pressure-balance valve costs far less than a tiled shower, and when it is set on a mortar bed with a sealed drain, it lasts decades. The valve is where to spend. The quote covers everything up front.

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in Parker

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. We install heavy-duty commercial valves, push-button or metered controls when you want to limit run time, and drains sized for several showers at once. ADA stalls get handheld units. We phase the work to keep some showers running.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Parker

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. A linear drain often works best. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Commercial Shower Installation in Parker, CO

    Locker rooms need showers that take hundreds of uses a day. A master mixing valve can protect the whole room from scalding.

    Shower Upgrades Without a Full Remodel in Parker

    Not every upgrade needs demolition. Replacing an old pan, converting a tub to a shower or swapping in a new valve and trim can make a big difference without tearing the bathroom down to the studs. You see each option's price. They tend to wrap up within a week.

    Digital Shower Systems in Parker, CO

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. The digital valve mounts in an accessible spot, often in a vanity or a closet, and needs low-voltage wiring to the controller plus a dedicated outlet near the valve. The outlet is an electrician's job. We plumb the valve and outlets.

    Emergency Shower System Replacement in Parker, CO
