    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Castle Rock, CO

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed far enough out that it drains away from the house.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Castle Rock

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and fasten it with termination bar. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Castle Rock

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Long heating seasons keep boilers and water heaters busy, so annual maintenance pays off in fewer midwinter breakdowns. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Castle Rock

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall.
