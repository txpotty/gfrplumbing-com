    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Castle Rock, CO

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. Cracks start along the bottom. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, so we cut out the failing section and replace it with ABS or PVC using shielded transition couplings. If the whole stack is failing, we price it separately.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Castle Rock, CO

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. It clogs with silt. Expansive clay soils are a well-known problem in parts of Colorado, swelling with moisture and lifting slabs, basement floors, and buried pipes. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, and we locate the bad section on camera before replacing it with wrapped perforated pipe in washed stone. The outlet gets checked as well.

    Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Castle Rock, CO

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Sometimes it is a bad glue joint. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then replace that section with new pipe joined by shielded couplings that fit the old and new outside diameters. The repair gets a flow test.

    Clogged Drain Repair in Castle Rock

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Some come back fast. The footage tells us what to fix.

    Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Castle Rock, CO

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Gaskets fail too.     Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Castle Rock, CO
