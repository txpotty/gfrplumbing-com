    Garage and Workshop Faucets in Castle Rock

    Workshop and garage sinks get abused. We install faucets with metal handles and a threaded spout, anchored to the wall or the tub deck so a pulled hose does not twist the supply lines. We check the flow at full open. If the tub drains slow, we clear the trap while we are there.

    Touchless Faucet Installation in Castle Rock

    Touchless faucets run on a solenoid valve. We mount the control box high on the cabinet wall, away from where water collects, and power it with batteries or a plug-in adapter. Scale is hard on solenoids, and we install the inline filter when the maker calls for one. Sensor range gets tested last.

    Restaurant and Office Faucets in Castle Rock, CO

    Restaurant pre-rinse units mount to the wall and the sink deck. The spring arm and spray valve take a beating, so we anchor the wall bracket into blocking and use check valves on the supply lines. Hand-wash sinks get wrist blades. We work after close so the line keeps running.

    High-End Faucet Installation in Castle Rock, CO

    High-end faucets ship with more parts in the box. Premium Brizo and Grohe models often come with a separate valve body and finish-matched trim, and the assembly order matters. We use padded wrenches on the finish. Brushed gold and matte black scratch easily, so nothing bare and metal touches the trim.

    Utility Sink Faucet Installation in Castle Rock, CO

    Utility tubs see hot water and harsh cleaners. A utility faucet needs a brass body and a firm mount, because people yank hoses off it, and a loose faucet cracks its own supply connection. Vacuum breakers go on the spout. We check these joints twice, since a garage tub can drip for months before anyone notices.

    Adding an Outdoor Faucet in Castle Rock

    Adding a new hose bib means tapping a supply line and running pipe through the wall. Before leaving a home for a winter trip, keep the heat at a safe setting and consider shutting off the main water supply. The bib type and the pipe route follow from that, and anywhere freezing is possible we go with a frost-free sillcock. Sealant goes around the wall penetration. It comes with a built-in vacuum breaker.

    Commercial-Style Kitchen Faucets in Castle Rock

    Commercial-style spring spout faucets are tall. Many spring spouts stand over 20 inches, so we measure up to the window sill or cabinet before you buy. Spare holes can take a soap pump. The hose weight goes where the spout docks cleanly.

    Wall-Mount Faucet Installation in Castle Rock, CO

    Wall-mount faucets start before the drywall closes. We set the rough valve for the sink height and the finished wall depth, because a valve set a half inch too deep leaves the trim floating off the tile. Trim goes on after tile. That frees up the counter and leaves nothing to clean around at the deck.
