    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Castle Rock, CO

    Correct sizing and smart placement are critical for a grease trap's effectiveness. Installing a unit that is too small will lead to frequent clogs and overflow, while an oversized one can be an avoidable expense. Our plumbers meticulously calculate the required capacity based on your kitchen's water flow, fixture count, and operating hours, ensuring optimal performance for your Castle Rock, CO business.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Castle Rock, CO

    Hydro-mechanical grease traps, also known as automatic grease removal units, offer an active solution for FOG management in Castle Rock, CO. These units often include internal baffles and flow control mechanisms to enhance separation efficiency. We provide professional installation of various hydro-mechanical models, ensuring all electrical and plumbing connections are correctly made for optimal performance and automated FOG removal.

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Castle Rock

    Gravity grease traps are a common and effective solution for many commercial kitchens in Castle Rock. These passive systems use the natural separation of FOG from water due to density differences. Our plumbers are experts in installing gravity grease traps, ensuring proper sizing, correct inlet and outlet piping, and convenient access for future cleaning and maintenance to maximize their efficiency.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Setup in Castle Rock, CO

    Smaller establishments or specific kitchen stations in Castle Rock, CO may benefit from compact under-sink grease traps. These units are well suited for capturing FOG at the source, preventing it from entering the main plumbing lines. Our team expertly installs these smaller units, ensuring proper sizing, sealing, and connection to your existing plumbing system for reliable waste management and odor control.

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Castle Rock

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid without trouble.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Castle Rock, CO

    Small under-sink hydromechanical traps generally cost much less to install than large outdoor interceptors. Indoor units are typically a few thousand dollars installed, while in-ground interceptors can run well into five figures with excavation and concrete work. Pricing depends on unit size, location, soil conditions and whether we need to cut and patch floors. We give you a written estimate that includes permits and inspection. That way there are no surprises during construction.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Castle Rock, CO

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the involved excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, maintaining compliance with all local Colorado ordinances for waste management.
