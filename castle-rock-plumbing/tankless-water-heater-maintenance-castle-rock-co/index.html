    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in Castle Rock, CO

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. We test gas pressure under full fire, not at idle.     Home Tankless Flushing in Castle Rock

    Mountain homes in Colorado frequently rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, manganese, or natural minerals. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. The flow returns. The temperature holds steady and the high-limit shutdowns stop.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Castle Rock

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. They see heavy use, and scale builds faster than at home. We descale each heater in the bank, check the cascade controller and confirm the load is shared evenly across every unit. Quarterly service is common.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Castle Rock, CO

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. You get a written report with the readings, so next year's visit has something to compare against.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Castle Rock

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.

    Emergency Tankless Repair in Castle Rock, CO

    No hot water before work is a rough start. Nine times out of ten, a no-heat call is a dead igniter or a scaled exchanger tripping the high-limit. The truck carries igniters and descaling gear, so we can usually fix it that day.
