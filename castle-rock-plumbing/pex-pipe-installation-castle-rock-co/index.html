    Leak Repair with PEX in Castle Rock, CO

    A single bad section does not always mean a repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two.     Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Castle Rock

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures. In an older house that usually means rust-choked galvanized or polybutylene with tired fittings, and a new faucet cartridge will not touch it. A whole-home repipe will. Every line from the main valve to each angle stop gets replaced, and the system is tested before anything gets closed up.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Castle Rock, CO

    Price on a repipe depends on a short list: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. Ask and we will price all three, PEX-A, PEX-B and copper, side by side.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Castle Rock

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. There is no crimp gauge to check. Because the fittings are wide inside, each joint costs very little flow.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Installation in Castle Rock

    Commercial PEX work in Castle Rock gets scheduled around your hours. Bigger buildings often need one-inch and larger trunks plus commercial manifolds.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Castle Rock, CO

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. All three are code-listed for drinking water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Castle Rock

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. After 40 or 50 years the bore is so choked with scale that a half-inch line delivers a trickle, and the water comes out tinted brown after it sits overnight. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. Pressure comes back at the fixtures and the rust stops.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Castle Rock, CO
