    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Castle Rock

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Clay joints let roots in every few feet, cast iron scales and rusts through along the bottom, and Orangeburg tar paper pipe slowly flattens into an oval. Modern plastic pipe avoids all three problems. HDPE is fused into one continuous piece with no joints for roots to find.

    Sewer Permits and Code in Castle Rock, CO

    Sewer replacement nearly always needs a permit. Our office files the permit and books the inspection, and our crew meets the inspector so you do not have to. The inspector looks at pipe size, slope, bedding and cleanouts. Unpermitted sewer work can show up on a home inspection when you sell.

    How We Price Sewer Replacement in Castle Rock

    Length and depth drive the price. A short, shallow run through grass is a very different job from a long, deep one under concrete. Method matters too. We break out labor, material, equipment, permits and restoration on the estimate. No line items show up later.

    Sewer Codes and Inspections in Castle Rock, CO

    Inspectors check what gets buried. Permits and inspections are typically required for water heaters, repiping, gas lines, and sewer replacements, through the state or the local building department. Work done without a permit can mean digging it back up to show the inspector, which nobody wants.

    How Pipe Bursting Works in Castle Rock

    A winch pulls the bursting head. Because the head is a little wider than the old pipe, it cracks it into the surrounding dirt as the new HDPE follows. You get a pit at each end. The fused pipe has no joints, so roots cannot get in again.

    Fixing a Bellied Sewer by Trench in Castle Rock, CO

    A belly is a low spot that holds water. Trenchless methods ride the old pipe, sag and all, which means a real belly has to be dug out and relaid on grade. Rocky ground slows the dig. We price that in after the locate shows depth. Joints are inspected by us before the city does.

    Commercial Sewer Lateral Work in Castle Rock, CO

    Apartment buildings, offices and restaurants in Castle Rock, CO need lateral work too.     Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Castle Rock, CO

    Concrete is expensive to replace. Bursting lets us run new HDPE under a driveway, patio or mature tree by pulling it through the path of the old pipe from a launch pit to a receiving pit. The HDPE is butt-fused on site into one long piece. Most bursts finish in a day. You end up patching two small pits rather than a whole slab.
