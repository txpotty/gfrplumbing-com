    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Castle Rock

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Basements are common in Colorado, and shifting soil can crack floor drains, sewer lines, and basement walls over time. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Gas Line Repair Costs in Castle Rock, CO

    Simple connector or fitting repairs often cost $150 to $450. Replacing a section of interior pipe usually runs $400 to $1,200, while buried yard line replacement can range from $1,000 to $4,000 or more depending on length and digging conditions. Tough digging raises labor. The written price includes the permit fee and is set before work begins.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Castle Rock

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and add an accessible shutoff valve if the old one is missing or seized. This is a quick fix. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Castle Rock, CO

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Castle Rock, CO

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. Once the coating cracks, the pipe starts to corrode, and wet soil from sprinklers or a low spot in the yard speeds that up a great deal. We call 811 before digging. We lay polyethylene with tracer wire or coated steel where exposed, then backfill and clean up after the test passes.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Castle Rock, CO

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.

    CSST Repair in Castle Rock

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Nails from a remodel, rodents chewing the jacket and nearby lightning strikes are the usual causes, and a damaged run has to be cut out and spliced with the manufacturer's own fittings. We check bonding on every CSST job. The system should be bonded to the electrical grounding system with a proper clamp and conductor, per the manufacturer's instructions and local code.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Castle Rock

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.
