    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Castle Rock, CO

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Switch and tank are a team. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, and a cheap new gauge beats guessing. Small parts, big role.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Castle Rock

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Threads on old tees weep. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Castle Rock

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. They will not rust. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, plus they are easier to move into cramped utility rooms. Same plumbing as steel.

    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in Castle Rock

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. Replacing just the tank can expose other weak spots. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, so you can choose a tank-only swap or a fuller refresh. Your call.

    Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Castle Rock, CO

    A bladder tank holds water inside a rubber balloon surrounded by compressed air. Air pressure forces water to the fixtures. Bladders crack with age, especially if the tank ran with low pre-charge, letting water fill the whole shell and kill the air cushion. Replacement is the fix.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Castle Rock, CO

    Once a year, test the pre-charge. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, then add air with a small compressor if it has dropped. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Castle Rock, CO

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. Worn contacts can fail to open or close. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, and we usually recommend replacing a switch older than the tank it served. Cheap part.
