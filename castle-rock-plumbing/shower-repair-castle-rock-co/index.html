    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat.     Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Castle Rock

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Mineral grit scores the sealing faces and flattens the O-rings until the cartridge can no longer stop the flow. We swap in a new one. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Castle Rock, CO

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain.     Hot Water Pressure Problems in Castle Rock, CO

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. The problem is on the hot side, either sediment in the water heater outlet, a partly closed valve at the heater or a cartridge whose hot port is packed with scale. We test both sides. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Castle Rock, CO

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. We check all of it. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Castle Rock, CO

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. The head comes off first. Low pressure everywhere sends us to the pressure regulator and main valve instead.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Castle Rock

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. We fix the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.
