    Well Pump and Pressure Tank Replacement in Castle Rock, CO

    Rural properties rely on well pumps to deliver water, and pump failure means no water at all. Typical signs include pressure that fluctuates, a pump that cycles rapidly or no water at the tap. We replace submersible and jet pumps and waterlogged pressure tanks with correctly sized equipment. Pressure switch settings are adjusted for steady pressure throughout the home.

    Whole-Home Pipe Replacement in Castle Rock

    Homes with multiple pinhole leaks, galvanized pipe or polybutylene may benefit from a complete repipe. We replace supply lines with PEX or copper, often rerouting through the attic to avoid future slab leaks. Most repipes take two to four days with water restored each evening whenever we can. Drywall openings are patched when the work is finished.

    Sewer Line Replacement in Castle Rock

    Persistent sewer backups, foul odors or lush spots in your yard can signal a failing sewer line. Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines slower, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth. We offer comprehensive sewer line replacement using trenchless technology whenever possible, minimizing disruption to your landscaping. For more severe damage, traditional excavation is performed with careful attention. We make sure new lines are properly graded, connected and compliant with local Castle Rock plumbing codes, preventing future blockages and costly repairs.

    Gas Line Replacement in Castle Rock

    Old or damaged gas lines pose a serious safety risk and require immediate attention. If you suspect a gas leak or your gas lines show signs of corrosion or age, our certified plumbers in Castle Rock can perform safe and code-compliant gas line replacement. We use approved materials like black steel pipe or corrugated stainless steel tubing (CSST) for secure installations, making sure all connections are leak-tested and meet strict safety regulations for your home's protection.

    Water Heater Replacement in Castle Rock

    When your water heater is leaking, not heating or providing too little hot water, it is often more economical to replace it than to repair it, especially if it is over 10 years old. We install standard tank water heaters, sized properly for your home's needs and compliant with current energy efficiency standards. Our team handles the safe removal of your old unit and the precise installation of your new one, connecting gas or electric lines and confirming correct venting and pressure relief valve operation for performance and safety.

    Water Line Replacement in Castle Rock, CO

    A leaking main water line can lead to major water loss, high bills and foundation damage, which is a serious concern for homes on slab foundations. Our water line replacement services address issues like corroded pipes, leaks or low water pressure. We locate the leak, excavate the area if necessary and replace the damaged section or the entire line with durable PEX or copper piping. We make sure of a secure, leak-free connection from the meter to your home, restoring reliable water flow.

    Water Softener Replacement in Castle Rock, CO

    Hard water leads to mineral buildup in pipes and appliances. Mountain homes in Colorado frequently rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, manganese, or natural minerals. If your old water softener is no longer effectively removing minerals, or if it is over 15 years old, a replacement will protect your plumbing and improve water quality. We replace all types of water softeners, including salt-based and salt-free systems, sized for your household's water usage and installed correctly on your main water line to provide truly soft water throughout your Castle Rock home.
