    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Castle Rock

    A standard disposal replacement with the unit typically costs $275 to $650. Premium models like InSinkErator Evolution usually run $500 to $1,000 installed.

    If your disposal is old, frequently leaking, or making grinding noises without grinding food, replacement is usually more cost-effective and provides a longer-lasting solution. Minor issues like a simple jam can often be repaired.

    Most garbage disposal replacements can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. This timeframe includes disconnecting the old unit, installing the new one, and testing it thoroughly. More complex situations, such as significant plumbing modifications or electrical work, might extend the duration a bit.

    Key signs you need a new garbage disposal include persistent leaks from the unit's housing, a humming noise without grinding, frequent clogs despite proper usage, foul odors that won't go away, or if the unit simply won't turn on. These symptoms often indicate internal motor or component failure.

    Most replacements take about an hour. Upgrades with new electrical or drain changes may take longer.

    For light use, a 1/3 HP or 1/2 HP unit is usually sufficient. For regular use and tough food scraps, a 3/4 HP or 1 HP unit is recommended. Our plumbers can help you assess your needs and choose the best fit for your Castle Rock home.

    In most cases, a simple like-for-like garbage disposal replacement in Castle Rock does not require a permit. However, if significant plumbing modifications or electrical work beyond a simple swap are needed, a permit might be required. We'll advise you if this is the case.

    While it's possible for a handy homeowner to replace a garbage disposal, it involves working with both electricity and plumbing, which can be hazardous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, electrical shorts, and voided warranties. For safety and peace of mind, hiring a professional plumber is the better choice.
