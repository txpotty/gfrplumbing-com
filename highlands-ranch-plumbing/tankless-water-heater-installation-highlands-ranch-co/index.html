    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Highlands Ranch, CO

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Around Highlands Ranch, a yearly water heater flush helps clear the sediment that collects at the bottom of the tank. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Highlands Ranch

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units.
