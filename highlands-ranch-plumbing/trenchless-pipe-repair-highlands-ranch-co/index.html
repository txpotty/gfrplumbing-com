    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Prep is half the job.     Pipe Bursting in Highlands Ranch, CO

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Highlands Ranch

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Highlands Ranch yard.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Highlands Ranch, CO

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. We burst or bore. The line is pressure tested before service is turned back on.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Highlands Ranch, CO

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. A winch pulls a bursting head through the line, cracking the old pipe outward into the soil while new HDPE follows right behind it, so the replacement pipe ends up in the same path.
