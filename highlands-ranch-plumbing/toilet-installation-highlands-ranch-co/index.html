    Floor-Mount and Wall-Hung Toilet Installs in Highlands Ranch, CO

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Highlands Ranch

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. It is set that way so a grab bar fits. Before anything is ordered we measure the drain, because shifting it to meet the ADA centerline means cutting into the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Highlands Ranch

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out. After scraping the old wax off, we check the flange for cracks, set new stainless bolts, and lower the new toilet straight down onto a fresh seal so it compresses evenly. The old one leaves with us.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Highlands Ranch, CO

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. For a toilet that means a 3 inch drain with a vent, plus a cold supply stub. We set the drain 12 inches from the framed wall plus the thickness of drywall, so the finished rough-in lands right where the fixture expects it. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in Highlands Ranch

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A grab bar and a taller bowl help a lot. If the drywall is already off, blocking gets added between studs so a grab bar can screw into wood, not hollow-wall anchors. We also center the bowl to ADA spacing.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Highlands Ranch

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. A flange belongs flush with or just above finished flooring. An early set means the tile goes around the flange, leaving it low, and that calls for an extender ring later. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Highlands Ranch, CO

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. We run the water side and mount the unit, and if a new outlet is needed we bring in a licensed electrician rather than splicing anything ourselves. Then we test every button.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Highlands Ranch

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. Water comes from a T-valve at the fill line. Heated seats with a warm-air dryer need a nearby GFCI outlet, and when there is none we bring in an electrician first. Non-electric seats take about thirty minutes.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Highlands Ranch

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.
