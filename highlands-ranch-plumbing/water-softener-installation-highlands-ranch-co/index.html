    Softener Loop Installation in Highlands Ranch

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service simple.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Highlands Ranch

    Accurate testing is key for proper softener settings. We test your water for hardness, iron and chlorine before installation. Then we program regeneration frequency and salt dosage. This helps reduce salt use while keeping water soft. We review settings with you so you understand how the system works.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Highlands Ranch

    A whole-house water softener system is installed directly on your main water line, treating all the water entering your Highlands Ranch home. This ensures every faucet, shower and appliance receives soft water. Our installation process involves carefully integrating the system into your existing plumbing, including necessary bypass valves and pressure regulators, to provide comprehensive protection against hard water throughout your property.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Highlands Ranch, CO
