    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Highlands Ranch

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron changes the math. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Highlands Ranch

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Highlands Ranch, CO

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. GFR Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Highlands Ranch, CO

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners.
