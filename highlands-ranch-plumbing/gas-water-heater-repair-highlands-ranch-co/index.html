    Pilot Light and Burner Issues in Highlands Ranch

    A pilot that lights, holds for ten seconds and then goes out is almost always a thermocouple or a weak magnet in the gas valve. A pilot that will not light at all is more often a clogged orifice or a dead igniter. Burner trouble looks different. The pilot stays lit but the main flame never comes on, which points to the thermostat side of the valve, a tripped thermal cutoff or a flame arrestor so clogged with lint that the burner starves for air. Each part gets tested in that sequence.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Highlands Ranch

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. When lint, dust or pet hair coats that screen, the burner starves, the chamber overheats and a thermal switch shuts the whole heater down, sometimes several times a week. The screen and the chamber both get cleaned. We replace any tripped thermal switch.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Highlands Ranch, CO
