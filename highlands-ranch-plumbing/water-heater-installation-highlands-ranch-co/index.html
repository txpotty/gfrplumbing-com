    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Highlands Ranch, CO

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Gas tankless models usually need a bigger gas line than the old tank had, plus a dedicated vent run through the wall or roof. They cost more to install. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Highlands Ranch, CO

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank.     Electric Water Heater Installation in Highlands Ranch, CO

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. They do not need a vent.
