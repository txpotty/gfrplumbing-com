    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Highlands Ranch

    The cost of drain repairs in Highlands Ranch, CO can vary widely, typically ranging from $150 for a simple clog removal to over $3,000 for complex main sewer line repairs. Factors like the type of drain, severity of the issue and access to the pipes all affect the final price. We provide a detailed estimate after a thorough inspection.

    While chemical drain cleaners are widely available, we usually advise against using them. They often contain harsh chemicals that can damage your pipes, especially older ones, and are typically only a temporary fix. For effective and safe clog removal, professional methods are always the better choice.

    Repeat clogs often come from grease buildup, hard water scale, poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a damaged pipe that catches debris. A camera inspection shows which cause you are dealing with so it can be fixed for good.

    You should call a professional if your DIY efforts with a plunger or basic snake haven't cleared the clog, or if you have recurring clogs, foul odors or multiple slow drains. These issues usually point to a deeper problem that requires specialized tools and expertise to resolve effectively.

    Common signs include slow draining sinks or tubs, gurgling noises from drains or toilets, unpleasant odors coming from your drains, recurring clogs or water backing up into fixtures. If you notice any of these, it is wise to call a professional before the problem worsens.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, effectively removing stubborn clogs, grease buildup and even tree roots. It's typically used when traditional snaking isn't enough, or for preventive maintenance on main sewer lines, especially in older homes with buildup issues.

    Most trap and fixture drain repairs take one to two hours. Replacing drain pipe inside a wall or under a floor can take half a day to a full day. We give you a time estimate when we quote the job.

    Harsh chemical cleaners can soften older ABS glue joints and corrode metal drain parts with repeated use. They also seldom clear grease or scale fully. A mechanical auger or hydro-jet is safer and more effective.
