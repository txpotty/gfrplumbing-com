    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Highlands Ranch

    The best time is a few weeks before your wettest season starts. Homes that see snow may also want a fall check before winter storms and snowmelt.

    If your basement is finished or storms often knock out your power, a backup is a wise choice. We install and maintain battery and water-powered backup systems.

    A standard sump pump maintenance appointment typically takes about 30 to 60 minutes. This time allows for a complete inspection, cleaning and testing of all components. If minor repairs are needed, the visit might run slightly longer.
