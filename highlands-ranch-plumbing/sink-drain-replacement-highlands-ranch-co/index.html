    Pop-Up Drain Assembly Replacement in Highlands Ranch, CO

    A stopper that drops on its own has a worn pivot. The plastic ball on the pivot rod wears flat, the nut that holds it loses its seal, and the rod no longer holds the stopper up, so the sink either will not drain or will not hold water. One new pop-up fixes every part of that. Takes about an hour.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Highlands Ranch, CO

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. A press on the cap opens and closes them, and a slotted body only goes in sinks with an overflow. Cleaning is simpler with no linkage. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.

    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Highlands Ranch, CO

    Mountain homes in Colorado frequently rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, manganese, or other minerals. Scale grows inside pop-up bodies and on stopper rods until the linkage seizes. Forcing a scaled lift rod usually snaps the pivot and leaves the stopper stuck, with a narrowed tailpiece slowing the drain. We replace the whole scaled assembly. Smooth PVC sheds buildup better.

    Emergency Drain Replacement in Highlands Ranch, CO

    Some drain failures cannot wait until Monday.     Utility Sink Drain Replacement in Highlands Ranch

    Laundry tubs see lint and paint water. Lint and grit wear out thin utility tub drains and pack the trap so it never drains right. A 1-1/2 inch trap with a cleanout plug goes in. A lint trap on the washer hose helps.

    Choosing Drain Material in Highlands Ranch, CO

    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It will not rust and costs less, while thick brass is worth it where the trap shows, like under a pedestal. Some houses use black ABS instead. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Highlands Ranch, CO

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. The pivot nut packing leaks, the drain body corrodes around the overflow holes, and the stopper stops sealing, so a sink that will not hold water usually needs a whole new pop-up assembly. Half-fixes do not hold here. The new drain matches your faucet finish.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Highlands Ranch

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. We see the wrong drain often. Swapping it takes under an hour.
