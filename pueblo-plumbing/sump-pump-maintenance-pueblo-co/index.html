    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Pueblo, CO

    The float switch is the most common reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Pueblo

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to early wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Pueblo residence.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Pueblo, CO

    Many Pueblo, CO homeowners rely on battery backup sump pumps for protection during power outages. GFR Plumbing provides thorough maintenance for these critical systems, including testing the battery's charge and health, inspecting the backup pump's operation and checking the charging system. This keeps your home protected even when the primary power source fails, a real concern during severe storms.
