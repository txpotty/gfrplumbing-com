    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Pueblo, CO

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. We handle both every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX top the list of what we find.

    Gas Line Leak Locating in Pueblo

    You cannot locate a gas leak by smell alone. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, then check each joint with a detector and soap solution. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe often marks a leak that has been there a while. We coordinate with the gas utility.

    Leak Detection Price Ranges in Pueblo, CO

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Pueblo

    Heat and wind can pull a lot of water out of a pool, so it is hard to tell evaporation from a leak. A bucket test settles it. We then pressure test the plumbing lines and dye test around lights, skimmers and return fittings, since a leak left alone can wash out the soil behind the shell.

    Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Pueblo

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab. Expansive clay soils are a well-known problem in parts of Colorado, swelling with moisture and heaving slabs, basement floors, and buried pipes. Isolating the line comes first. Then acoustic listening and thermal imaging find the leak without breaking up the floor to look, and you get a marked spot plus repair options before the saw comes out.

    Preventive Leak Inspections in Pueblo, CO

    Checking for leaks once a year catches the small stuff. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves. Older homes with galvanized pipe or original water heater connectors get the most out of this, because those parts often fail together. Short visit, big savings.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Pueblo, CO

    Sprinkler laterals crack and zone valves stick open, and either can lose thousands of gallons before the bill shows it. Check the yard first. Our tech tests the irrigation system separately from the house, runs each zone and traces buried lines, because many water providers say to rule out irrigation first when a summer bill jumps.
