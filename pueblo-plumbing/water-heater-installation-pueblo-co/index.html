    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Pueblo, CO

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Gas tankless models usually need a bigger gas line than the old tank had, plus a dedicated vent run through the wall or roof. Upfront cost is higher. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Pueblo

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. We test draft before leaving. Big families do well with gas.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Pueblo, CO

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. They do not need a vent. You get more spots to put it.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Pueblo, CO

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. First step is to close the cold water valve above the tank and shut off the gas or the breaker, which stops the leak from getting worse. We carry common sizes. Same-day replacement is normal.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Pueblo

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats.
