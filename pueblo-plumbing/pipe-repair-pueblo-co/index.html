    Pipe Repair Jobs We Handle in Pueblo, CO

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Pueblo

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. It can be patched. The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, so we replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric fittings and talk through a full replacement plan when the rest is close behind. The schedule is yours.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Pueblo

    Drain pipe made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. Look for ceiling spots. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet, so we find it, swap the bad length and run water at full volume to prove it. We look at venting as well.

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Pueblo

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Frozen water cracks it. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We repair the split and add insulation. We can add heat cable where needed.

    PEX and Copper Pipe Repair in Pueblo, CO

    PEX and copper fail in different ways. Copper pins. Copper thins from the inside and develops pinholes, while PEX usually fails at fittings or where it was kinked during installation, and our plumbers carry soldering, press and crimp tools so they can repair either one with the right connection. We test before closing up.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Pueblo

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water quality is often behind it. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, which we fix by replacing that length with new pipe. More pinholes often follow.

    Pipe Leak Detection in Pueblo, CO

    A repair starts with finding the leak. Wrong guesses cost money. We use electronic listening devices, thermal cameras and moisture meters, shutting off zones one at a time until the leak has nowhere to hide. Then we make one opening.

    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Pueblo, CO

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Each fails its own way. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, so we replace failed fittings and damaged sections with parts matched to each material. Brittle CPVC needs a gentle hand.

    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Pueblo

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. Fittings let go. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, and we cut out the bad fitting and replace it with a crimp, clamp or expansion connection rated for the pipe. Usually a quick fix.
