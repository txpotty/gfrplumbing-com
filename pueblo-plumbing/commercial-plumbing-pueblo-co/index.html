    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Pueblo

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile.     Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage.
