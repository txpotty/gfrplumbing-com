    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Pueblo

    Undermount sinks rely on clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Then come the strainers and trap.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Pueblo, CO

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and most are single deep bowls with an offset drain. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Pueblo, CO

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. The disposal gets mounted, the dishwasher drain goes through an air gap or high loop, and the dishwasher gets its own hot line and shutoff. One visit costs less than three. Every connection is tested under real use.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Pueblo, CO

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. A support frame goes into the cabinet and the sink is leveled before the countertop goes on. A deep bowl means a lower trap. The drain gets planned for that depth from day one.

    Composite Sink Installation in Pueblo

    Granite and quartz composite sinks are tough on top but can chip at the drain opening if a strainer is overtightened. Hand tight comes first. Most composite brands want silicone rather than putty under the strainer because putty can stain them, so we go by the maker's instructions.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Pueblo, CO

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, plus a bead of silicone along the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, usually overnight, before loading it with water. Skipping the cure time leads to sagging. Just an eighth of an inch of sag leaves a gap that catches water and food along the edge.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Pueblo

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. We pick the layout around your disposal and dishwasher, since a disposal on one side changes where the tee can go and how much fall each side gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.
