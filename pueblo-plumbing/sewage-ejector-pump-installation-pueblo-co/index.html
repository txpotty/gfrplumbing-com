    What Goes Into an Ejector System in Pueblo, CO

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Pueblo, CO

    Businesses in Pueblo, CO with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. A duplex system with an alternating panel is common, so if one pump fails the other keeps things running. Closed restrooms cost business. Each pump can carry the load alone.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Pueblo, CO

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Redundancy matters here.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Pueblo, CO

    On a new build, we set the basin ahead of the slab pour. With no concrete to cut, it costs the least then, and we stub drains, vent and discharge to the plans for a clean rough-in inspection. It keeps the pour on schedule.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Pueblo

    Liberty ejector pumps are a common pick on our jobs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. Their packaged systems come with the basin, lid, pump and check valve matched together, which cuts down on field fitting and gives the inspector a clean, listed assembly to look at. We still add a shutoff.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Pueblo, CO

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A smaller laundry tray pump or a compact ejector can handle it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer dumps at once, with a lint filter so fibers do not wrap the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Pueblo

    Residential ejector systems in Pueblo serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. Each system needs a sealed basin, a pump with a float, a check valve and a vent. Simple parts, exact install. Install mistakes cause more trouble than hardware, so the vent and lid seal get extra attention.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Pueblo

    A high water alarm should be on any ejector basin, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. Audible alarms on their own power or Wi-Fi alarms that text you give warning while the pit is still filling, not after waste reaches the floor. Sudden summer thunderstorms bring flash flooding and hail, and heavy runoff can back up storm drains and sewer lines. Battery backup is an option.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Pueblo

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.
