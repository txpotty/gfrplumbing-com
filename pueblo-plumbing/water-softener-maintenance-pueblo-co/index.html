    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Pueblo, CO

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. The beads turn rusty brown and stop grabbing hardness. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.

    Resin Rebedding in Pueblo

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. A resin cleaner is the first thing we try. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Pueblo, CO

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. The installer never changed them, or the household grew and nobody updated the valve. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used rather than on a fixed timer schedule.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Pueblo

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. A hard stop, then a drop, means a bridge. We break the crust, pull out the mushy salt underneath with a wet-dry vac, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.
