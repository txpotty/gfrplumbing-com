    Older Pueblo homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. They wear out. Clay joints let roots in and cast iron scales and rusts through, and a liner seals both problems with a new jointless pipe that often outlasts the original material by decades. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Pueblo, CO

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE resists corrosion and leaks in Pueblo, CO soil.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Pueblo
