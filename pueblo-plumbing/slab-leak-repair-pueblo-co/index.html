    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Pueblo, CO

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. It is a single small opening. We open the floor over the leak, dig to the pipe, replace the bad section and pressure test it, then compact the trench and patch the concrete so your flooring contractor can finish the surface.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Pueblo, CO

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. That is when a repipe pays off. PEX flexes with the house, does not corrode and can be run through the attic and walls, so a full repipe replaces every supply line and takes the slab out of the picture. It suits many Pueblo homes.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Pueblo, CO

    Direct repair is often the most practical fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. With the pipe exposed, the damaged length is cut out, new pipe is joined in, and the line is pressure tested before the soil goes back in and gets compacted in layers. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Pueblo

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. With the mic catching the hiss through the concrete and the camera showing warm water spreading, we can usually narrow the leak to about a foot. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Pueblo home.

    Emergency Slab Leak Services in Pueblo

    A burst line under the slab can push water through grout lines within hours. Close your main valve. We send someone to cap the broken line, get water back to the rest of the house and repair it, temporary or permanent depending on access. That goes for anywhere in Pueblo.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Pueblo

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. Rerouting ends that. New PEX runs through the attic and down the walls while the old pipe stays buried, with openings placed behind vanities or in closets.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Pueblo, CO

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Slab Leak Detection in Pueblo

If the meter still turns, we close the water heater inlet and pressure test hot and cold separately, then walk the losing line with a ground mic until the hiss is loudest. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.
