    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Colorado Springs

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide a backup. We schedule work to avoid disrupting operations.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump System Installation in Colorado Springs

    Spring snowmelt raises groundwater in Colorado, which can push water into basements and overwork sump pumps. Power outages during severe storms can leave standard sump pumps useless. Our battery backup sump pump system installation in Colorado Springs provides an important layer of protection.     Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Colorado Springs

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Colorado Springs, CO

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself might be cracked, damaged or too small. GFR Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Colorado Springs, CO so your entire system works properly. A properly sized and intact pit is important for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.
