    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Colorado Springs

    The cost of rooter service in Colorado Springs, CO typically ranges from $200 to $600, depending on the severity and location of the clog. More complex issues like main sewer line blockages or those requiring hydro-jetting can be higher, generally between $400 and $1,000. We provide clear, upfront pricing after a thorough diagnosis.

    Drain snaking, also known as augering, uses a long, flexible cable with a cutting head to physically break through or retrieve blockages like hair or tree roots. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the entire interior of the pipe, removing grease, mineral buildup, and sludge, leaving pipes much cleaner than snaking alone. Hydro-jetting is usually more effective for severe or recurring clogs.

    Snaking uses an auger to break through and pull out blockages, while hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire inside of the pipe, removing buildup like grease and mineral deposits. Hydro-jetting provides a much more thorough cleaning, often recommended for recurring clogs or main sewer lines.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots will eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a lasting fix.

    Homes with tree roots or older clay pipe often need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Homes with newer PVC lines may go years without it. A camera inspection can help set the best schedule.

    Most standard rooter services for isolated clogs can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex main sewer line blockages, especially those involving extensive root removal or hydro-jetting, might take longer, sometimes up to a full day. We aim for efficient service with lasting results.

    Common signs include slow drains, gurgling sounds from pipes, water backing up in sinks or toilets, and foul odors coming from drains. If you notice any of these issues, especially multiple fixtures backing up, it's a clear sign you need professional rooter service.

    Tree roots are naturally attracted to water and nutrients, which they find in sewer lines through tiny cracks or loose joints. As roots grow, they can expand and completely block the pipe, a common issue in Colorado Springs neighborhoods with mature trees and older pipes.
