    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Colorado Springs

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak. We pump it down and look at the walls and seams with a light, and dye flushed down a fixture shows us where the water moves. Hairline cracks get a hydraulic cement patch. Split tanks get replaced.

    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Colorado Springs

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. We replace failing lids and add risers that bring access up to the lawn. Future pumping gets cheaper. Each lid we install gets screws or a lock so a child cannot lift it.

    Septic Repair for Businesses in Colorado Springs, CO

    Daycares and small shops on septic put more flow through the tank, more often, than a typical three-bedroom house with four people in it. Kitchen grease and restroom paper towels cause most of the trouble. We add a larger effluent filter and set a pump-out schedule based on use. Repairs get scheduled around your hours.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Colorado Springs, CO

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. Once the outlet baffle is gone, solids head straight for the field. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Colorado Springs

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Press the silence button and cut back on water. We check whether the pump runs and whether a float is hung up on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.

    Emergency Septic Repair in Colorado Springs

    Sewage in the shower pan on a Sunday is an emergency, and we treat it like one. The first thing we ask is whether the tank has a riser we can reach, because uncovering a buried lid can add an hour or more. Stop using water until the tech arrives. Then we find the blockage, clear it and tell you what caused it.

    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Colorado Springs, CO

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days. Our techs rebuild linear aerators and swap rotary ones, and we clear diffusers that have plugged with sludge. Timers and panels get tested too. We also keep the service log your permit may require.

    Septic Pipe Repair in Colorado Springs, CO

    Old homes may still have clay tile or Orangeburg pipe running to the tank, and both fail badly with age.
