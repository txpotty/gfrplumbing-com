    Sewer Pipe Materials We Install in Colorado Springs, CO

    PVC covers most of our trenched work. SDR 35 is the common sewer grade for buried laterals, Schedule 40 shows up where code or the inspector wants a heavier wall, and HDPE is our pick for bursting. Expansive clay soils are a well-known problem in parts of Colorado, swelling with moisture and heaving slabs, basement floors, and buried pipes. Cast iron still gets used indoors on some jobs. We will go over which one fits your depth, soil and budget.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Colorado Springs, CO

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. It was installed in a lot of homes in the decades after the war, and over time it softens, blisters and squashes into an oval that catches everything going by. Clay is tougher but leaks roots. When the camera shows trouble in three or more places, a full replacement is usually cheaper than chasing spot repairs.

    How We Price Sewer Replacement in Colorado Springs

    Length and depth drive the price. A forty-foot lateral four feet down in an open lawn costs a lot less than ninety feet under a driveway at eight feet, and we price both honestly. Method matters too. We break out labor, material, equipment, permits and restoration on the estimate. The total is what you pay.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Colorado Springs

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. After cutting roots and jetting the line, we pull in a resin-filled liner and inflate it until it cures against the old pipe wall. It needs a pipe that still holds its shape. It is a strong choice under slabs and finished rooms. You will get a straight answer on whether yours is a candidate.

    Replacing a Collapsed Sewer by Trench in Colorado Springs

    Collapsed pipe cannot be burst or lined. A camera that dead-ends at broken clay or squashed fiber pipe means we have to dig, clean out the debris and replace that run. Being in the trench shows us what the surrounding soil is doing. Often there is a void or a root mat right at the failure point.

    Emergency Sewer Replacement in Colorado Springs, CO

    Sewage coming up a floor drain is an emergency. We answer the call, get a camera down the line, and tell you within the first visit whether the pipe has collapsed or just needs to be cleared for now. A collapse means we put in the permit and utility locates right away. GFR Plumbing can often run a temporary bypass. With a bypass in place, the house can use a toilet and a sink until the new pipe goes in.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Colorado Springs

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. We open a trench along the old line, pull the broken pipe and lay new PVC on compacted bedding at a steady fall of an eighth to a quarter inch per foot. Sod gets cut and rolled aside first when the lawn allows it. The inspector sees open pipe. We compact the backfill in layers, which keeps a dip from forming along the trench.
