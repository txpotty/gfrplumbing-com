    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Colorado Springs, CO

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Colorado Springs

    Mountain homes in Colorado frequently rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, manganese, or other minerals. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Signs include crusted aerators, clogged showerheads, slow toilet fill and heater sediment, and we can test hardness during the visit. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Commercial Property Plumbing Checks in Colorado Springs

    Businesses in Colorado Springs use our inspections to plan maintenance instead of reacting to breakdowns. Backflow devices, heaters, flush valves, floor drains, trap primers and the sewer all get checked. Downtime costs money. With a ranked report, repairs get scheduled after close instead of forcing a shutdown.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Colorado Springs, CO

    Long heating seasons keep boilers and water heaters busy, so regular maintenance pays off in fewer midwinter breakdowns. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. Outdoor faucets, exposed pipe, irrigation backflow devices and the heater drain and relief valve all get checked. We look at pipe insulation. Fixing a cracked hose bibb or adding insulation is a small job compared to a burst pipe in the wall behind it.

    Plumbing Inspection Costs in Colorado Springs, CO

    A basic plumbing inspection usually costs $150 to $300, and adding a sewer camera usually brings it to $250 to $500. Larger homes are priced by scope.     Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Colorado Springs, CO

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Some need replacing soon. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Colorado Springs, CO

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Colorado Springs

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. We check supply pipe, record the sewer, date the water heater and test every fixture. We send the report to your agent too. Many buyers use our findings to ask for repairs or credits, and the sewer video alone has saved buyers from five-figure surprises.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Colorado Springs, CO

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. The camera goes in at a cleanout and travels to the main or the tank, recording roots, cracks, offsets and bellies along the way. A locator marks the depth. Basements are common in Colorado, and shifting soil can crack floor drains, sewer lines, and basement walls over time.
