    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Colorado Springs

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Pipe Bursting in Colorado Springs, CO

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is best for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It typically requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Colorado Springs

    The cost of trenchless sewer line repair in Colorado Springs, CO can vary a lot, typically ranging from $4,000 to $15,000 or more, depending on the length of the pipe, the method used and the complexity of the job. Factors like pipe diameter and accessibility also play a role, so a professional assessment is key for an accurate estimate.

    The main advantages include minimal disruption to your property, faster completion times and often a more durable repair thanks to new materials like HDPE pipe or epoxy liners. You save on landscaping and restoration costs, which can be significant in Colorado Springs.

    Lines that are cracked, root-infested or corroded but still hold their shape are usually good candidates for lining. Collapsed or badly offset lines may need pipe bursting or a spot excavation.

    You should consider trenchless repair if you have recurring clogs, slow drains, foul odors, lush patches in your yard or visible sewage backups. It is especially helpful if your sewer line runs under valuable landscaping, driveways or structures you wish to keep.

    For pipe bursting, we usually use high-density polyethylene pipe, known for its strength, flexibility and resistance to corrosion and roots. For CIPP relining, a felt liner saturated with epoxy resin, such as products from Perma-Liner or NuFlow, is used to create a new, seamless pipe within the existing pipe.

    We partner with established brands known for their quality and durability, such as Perma-Liner and MaxLiner. These manufacturers provide materials and systems that deliver a strong and long-lasting repair for your sewer lines.

    Trenchless repair is highly versatile and effective for issues like cracks, fractures, root intrusion and minor collapses. If the pipe has completely disintegrated, has severe offsets or a major belly, traditional excavation might be a more suitable option. A video inspection will determine the best approach.

    Most trenchless sewer line repairs can be completed in one to three days, much faster than traditional excavation methods, which can take a week or more. The exact timeline depends on the length of the pipe, the severity of the damage and the specific trenchless technique used.
