    Softener Upkeep in Colorado Springs, CO

    Softeners fail quietly. A softener that quit regenerating still sends water, just hard water, and you only notice when scale appears. We check the bypass and drain line. Then we test the treated water.

    Drain Cleaning as Maintenance in Colorado Springs

    Slow drains rarely fix themselves. A yearly camera look and a cleaning on known problem lines, especially near big trees, keeps grease and roots from building to the point where the main backs up during a holiday. Jetting helps on grease. Rooted lines get regular cutting.

    Repipe Assessments in Colorado Springs, CO

    Polybutylene and old galvanized pipe leak more. During maintenance we identify your supply pipe material, look for pinhole leaks, rust stains and pressure drops, and tell you honestly whether repiping is something to plan for now or years from now. We quote PEX and copper. Repiping can be done in stages.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspection in Colorado Springs, CO

    Room by room, fixture by fixture. Under-sink checks, toilet valve tests, fixture runs, washer hose inspection and a pressure reading cover the house. Small fixes happen on the spot. Bigger items go on the report.

    Sump Pump Testing in Colorado Springs, CO

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Sudden summer thunderstorms bring flash flooding and hail, and heavy runoff can overwhelm storm drains and sewer lines. We fill the pit to test the float, check the discharge line and confirm the check valve holds. Battery backups get load tested.

    Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Colorado Springs

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life. We drain sediment, check the anode rod, inspect the burner or elements and confirm the relief valve opens and reseats, whether the unit is a standard tank or a tankless model on the wall. Most failures give warning. We spot the signs early.

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in Colorado Springs, CO

    A camera shows what the sewer looks like inside. Roots, sags, cracks and grease show up on camera, especially in old clay and cast iron. You get a copy of the video. If cleaning or repair makes sense, we explain the choices.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Colorado Springs

    Tankless units need descaling. Hard water scales the heat exchanger until the unit runs lukewarm or shuts off mid-shower. We pump descaler through the service valves. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.
