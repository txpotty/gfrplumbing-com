    Electric Tank Repair in Colorado Springs, CO

    Four parts can fail on an electric tank, two elements and two thermostats. Scale from hard water buries the lower element and burns it out. After testing resistance and voltage, we replace what failed, reset the high limit and watch it heat up. A breaker that keeps tripping gets traced first.

    Pilot Light Repair in Colorado Springs

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Less often it is a dirty flame arrestor screen choking off combustion air. The thermocouple gets a millivolt test. A good reading points us to the gas valve instead.

    Same-Day Emergency Heater Repair in Colorado Springs

    A tank that is actively leaking or a relief valve that keeps discharging needs prompt attention. We answer emergency calls day and night.     Gas Water Heater Repair in Colorado Springs, CO

    Gas units add a burner, a pilot or igniter and a gas control valve to the list of parts that can fail. The thermocouple and the control valve cause most no-heat calls. After any repair, every gas connection gets a leak test. Draft at the vent gets checked too.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Colorado Springs, CO

    The anode rod sacrifices itself so the steel tank does not rust. Hard water can eat through one in a few years. We pull and inspect the rod, then install a magnesium, aluminum or powered anode depending on your water and any odor problems. Rotten-egg odor often clears up.
