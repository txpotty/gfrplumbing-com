    Trenchless vs Traditional Repair Cost in Colorado Springs, CO

    Per foot of pipe, trenchless can cost more than open trenching. Look at the full cost. After adding drive, patio, landscaping and sprinkler repairs to the trench bid, trenchless usually wins on price and speed. We compare both with real numbers for your property.

    Pipe Bursting in Colorado Springs, CO

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Colorado Springs

    Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines. Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines slower, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth. Small cracks get a sectional liner. Longer damage gets a full-length liner, and a fully collapsed line gets burst and replaced, all without trenching across the yard or cutting the driveway at your home.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Colorado Springs, CO

    A broken water service line wastes water and soaks the yard. Pressure falls too. After locating the leak, we burst new HDPE through the old path or bore a new one, keeping the lawn and walk intact. We pressure test before turning water on.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Colorado Springs

    Older Colorado Springs homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. They wear out. Clay joints let roots in and cast iron scales and rusts through, and a liner seals both problems with a new jointless pipe that often outlasts the original material by decades. Only one or two access points are needed.
