    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Colorado Springs, CO

    Food buildup and grease on the chamber walls and under the splash guard cause most disposal smells. Around Colorado Springs, a yearly water heater flush helps clear the scale that collects at the bottom of the tank. Scale gives that food something to stick to.     Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Colorado Springs

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    Leaking Disposal Fix in Colorado Springs, CO

    A drip at the top usually means the sink flange putty has dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. That fixes most top leaks. Side leaks are usually the discharge gasket or the dishwasher hose clamp.

    Preventive Care and Longevity in Colorado Springs, CO

    Run cold water before, during and after grinding, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Grease poured in with hot water coats the chamber and the pipe, while cold water keeps it solid so the impellers can break it up. Fibrous scraps jam it most. Throw out celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Colorado Springs, CO

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. Water from the bottom usually means a failed internal seal, which makes replacement the smarter move. That seal cannot be replaced.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Colorado Springs

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Colorado Springs, CO

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Three of the four are fixable. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. We stock the common kits. That keeps most repairs to one visit.
