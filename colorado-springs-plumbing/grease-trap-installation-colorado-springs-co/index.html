    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Colorado Springs

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, typically under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. GFR Plumbing installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Colorado Springs for the best efficiency.

    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Colorado Springs, CO

    Larger restaurants and high-volume kitchens often need an outdoor gravity interceptor. These concrete or polyethylene tanks usually range from several hundred to over 1,500 gallons. We excavate, set the tank on a stable base, connect inlet and outlet piping and install manhole risers. Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines harder, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth. We plan excavation equipment around the ground conditions we find on site. A properly installed interceptor can go much longer between pump-outs than an indoor trap.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Colorado Springs, CO

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the involved excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, ensuring compliance with all local Colorado ordinances for waste management.
