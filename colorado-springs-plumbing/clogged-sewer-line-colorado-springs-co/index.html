    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Colorado Springs

    Roots from thirsty trees and shrubs seek out moisture and push into sewer pipe joints. Clay and older cast iron pipe are especially prone to root intrusion because of their many joints. We cut roots with a sewer machine fitted with root saws and then camera the line to see how much damage remains. For ongoing root problems, we can recommend a root treatment schedule or lining.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Colorado Springs

    Some older homes have no accessible cleanout, which forces plumbers to pull a toilet or go through a roof vent to clear the main line. We install a two-way cleanout near the foundation so future clearing is faster and cheaper. The cleanout also gives the city or a future plumber easy access for inspections. This is a common upgrade after a serious sewer clog.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Colorado Springs

    Hydro jetting uses water at thousands of psi through a specialized nozzle to cut roots, grease and scale. It cleans the full diameter of the pipe rather than just boring a hole through the clog. We check the pipe condition with a camera first so jetting is only used on lines that can handle it. Many homeowners find that jetting buys them years before another clog.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Colorado Springs

    Before any work begins, a sewer camera inspection is crucial for accurately diagnosing the cause and location of a clog. Our high-resolution cameras provide a clear view inside your sewer lines, allowing us to identify issues like cracks, breaks, root intrusions, or excessive grease. This non-invasive method saves time and money by avoiding unnecessary digging, ensuring a precise repair plan for your Colorado Springs property. It is essential for older homes with cast iron or clay pipes.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Colorado Springs

    GFR Plumbing uses powerful hydro-jetting technology to clear even the most stubborn sewer line blockages. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away grease, scale, mineral buildup, and tree roots, restoring your pipes to their original diameter. It's a very effective, non-invasive way to clean your sewer lines thoroughly and prevent future clogs. Unlike snaking, hydro-jetting cleans the whole pipe wall.

    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Colorado Springs, CO

    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is key for proper diagnosis and repair in Colorado Springs, CO. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are typically isolated to a single fixture or area. Our technicians are skilled at finding the exact location of the blockage, whether it is in the main line or a specific branch, for a targeted and efficient repair.
