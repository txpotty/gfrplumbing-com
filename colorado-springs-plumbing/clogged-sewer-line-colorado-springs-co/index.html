    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Colorado Springs, CO

    Basements are common in Colorado, and shifting soil can crack floor drains, sewer lines, and foundation walls over time. A section of sewer pipe that sinks forms a belly. Waste settles in the low spot. Over time that standing water collects paper and grease, so the clog keeps returning in the same place. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. Then we quote a spot repair.

    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Colorado Springs, CO

    One slow drain is a branch clog. More than one usually means the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, so the pattern of slow drains tells us where to go in. The right entry point saves time.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Colorado Springs

    A sewer camera is a waterproof head on a push rod with a locator transmitter built in. We see what the pipe looks like. Cracks, offsets, sags, roots and heavy scale all show up on the screen, while the locator marks the spot and depth from above. That keeps digging to a minimum. You get a copy of the footage.

    Emergency Sewer Backup Service in Colorado Springs, CO

    Sewage coming up through a shower or floor drain needs attention right away. Stop using water. Our emergency crews stop the backup, clear the line and contain the mess, and we can refer you to a restoration company if sewage reached carpet or drywall. We document it for your claim.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Colorado Springs, CO

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. They stay intact for weeks. Mixed with grease that hardened in the main, they form a dense plug that a cable can poke a hole through but not fully remove from the pipe. Jetting scours the walls and flushes the debris downstream. Toss wipes in the trash.
