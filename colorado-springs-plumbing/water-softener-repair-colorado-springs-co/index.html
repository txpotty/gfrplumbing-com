    Resin Bed Replacement & Regeneration Issues in Colorado Springs

    Over time, the resin beads inside your water softener can become fouled, damaged, or depleted, reducing their ability to remove hardness minerals. If your system is regenerating but still delivering hard water, the resin bed may be the culprit. We can test the resin, flush the system to remove iron or sediment buildup, or perform a complete resin replacement. This process restores your softener's core function, ensuring effective hard water treatment for years to come.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Colorado Springs, CO

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. We also suggest salt types that bridge less in warm or humid spaces.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Colorado Springs

    If hard water symptoms return, the softener may not be regenerating. Causes include a failed timer motor, a bad circuit board, a stuck meter turbine or incorrect settings after a power outage. We test each component, replace the failed part and program the valve for your household size and hardness. You get soft water again by the next cycle.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Colorado Springs, CO

    Leaks in a water softener can range from minor drips around fittings to significant puddles from a cracked tank or faulty valve. These leaks can cause water damage and waste water. Our plumbers quickly pinpoint the source of leaks, whether from seals, O-rings, hoses, or internal components, and perform durable repairs. We use appropriate sealing compounds and replacement parts for a lasting fix in Colorado Springs, CO.

    Control Valve Repair in Colorado Springs

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, directing the flow of water and initiating regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water or waste salt. GFR Plumbing technicians are experts at diagnosing and repairing common control valve issues, whether mechanical or electronic, ensuring precise timing and efficient operation. We stock common replacement parts to speed up your repair in Colorado Springs.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Colorado Springs

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a common issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. GFR Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, properly removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in Colorado Springs.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Colorado Springs

    A common issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to poor regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and making sure the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.
