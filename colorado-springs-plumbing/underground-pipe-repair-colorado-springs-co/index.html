    Pipe Bursting Services in Colorado Springs, CO

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. The new line takes its place. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Underground Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Colorado Springs, CO

    A single point repair on a shallow water line is the cheapest option. Pull-through costs more. It avoids open trenching and the long-term risk of more leaks on old pipe, and open trenching becomes necessary when the old path is unusable. We price all three.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Colorado Springs

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It fails without warning. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, and buried runs tend to split at fittings or where the pipe was bent tight during installation. Replacement is the only lasting fix. We use PEX or copper.
