    Picking a Tankless for Your Colorado Springs, CO Home

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Colorado Springs

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. They run on a single 20 or 30 amp circuit and work well for a far bathroom or an office sink. Swaps take an hour or two.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Colorado Springs, CO

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Across 20 years the gap narrows, because one tankless may outlast two tanks, but your usage decides the payback. You see both quotes.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Colorado Springs

    Sizing a tankless starts with how many fixtures run at once and how cold the incoming water is, not the square footage of the house. Two showers and a dishwasher at the same time can need 7 or 8 gallons per minute. The unit is chosen from that figure. Any gas, water or vent changes the new unit needs get done to current code.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Colorado Springs, CO

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. Both readings get taken.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Colorado Springs, CO

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Hard freezes are a normal part of winter in Colorado, and pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, and garages are particularly vulnerable. We check the freeze protection settings and the pipe cover kit on every outdoor swap. Often the simplest job we do.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Colorado Springs

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger. Pipe length and meter capacity get checked against the sizing tables, and we confirm pressure at full fire. An undersized line causes ignition faults.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Colorado Springs, CO

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. We replace failed units without shutting down the rest. Hand sinks and dish stations have to hit the temperatures health code requires.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Colorado Springs, CO

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. Expect a bigger gas line, a new vent run and a wall-mounted spot for the heater. The old flue gets capped.
