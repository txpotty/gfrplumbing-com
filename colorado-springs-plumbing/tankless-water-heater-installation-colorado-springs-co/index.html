    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Colorado Springs

    Gas tankless units in Colorado Springs are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature.     Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Colorado Springs

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. It saves water too.
