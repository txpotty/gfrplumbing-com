    The cost for residential plumbing service in Colorado Springs varies a great deal depending on the specific issue.     Watch for persistent drips, slow drains, strange noises from pipes or water heaters, sudden drops in water pressure, discolored water or unexplained wet spots. Any of these signs indicate a potential problem that warrants a professional residential plumbing service check to prevent further damage.

    Hard water contains high levels of dissolved minerals, mainly calcium and magnesium. Soft water has these minerals removed. Hard water can cause mineral buildup in pipes, reduced soap lather and appliance damage, while soft water is easier on plumbing and skin.

    We recommend having your water heater inspected annually, especially where hard water can speed up sediment buildup. Regular inspections can extend the lifespan of your unit, improve efficiency and help identify potential issues like anode rod corrosion or pressure relief valve problems before they become major repairs.
