    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Colorado Springs

    Direct vent water heaters draw combustion air from outside and vent exhaust through a sealed system. They are a good choice for tight utility rooms or interior closets. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall with proper clearances. Many direct vent models do not need electricity. They offer safe, efficient operation.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Colorado Springs, CO

    Power vent water heaters use a fan to push exhaust through PVC or other approved vent pipe. This allows installation in places without a chimney or vertical flue. Power vent units need an electrical outlet nearby. We install the vent run to manufacturer specs and check for proper termination. These units offer versatile placement options.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Colorado Springs, CO

    For endless hot water on demand, gas tankless water heaters are an appealing option for many homeowners. Installing these units requires specific venting and gas line sizing to handle the higher BTU input. GFR Plumbing provides professional tankless water heater installation, improving efficiency and making sure your unit delivers hot water only when you need it. Many larger systems in Colorado draw on mountain snowmelt that runs fairly soft, while plains communities and groundwater sources often deliver more mineral-heavy water. We use your local water quality to recommend the right pre-filtration.

    Gas Water Heater Installation Cost in Colorado Springs, CO

    Standard atmospheric vent gas water heaters typically cost less to install than power vent or tankless units. Venting changes, gas line upgrades and code corrections can add cost. We provide a written quote that includes the unit, labor, permits and haul-away. We also compare operating costs between gas and electric models. That helps you choose the best long-term value.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Colorado Springs

    Atmospheric vent water heaters rely on natural draft to move exhaust through a metal flue. They are common in older homes and are often the most budget-friendly gas option. We inspect the existing vent for damage, proper slope and clearance. If the vent is undersized or corroded, we replace it. Proper draft is critical for safety.

    Propane Water Heater Installation in Colorado Springs

    Homes in rural areas often use propane instead of natural gas. Propane water heaters require specific burner orifices and gas pressure settings. We install propane models or convert units with manufacturer kits where allowed. We coordinate with your propane supplier when needed. Every connection is leak tested before use.
