    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Colorado Springs, CO

    Pressure vacuum breakers are the low-cost answer for lawn irrigation in Colorado Springs, CO. They let air in when supply pressure drops, which breaks the siphon, but they only protect against back-siphonage, so they must sit above every downstream outlet and cannot be used where back pressure is possible. Clearance and drainage get checked as well.

    Commercial Backflow Prevention in Colorado Springs, CO

    Restaurants and multi-family buildings fall under commercial cross-connection rules. Big mains get heavy-duty RPZ assemblies, up to 4 inches and beyond, sized and supported to meet Colorado regulations for businesses, and we handle the permits and the inspection schedule with the water provider in Colorado Springs, CO. Big valves need solid hangers.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Colorado Springs

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. If the zone between the checks loses its pressure margin, the relief valve opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Colorado Springs. We install Watts and Febco units.

    Emergency Backflow Preventer Replacement in Colorado Springs

    A failed backflow device can mean a compliance letter or a shutoff notice. We pull the bad unit and set a new one, usually the same day in Colorado Springs, with unions on both sides so the next repair is a quick swap instead of a cut-and-solder job on a cold morning. We certify it on the spot.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Colorado Springs, CO

    Sprinkler heads sit in fertilizer and standing water, and a pressure drop on the main can siphon that water back into the house. A pressure vacuum breaker stops back-siphonage on most residential systems, and we mount it at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head so it can do its job in Colorado Springs, CO. Mounting height is the common mistake.

    Residential Backflow Devices in Colorado Springs

    Homes with irrigation or a fire sprinkler line often need a device to keep that water out of the municipal supply. The hazard decides which assembly we install, then mount it where the tester can reach it. Hard freezes are a normal part of winter in Colorado, and pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, and garages are particularly vulnerable. Outdoor units get weather-rated parts.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Colorado Springs, CO

    Pool auto-fill lines and hose connections give chlorinated water a straight path back to the supply. We add the right device on the fill line, check that every hose bibb has a vacuum breaker, and can fold the work into a pool build or remodel so it is done before the deck goes in. Hose bibb breakers are cheap.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Colorado Springs

    A double check valve assembly, or DCVA, uses two spring-loaded checks in series with four test cocks. It is the usual pick for low-hazard lines such as many fire sprinklers, and because it does not discharge water, it can go in a vault where local rules allow it, sized so it does not rob your system of pressure in Colorado Springs. We leave room for the tester.
