    Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in Lakewood, CO

    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve. A weak flush may mean the tank isn't filling correctly, while a loose handle might not be engaging the flapper. Our plumbers are adept at diagnosing these internal tank problems, replacing worn or broken components like flush valves, fill valves, and toilet handles to restore full and effective flushing power.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Lakewood, CO

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom.     Running Toilet Fixes in Lakewood

    A toilet that constantly runs or refills itself intermittently is not only annoying but also a major water waste.     Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Lakewood, CO

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem could be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully.
