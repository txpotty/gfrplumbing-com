    What We Replace on Well Systems in Lakewood, CO

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Lakewood

    Sand kills submersibles early. Grit in a well chews up impellers fast, so we look for it on the old pump and may set the new one higher or add a sand separator. Pump setting depth matters.

    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Lakewood

    Pump and tank work as a pair. An undersized or waterlogged tank forces dozens of extra starts a day, and each start puts heat into the motor windings. We check it on every pump job.

    Well Pump Sizing in Lakewood

    Sizing starts with the well log. The log, or a sounder down the casing, gives us static water level and well depth, and a draw-down test tells us roughly how many gallons per minute the well can actually give. Pumps bigger than the well can supply run dry.

    Well Pump Brands We Install in Lakewood, CO

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. Each brand has strengths, and we choose based on your well. Goulds and Franklin parts are on every supply shelf, which helps when a control box fails late on a Saturday afternoon.

    Well Pump Efficiency Upgrades in Lakewood, CO

    Older 1 HP pumps running against a 60 psi switch use more power than most people expect, especially on a well with a long horizontal run to the house. A correctly sized pump, or a variable-speed drive, can cut the run time. Not always worth it. We run the numbers with you.

    No-Water Calls in Lakewood

    Pump trouble rarely gives notice. One morning the faucet coughs air and then nothing, and by then the motor has often been struggling for weeks. Before we pull the pump, we test the switch and control box.

    Solar Well Pump Systems in Lakewood, CO

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A DC pump and a controller run off a few panels during daylight. The storage tank does the job a battery would. Panel count comes from how many gallons you need a day.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Lakewood

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. Short cycling, where the pump starts every few seconds of water use, usually means the tank bladder has failed and the pump is paying for it. We check the air charge with a gauge. Precharge should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure.
