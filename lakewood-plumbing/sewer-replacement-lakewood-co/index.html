    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Lakewood, CO

    When appropriate, trenchless sewer replacement offers a less invasive alternative to traditional digging.     Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Lakewood

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup catches debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and ends corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Lakewood, CO

    A cleanout provides easy access for future cleaning and inspection. Many older homes lack an exterior cleanout. We install one during sewer replacement. This saves time and money on future maintenance. It also helps in emergencies.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Lakewood

    For severely damaged or collapsed sewer lines, traditional trenching is often the most reliable solution. This involves excavating a trench along the sewer line's path to allow for direct access and replacement of the entire pipe. While more invasive, it provides a complete overhaul and is often required for older homes with clay or cast iron pipes that have reached the end of their lifespan.
