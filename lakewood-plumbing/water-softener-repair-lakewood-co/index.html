    Reliable Water Softener Repair Services in Lakewood, CO

    Resin Bed Replacement in Lakewood

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a common issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. GFR Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, properly removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in Lakewood.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Lakewood

    A softener using far too much salt or water usually has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use. We retest hardness, reprogram the valve or upgrade the head to a metered control. Proper settings can noticeably cut salt and water use.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Lakewood, CO

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and initiating regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water, regenerate too often, or leak. We diagnose issues like stuck pistons, worn out seals, motor failures, or programming errors. Our plumbers carry a range of replacement parts for popular brands like Fleck, Clack, and Autotrol, ensuring a quick and effective repair to restore proper operation.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Lakewood, CO

    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. Our team can effectively break up and remove salt bridges, clean the brine tank, and advise on best practices to prevent future occurrences. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, common in older systems.

    Commercial Water Softener Repair in Lakewood, CO

    Businesses in Lakewood, CO rely on soft water for various operations, from laundromats and restaurants to manufacturing facilities. GFR Plumbing provides expert repair services for commercial-grade water softeners, understanding the larger scale and specialized components involved.     Softener Not Regenerating in Lakewood

    If hard water symptoms return, the softener may not be regenerating. Causes include a failed timer motor, a bad circuit board, a stuck meter turbine or incorrect settings after a power outage. We test each component, replace the failed part and program the valve for your household size and hardness. You get soft water again by the next cycle.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Lakewood, CO

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. We also recommend salt types that bridge less in warm or humid spaces.
