    Septic Pump and Alarm Repair in Lakewood

    Pumped systems move effluent uphill or to a far field, and the pump sits in its own chamber with a float tree and an alarm. An alarm going off means the water rose past the high-level float.     Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Lakewood, CO

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.
