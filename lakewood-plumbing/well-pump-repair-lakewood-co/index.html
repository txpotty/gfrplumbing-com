    Pressure Switch Replacement in Lakewood

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Lakewood, CO

    Many well pump issues stem from electrical problems, including tripped circuit breakers, faulty wiring, or a malfunctioning control box. These can prevent the pump from starting or cause it to run erratically. Our licensed plumbers are also skilled in electrical diagnostics, safely testing circuits, relays, and capacitors to identify and repair electrical faults. We ensure all wiring and connections meet Colorado electrical codes, providing a safe and reliable power supply to your well pump.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Lakewood, CO

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. GFR Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Lakewood operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.

    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Lakewood

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a frustrating problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. Our team conducts careful diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Lakewood home, ensuring a targeted and effective repair solution.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Lakewood, CO

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Sudden summer thunderstorms bring flash flooding and hail, and heavy runoff can overwhelm storm drains and sewer lines. Lightning and power surges often damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.
