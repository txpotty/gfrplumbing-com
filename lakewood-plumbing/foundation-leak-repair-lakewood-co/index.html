    Frequently Asked Questions About Foundation Leak Repair in Lakewood

    Leak detection usually costs $150 to $450. Repairs vary more. Direct repairs through concrete usually range from $1,500 to $4,000, and reroutes often run $1,800 to $5,000 depending on the length of the new line and how hard the walls are to get into. Full repipes get their own quote.

    Policies often cover sudden leaks. The pipe itself usually isn't covered. What insurers tend to cover is the cost of getting to the leak and fixing damage it caused, like flooring and drywall, so call your agent and ask how your own policy handles access and resulting damage.

    Spot repairs often skip the permit, while reroutes and repipes may need one depending on the city. We file for permits whenever required.

    A spot repair is often done in a few hours. Tunneling adds time. For a reroute or a tunnel, plan on one to three days, depending on line length and what is in the way. Your Lakewood tech will give you a closer estimate.

    Parts and labor are both covered on our foundation leak repairs. You see the terms first. A new PEX reroute typically gets longer coverage than a patch on aging copper, since the pipe around it is new. Details go on your Lakewood estimate.

    A hairline crack in a basement wall can sometimes be sealed with a store-bought kit. Pipe leaks under the slab are not DIY. A repair that is done wrong can soak more soil, cost more to redo and fail local code, so anything involving a pipe under the floor or a structural crack should go to a licensed plumber or engineer.

    We repair copper, PEX and PVC lines in Lakewood homes. With PEX, we match the existing fitting system, such as Uponor expansion or Viega crimp, so the new joint works with what is already there. Every part is code-approved.
