    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Lakewood

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Lakewood, CO

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank absorbs excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is essential to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and maintaining system integrity.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Lakewood, CO

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, sparing you a full tank replacement.

    Burner and Pilot Light Maintenance in Lakewood, CO

    For gas hot water tanks, proper burner and pilot light operation is essential for efficient heating. Our service includes cleaning the burner assembly to remove dust and debris that can interfere with combustion. We also check and adjust the pilot light, making sure it ignites reliably. This attention to detail prevents common issues like intermittent hot water or complete heating failure, which can be stressful for homeowners in Lakewood, CO.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Lakewood

    Sediment buildup is a common issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, giving you clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is crucial for extending the life of your unit.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Lakewood

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to confirm they are functioning correctly and safely.
