    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Lakewood

    Calcium carbonate drops out of hard water every time the tank heats, and over a year it can harden into chunks the size of a thumbnail. That is what makes the popping noise. For stubborn scale we use a potable-rated descaler, then flush it clean. A clean bottom puts less heat stress on the seam.

    T&P Valve Replacement in Lakewood

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. The new valve must match the tank's BTU or kilowatt rating. We drain the tank below the valve opening, thread in the new one with pipe dope rated for potable water, and reconnect a discharge pipe in copper, CPVC or another approved material. Never plug or cap that pipe.

    Electric Element Check in Lakewood

    Electric tanks have two elements, usually 4,500 watts at 240 volts, and the lower one does most of the work and takes most of the scale. We test each one with a meter. A failed lower element shows up as hot water that runs out halfway through a shower. We replace it with the matching wattage and voltage.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Lakewood, CO

    A yearly flush costs a small fraction of a new installed tank, which often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more depending on size and venting. We tell you which one makes sense. A tank over 10 to 12 years old with rust at the base is a replacement. Younger tanks with a sound liner are worth maintaining.

    Yearly Maintenance Plans in Lakewood, CO

    A maintenance plan puts your tank on the calendar so nobody has to remember. We call you when it is due. Plans cover one or two visits a year at a fixed rate, with priority scheduling if the heater fails between visits and a discount on parts like anodes and drain valves. Homes on well water or very hard water usually do better with the twice-a-year option.

    Expansion Tank Service in Lakewood, CO

    A thermal expansion tank is the small steel tank on the cold line above the heater, and it gives heated water somewhere to go when a check valve or pressure reducer closes the system. We test the air side with a tire gauge. The precharge needs to match static pressure in the house. A waterlogged expansion tank sounds solid when you knock on it, and it lets pressure spike every time the burner fires.

    Burner and Pilot Service in Lakewood, CO

    On a gas tank, a dirty burner throws a lazy yellow flame and leaves soot on the flue baffle. The burner comes out and gets brushed and vacuumed. We also check the thermocouple or flame sensor, since a weak one lets the pilot go out every few days and leaves you resetting it on your knees. The goal is a steady blue flame.

    Anode Upgrades for Odor in Lakewood, CO

    A rotten egg smell from the hot side only is almost always sulfate-reducing bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. Swapping the rod usually fixes it. We replace it with an aluminum-zinc rod or a powered anode, then chlorinate the tank to kill the bacteria living in it. Powered anodes never wear out and draw very little electricity.
