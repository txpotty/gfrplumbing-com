    Sump Pump Maintenance in Lakewood, CO

    Sudden summer thunderstorms bring flash flooding and hail, and heavy runoff can overwhelm storm drains and sewer lines.     Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Lakewood

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. Visible piping and connections get leak tested, shutoffs get turned, and old flex connectors get replaced. Gas work is done to code. If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside.

    Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Lakewood

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life. We drain sediment, check the anode rod, inspect the burner or elements and confirm the relief valve opens and reseats, whether the unit is a standard tank or a tankless model on the wall. Most failures give warning. We spot the signs early.

    Hidden Leak Checks in Lakewood, CO

    A leak you cannot see still costs money. With fixtures off, we watch the meter and then use acoustic and thermal tools to find the source.     Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Lakewood

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. We pump descaler through the service valves. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Softener Upkeep in Lakewood, CO

    Softeners fail quietly. A softener that quit regenerating still sends water, just hard water, and you only notice when scale appears. Bypass and drain get tested. Then we test the treated water.

    Water Heater Flush and Inspection in Lakewood

    Popping or rumbling means sediment. Minerals settle on the tank bottom, trap heat and cause the popping you hear when it fires. We flush it out. We descale tankless units through their service valves. Regular service helps keep the warranty valid.
