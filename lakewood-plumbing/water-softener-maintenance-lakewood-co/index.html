    Commercial Softener Service in Lakewood

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so a twin-tank system keeps one tank online while the other recharges. We set visits around your business hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Lakewood, CO

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. We empty, scrub and rinse the tank. Then we look for cracks around the brine well and overflow fitting and refill with fresh salt.

    Commercial Twin-Tank Maintenance in Lakewood

    Salons, car washes, hotels and restaurants put far more gallons through a softener than any house does, and a hard-water day can mean spotted glassware or a scaled ice machine. Twin-tank and progressive-flow systems need closer checks on the meter and the tank changeover. We service them every one to three months, depending on volume.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Lakewood, CO

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. Either the installer left the defaults in, or the family got bigger and the settings stayed put. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used rather than on a fixed timer schedule.

    Softener Upkeep for Restaurants and Shops in Lakewood

    Ice machines, espresso machines, dish machines and boilers all scale up fast on hard water, and equipment warranties often require treated water. We test hardness on every visit and log it for your records. Brine tank cleanouts happen more often on commercial units.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Lakewood

    Resin does the actual softening. The beads swap sodium for calcium and magnesium, and once they are loaded up they need brine to recharge. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to see whether the bed still holds its rated capacity.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Lakewood, CO

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we set the valve to regenerate more often so iron cannot load the bed again.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Lakewood, CO

    A bypass valve that does not seat fully lets raw water blend into the softened supply, and the hardness test is usually the first clue. The bypass gets rebuilt or swapped out and check the drain line for kinks. The drain line has to end above its receptor with an air gap.
