    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Lakewood, CO

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without squandering salt or water.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Lakewood, CO

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their capacity. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for maximum softening capacity. We make sure your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Commercial Softener Maintenance in Lakewood

    Restaurants, laundromats, salons and car washes rely on soft water to protect equipment like dishwashers, ice machines and boilers. Commercial twin-tank systems regenerate often and need closer attention. We set up scheduled visits to test hardness, clean brine tanks and service valves. Consistent maintenance keeps equipment warranties valid and reduces repair costs.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in Lakewood, CO

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can accumulate in your brine tank, hindering the softening process. Our maintenance includes thoroughly cleaning the brine tank, inspecting for any cracks or leaks and ensuring the float valve operates correctly. This step is crucial for the efficient regeneration of your water softener in Lakewood, CO.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Lakewood

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs much less than a new system.

    Preventative Maintenance for All Brands in Lakewood, CO

    Whether you have a Fleck, Culligan, Kenmore, GE or EcoPure system, our technicians are trained to service all major water softener brands.     Control Valve and Injector Service in Lakewood, CO

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Lakewood, CO

    The brine tank is crucial for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can accumulate, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a frequent issue we address in Lakewood homes.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Maintenance in Lakewood

    The cost of water softener maintenance in Lakewood, CO typically ranges from $100 to $250 for a standard service. This can vary based on the system's age, brand and any specific repairs needed. For a precise quote, please contact GFR Plumbing to assess your unit.

    We recommend professional water softener maintenance at least once a year. For homes with very hard water or high water usage, a twice-yearly check-up might be beneficial to support good performance and longevity of the unit.

    A brine tank full of water usually means a clogged injector, a stuck float or a drain line problem. Our service clears the cause and resets the system.

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank, preventing salt from dissolving into the water. This means the system can't regenerate properly, leading to hard water. Our maintenance service includes breaking up and removing salt bridges, bringing back your softener's function.

    While you can perform basic tasks like checking salt levels and refilling the brine tank, professional maintenance involves inspecting internal components, testing resin efficiency and diagnosing control valve issues. These tasks require professional tools and experience to ensure the system functions correctly and safely.

    Maintenance typically includes inspecting the brine tank for salt bridges, cleaning the tank, checking and cleaning the resin bed, lubricating moving parts, sanitizing the system and running diagnostic checks on the control valve. We also test water hardness to verify effectiveness.

    A standard water softener maintenance appointment typically takes 1 to 2 hours. This allows our technicians to thoroughly inspect, clean and test your system, confirming everything is operating correctly and efficiently. More complex issues might extend this time slightly.
