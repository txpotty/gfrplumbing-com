    Types of Toilet Installation in Lakewood, CO

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Lakewood

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. The tank sits behind an access plate. Because the carrier carries all the weight, we anchor its feet to the slab or framing first and check it for plumb before the drywall or tile goes up. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Lakewood, CO

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. It adds up quickly. Replacing a single 3.5 gallon toilet in a household of four can save several thousand gallons a year, and some water utilities have offered rebates on high-efficiency models, so it is worth asking yours. Pick a strong MaP rating.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Lakewood

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right. Before suggesting one, we put a gauge on a hose bib, and we mention the noise, because the flush carries through the whole house. They rarely clog.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in Lakewood

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A grab bar and a taller bowl help a lot. While the wall is open, we add 2 by 6 blocking between the studs at grab bar height, so the bar can later be screwed into solid wood instead of drywall anchors that pull loose. We also center the bowl to ADA spacing.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Lakewood

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. A frame set wrong puts the bowl too low or too high, and once tile is up there is no adjusting it. Measure twice here.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Lakewood

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. Set it too early and the tile setter has to cut around it, which leaves the flange low and forces a thick seal or an extender later on. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Lakewood

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out. Once the old wax is scraped off, the flange gets checked for cracks, new stainless bolts go in, and the new bowl is lowered straight onto a fresh seal. The old one leaves with us.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Lakewood

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. Water comes from a T-valve at the fill line. Heated seats with a warm-air dryer need a nearby GFCI outlet, and when there is none we bring in an electrician first. Non-electric seats take about thirty minutes.

    Local Toilet Installation in Lakewood, CO

    Older Lakewood homes can hide surprises under the toilet, like a cast iron flange rusted thin or a subfloor gone soft from an old leak. We check for both before setting anything. Any soft plywood gets cut back and replaced with new, glued and screwed down. It adds an hour, sometimes two.

    Toilet installers near you in Lakewood should be licensed and insured, and they should be willing to pull a permit when the job calls for one. Ask before anyone sets foot inside. A permit is usually needed to relocate a toilet or add a bath, but a straight swap in the same spot usually goes without one. We tell you which applies.
