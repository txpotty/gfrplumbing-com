    Hard Water and Disposals in Lakewood

    Around Lakewood, a yearly water heater flush helps clear the scale that collects at the bottom of the tank. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. Scale makes a disposal noisy. It also drains slower, because mineral crust narrows the tailpiece and the trap, so we look inside the old drain parts before we connect anything new to them. Heavily scaled parts get replaced the same visit.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Lakewood

    Kitchens that never had a disposal need some rework. First, the sink gets a disposal flange. Then the drain gets reworked, and a switched outlet goes in, with an electrician if there is no power in the cabinet yet. Drain slope to the stack gets checked as well.

    Evolution Upgrade Installs in Lakewood, CO

    Quiet is the main thing people notice after an Evolution install. Insulation around the motor drops the noise a lot.     Standard InSinkErator Installation in Lakewood

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty.
