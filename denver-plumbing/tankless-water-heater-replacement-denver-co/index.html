    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Denver

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger. Pipe length and meter capacity get checked against the sizing tables, and we confirm pressure at full fire. An undersized line causes ignition faults.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Denver

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. They vent through PVC. Acidic condensate goes through a neutralizer and then to an approved drain. Long heating seasons keep boilers and water heaters busy, so regular maintenance pays off in fewer midwinter breakdowns. The savings carry through every season because hot water use does.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Denver

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Most take under two hours.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Denver

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. The replacement gets matched to the existing gas, vent and mount where possible. Most swaps take four to six hours.

    Navien Venting and Install in Denver

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. We stay inside those limits. Go past them and the warranty suffers.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Denver, CO

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Navien also makes combi units that heat the house and the water. Both are in our wheelhouse.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Denver, CO

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. The vent swap is part of our job. Gas and water connections usually line up.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Denver

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Skip that and the new unit wears out first. Descaling gets scheduled by usage.
