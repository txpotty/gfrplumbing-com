    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Denver

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent of the damage. If your main water line in Denver has multiple leaks, severe corrosion, or is nearing the end of its lifespan, replacement is often the more sensible long-term solution. Our technicians will perform a complete inspection, including pressure testing and possibly video inspection, to recommend the best course of action. We consider factors like the age of your home, the material of the existing pipe, and the nature of the damage before making a recommendation.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Denver, CO

    Polybutylene pipe was used in some homes built in the 1980s and 1990s. It can become brittle and fail without warning. Many insurers flag it as a risk. We replace polybutylene service lines with durable materials. This reduces the chance of sudden leaks.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Denver, CO

    Many homes have high street pressure that can strain pipes and fixtures. We install pressure reducing valves set to a safe range. We also replace old gate valves with full-port ball valves at the house. This makes it easier to shut off water in an emergency. These upgrades safeguard your plumbing system.

    Commercial Water Service Replacement in Denver

    Commercial properties often have larger service lines and backflow assemblies. We replace commercial water lines and work with the utility when needed. We schedule work to limit disruption to tenants and customers. Backflow devices are tested and certified. We take care of permits and inspections.

    Emergency Water Line Repair and Replacement in Denver, CO

    A major water line break can flood your yard or leave you without water. Call (720) 727-9398 for emergency service. We find the break and make temporary repairs if needed. Then we plan a lasting replacement. Quick response limits damage.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Denver, CO

    Type K copper is a tough option for underground water lines. It resists punctures and lasts for decades. Copper costs more than PEX but is preferred by some homeowners. We install copper with proper bedding to cushion it. We can also install sleeves where it passes through foundations.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Denver

    Copper has long been a popular material for water lines due to its durability, corrosion resistance, and longevity. GFR Plumbing specializes in installing new copper water lines, known for their ability to withstand high water pressure and limit bacterial growth. While a higher upfront investment, copper provides a reliable and long-lasting water supply system, a great choice for many homes in Denver, CO.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Denver, CO

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a modern, flexible, and increasingly popular choice for water line replacement. It resists corrosion, scaling, and chlorine, making it highly durable for most water conditions. Before leaving a home for a winter trip, keep the heat at a safe setting and consider shutting off the main water valve. PEX can expand and contract without bursting, which is a real advantage when a cold snap hits Denver. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and quicker installation.
