    Sump Pit Installation in Denver, CO

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. A small 15 inch pit makes the pump cycle every minute in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Fewer starts mean a longer life.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Denver

    Duplex systems with two pumps and an alternating panel are standard for parking garages and multi-unit buildings with below-grade levels in Denver. The panel swaps the lead pump every cycle, so wear stays even. A high water alarm alerts building staff. We coordinate the wiring with your electrician.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Denver

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. The motor stays dry and you can reach it without pulling the pump, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Denver homes. Expect more noise.

    Residential Basement Sump Pump Installation in Denver

    A typical house pit holds an 18 by 22 inch basin with a 1/3 horsepower pump, and that covers most homes. We size the pump to your inflow and lift, not to the sale sticker. In Denver, we set the basin at the low corner, bed it in washed gravel and run 1-1/2 inch PVC out through the rim joist. The discharge ends well away from the foundation.

    Quiet Submersible Pumps for Finished Basements in Denver

    Finished basements call for a quiet pump and a sealed lid.
