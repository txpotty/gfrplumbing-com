    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Denver

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Either one lasts for decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    New Construction PEX Plumbing in Denver, CO

    On a new build, GFR Plumbing runs PEX supply lines while the framing is still open in Denver, CO. Plates get drilled, the manifold goes up, and every stub-out lands at the plan heights before the rough-in inspection and its pressure test. Steel nail plates cover every stud crossing. Builders like the pace.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Denver

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures.     Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in.     Uponor PEX System Installation in Denver

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring. The tech expands the pipe and ring together, pushes the fitting in, and the plastic shrinks back around the barb over the next few seconds, tighter than when it started. There is no crimp gauge to check. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Denver, CO

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting is pushed in while the pipe is open, and the plastic's memory shrinks it back around the barb, sealing tighter during the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Denver, CO

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.
