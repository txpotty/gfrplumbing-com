    Upsizing Water Lines for Better Pressure in Denver, CO

    Many homes built decades ago have a 3/4 inch line that cannot keep up with modern fixture loads, irrigation and pools.     PEX Water Line Installation in Denver

    PEX, or cross-linked polyethylene, is a common choice for its flexibility, durability and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Long heating seasons keep boilers and water heaters busy, so regular maintenance pays off in fewer midwinter breakdowns. GFR Plumbing professionally installs PEX water lines for both new construction and replacements in Denver. PEX offers excellent flow rates and is less prone to bursting than traditional rigid pipe, providing a long-lasting, cost-effective solution for your home's plumbing.
