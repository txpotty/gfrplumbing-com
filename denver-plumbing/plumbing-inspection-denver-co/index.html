    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Denver, CO

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Denver

    Many larger systems in Colorado draw on mountain snowmelt that runs fairly soft, while plains communities and groundwater sources often deliver harder water. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. You get options, not a pitch. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Report in Denver

    Before buying a home in Denver, a plumber's inspection can keep you from inheriting a sewer replacement. Each finding gets a photo, a short description and a repair range, which your agent can put to work in negotiations. Reports usually arrive same day. That timing fits most option periods.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Denver, CO

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location, and you keep a copy of the recording for your records or for a contractor.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Denver

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Colorado houses. Common finds include old pipe, stiff shutoffs, tired heaters, slow drains and rooty or sagging sewers. We test each one directly. Catching a corroded fitting or a partial sewer blockage now keeps it from becoming a ceiling stain or a backup on a holiday weekend.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Denver

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. We test the meter, watch for pressure loss and look for damp baseboards and mildew, and on slab homes we follow up with acoustic and thermal equipment to pinpoint the leak. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Denver, CO

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. Backflow devices, grease traps, large heaters, flushometers and the main sewer are all part of it. Inspectors like documentation. The written report helps facility managers plan and budget repairs before a health inspector or the water provider writes them up.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in Denver, CO

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. With all fixtures off, we watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera to trace the leak behind walls, under floors or in the slab. Catching it early keeps it small. Basements are common in Colorado, and shifting soil can crack floor drains, sewer lines, and basement walls over time.
