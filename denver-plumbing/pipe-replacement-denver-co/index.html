    What Goes Into a Repipe in Denver, CO

    PEX Pipe Installation in Denver

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. It resists scale and corrosion. Around Denver, a yearly water heater flush helps clear the scale that collects at the bottom of the tank. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, choosing a layout that fits the house. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Denver, CO

    We often leave old galvanized pipe in the wall. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, which keeps drywall damage low. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in Denver, CO

    A single bad spot can usually be fixed. Several leaks in different rooms, rusty water throughout, or pipe that crumbles when we cut it means the rest is close behind. We show you what we find.

    Emergency Pipe Replacement in Denver, CO

    A burst pipe needs fast action. Close the main shutoff right away. Then call, because water spreading through walls and floors does damage every minute, and we can usually get there, stop the leak and replace the failed section the same day. Drying comes next.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Denver, CO

    Copper still has fans. It handles heat well and lasts decades. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, while strapping each line properly, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in Denver

    Water under a slab shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a sound of running water, or a meter that spins with everything off. We locate the leak first. Then you have a choice between breaking the slab to fix that spot or rerouting the line through walls or the attic, and rerouting often costs less and avoids future slab leaks on that line. We explain both.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Denver, CO

    Pinhole leaks start small. A drip, then a stain. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, and when one shows up, more are often on the way along the same lines. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Denver, CO

    Drain lines fail too. Cast iron cracks and scales. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.
