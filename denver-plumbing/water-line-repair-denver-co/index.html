    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Denver

    Underground leaks hide well. The first clue is often a water bill that jumped for no reason. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or a spot near the walk that stays muddy days after rain. We confirm the leak at the meter, trace the pipe route and listen along it. The dig follows the sound.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Denver, CO

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. By the time one leaks, the rest of the pipe is close behind. We can clamp or replace a short section to get you through, and sometimes that buys a few good years. More often we recommend replacing the whole run from the meter to the house with copper or PEX. You get your flow back as a bonus.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Denver

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. First step is a pressure gauge on the outside spigot. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the main shutoff and the curb valve for anything half closed, and look at the pipe itself. Old galvanized can be choked to half its size with rust. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Denver, CO

    Replacement beats repair once a line has leaked two or three times, or when it is galvanized or polybutylene. Patching that kind of pipe tends to buy months, not years. We run new Type K copper or PEX from the meter to the house, usually in one day with a trench or a trenchless pull. We pull the permit, set up the inspection and pressure test the new line before it is covered.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Denver, CO

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and sunlight exposure before burial weakens the plastic over time. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Denver, CO

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Mountain homes in Colorado frequently rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, manganese, or natural minerals. Rust and scale close the pipe from the inside. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, along with pressure that drops when two fixtures run at once. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Most homes are done in a day.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Denver, CO

    A full break can flood the yard and leave the house dry at the same time. We shut it down first. The crew pumps the hole, finds the break and decides whether a coupling will do or whether a section needs replacing, setting up a temporary supply to the house if the job will take a while. Most breaks are fixed the same day. We photograph the damage for your insurance or HOA.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Denver

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. Trenchless avoids most of that. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, following the old line or cutting a new path beside it. Most replacements finish in a day. You also get the manufacturer warranty on the new pipe.

    Look for a wet or extra-green patch in the yard. A hiss at the meter is a giveaway. Low pressure at every fixture, rusty water and a climbing bill are other common clues. The surest check is your meter. A moving dial means water is escaping somewhere.
