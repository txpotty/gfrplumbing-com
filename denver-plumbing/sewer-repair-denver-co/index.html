    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Denver

    Sewer backups are usually caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or heavy tree root intrusion. Our team uses HD sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. This approach is very effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Colorado homes.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Denver, CO

    A single spot repair is the most affordable fix when the rest of the line is sound. Full replacement is the right call when the pipe is Orangeburg, badly bellied or broken in several places. We lay out all three options with real numbers so you can compare cost against how long the repair will last.
