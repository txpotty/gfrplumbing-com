    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Denver

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. The full length gets recorded. Older homes in Denver often hide two or three problems in one line, and the footage shows every one.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Denver, CO

    Replacement makes sense when the camera shows failures every few feet. Patching that line is wasted money. We price open trench replacement, pipe bursting and full-length lining side by side for your Colorado property, showing what each method costs and what it does to the yard. The right pick depends on depth and access.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Denver

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. Lining or a joint replacement keeps them from coming back.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Denver, CO

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Sewer gas follows soon after. Bird nests, leaves and ice can block a roof vent, and older galvanized or cast iron vent stacks crack inside the walls where nobody sees them, so we check the vent any time the drains act up but the main line looks clean. Fixes range from a quick clearing to a new section of stack.

    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Denver

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The clog is past the branches. We clear it from the nearest cleanout, then run the camera to see what caused it, because wipes, grease and roots all call for different follow-up. Older Colorado homes with clay lines often show roots right at the joints. We record what we find.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Denver, CO

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. Downtime costs money.     Root Intrusion Repair in Denver, CO

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. The joint is the real problem. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. A line rooted end to end is a lining or replacement job.
