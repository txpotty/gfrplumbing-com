    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Leak Repair in Denver

    Basement plumbing leak repairs in Denver typically cost $200 to $1,500, depending on the pipe and how hard it is to reach. Pipe on an open ceiling is the cheap case.     Most supply and drain repairs are finished in a few hours. A new sump takes most of a day. Drain stack replacements and slab leak repairs usually take one to two days, since they involve opening finished surfaces, letting any new concrete patch cure, and running a final test before we close things up.

    Plumbing leaks show up no matter the weather. Groundwater leaks track the rain. Turn off every fixture and watch the water meter, and if it still moves, the leak is in the pressurized lines, while water that only appears a few hours after a storm is almost always coming from outside.

    Gutters and downspouts do most of the work. Clear them and extend the outlets away from the walls. Test the sump each spring and swap rubber washer hoses for braided ones every five years.

    Simple pipe repairs and crack injection usually do not need a permit. Larger work is a different story. Replacing a drain stack, installing a new sump with a discharge line, adding a backwater valve, or excavating near the foundation may require a permit, and we pull it when it is needed.

    Crack repairs use two-part polyurethane or epoxy injection resin, depending on whether the crack is actively wet. Wet cracks get polyurethane. Our interior drains use rigid perforated pipe, which keeps its slope and can be jetted later.
