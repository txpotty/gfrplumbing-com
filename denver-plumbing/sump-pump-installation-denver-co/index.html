    Sump Pump Replacement in Denver, CO

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Denver

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Denver, CO

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary runs on house power and handles the everyday inflow, while the battery unit waits above it on a separate float. Finished basements are the usual reason. We install them in homes where a flood would ruin flooring and drywall.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Denver

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Denver

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. That saves some labor. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Denver, CO

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Denver, CO

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. Their 250 and 280 series cover most residential lifts, and the vertical float version fits narrow pits where a wide tethered float would rub. Parts are easy to find. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Denver

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.
