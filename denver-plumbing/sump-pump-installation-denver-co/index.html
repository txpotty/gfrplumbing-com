    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Denver, CO

    Severe storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out. We install backup systems with sealed batteries and charging units. Many include alarms that alert you to problems. Backups add an extra layer of protection for finished basements.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Denver, CO

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Denver

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Denver

    The cost of sump pump installation in Denver, CO typically ranges from $600 to $1,800, depending on the pump type, whether a new pit needs to be dug and the complexity of the plumbing and electrical work. Battery backup systems or advanced features will increase the price. We provide a detailed quote after assessing your home.

    A submersible sump pump sits inside the sump pit, making it quieter and more powerful, well suited for heavy use. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to reach for maintenance and often more affordable, though it can be louder.

    A well-maintained sump pump typically lasts between 7 and 10 years. The lifespan can vary depending on the quality of the pump, how often it runs and how well it is maintained. Regular inspections and cleaning can help extend its life.

    Yes, GFR Plumbing stands behind our work. We offer warranties on both the sump pump equipment we install and our labor. Exact warranty details vary by pump manufacturer and installation type, which we will discuss clearly with you before starting any work in Denver, CO.

    Strange noises, constant running, failure to turn on and visible rust are common signs. Testing your pump before the rainy season helps catch problems early.
