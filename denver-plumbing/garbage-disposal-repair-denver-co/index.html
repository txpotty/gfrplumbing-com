    Reliable Garbage Disposal Repair Services in Denver, CO

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Denver

    A humming disposal that will not spin almost always has a jam. Bones, fruit pits, glass or utensils can wedge the flywheel. We cut power, use a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit to free the flywheel and remove the object. Then we test the motor and press the reset button if needed.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Denver, CO

    A disposal that grinds but drains slowly usually has a clogged discharge tube or trap arm. Grease, rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We clear the line with the right tools and check for proper slope. We also explain what not to put the disposal.

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Denver, CO

    Food buildup and grease in the chamber and baffle create odors. Mountain homes in Colorado frequently rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, manganese, or natural minerals. Mineral scale can trap food particles and make smells worse. We clean the splash guard and chamber and check the drain for buildup. Replacing a worn splash guard often helps control smell.

    Preventative Maintenance and Longevity Tips in Denver, CO

    Beyond repairs, GFR Plumbing also offers advice on preventative maintenance for your garbage disposal in Denver, CO. We can inspect your unit for early signs of wear and tear, recommend proper usage habits, and suggest products to keep it running smoothly. This proactive approach helps extend the lifespan of your disposal and avoids costly future repairs, saving you time and money.

    Disposal Repair vs Replacement Costs in Denver, CO

    Many jams and minor leaks can be fixed for much less than a new unit. If the disposal is over eight to ten years old, leaks from the body, or has a failing motor, replacement is generally the better value. We provide both prices when it is close. You decide what makes sense for your kitchen.

    Electrical Issues & Motor Repair in Denver

    If your garbage disposal hums but doesn't grind, or doesn't turn on at all, it could be an electrical issue or a motor problem. We check circuit breakers, reset buttons, and internal wiring. For motor failures, we determine if a repair is feasible or if replacement is the more cost-effective solution, always putting your safety first in Denver.

    Noisy or Grinding Disposal Repair in Denver

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove debris. If the bearings are failing, the noise will continue. In that case, we can quote a less noisy replacement unit.

    Leaking Disposal Repair in Denver, CO

    Leaks can come from the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube or the disposal body itself. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty, and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed. In that case, replacement is the smarter option.
