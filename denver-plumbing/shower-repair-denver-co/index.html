    Fixing Showers in Denver, CO

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Denver

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. We swap in a new one. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Denver, CO

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. Both ride on our trucks. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.

    Commercial Shower Service in Denver

    Hotels and dorms cannot wait days on a shower. With commercial cartridges on board, we fix rooms one at a time so the rest stay open. We can work nights. We set and test the limit stop on each valve before the next room.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Denver, CO

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not. A sudden leak behind the wall or a valve that will not close can soak framing fast, so the main valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house, is your first stop. Then call us. We stop the leak, make the repair or a safe temporary fix and check for anything that needs drying.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Denver, CO

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Minerals bind soap scum and hair into a tough mat that clings to the pipe walls and gets thicker with every shower. We cut it out with an auger. For stubborn buildup in the branch line, we jet the line with water instead of using acid cleaners.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Denver

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. The spool should shift to keep the mix steady when one side loses pressure, but scale can lock it in one spot. A new cartridge fixes it. Water that never gets hot enough may only need the limit stop turned.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Denver

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Denver, CO

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. We start with the head. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.
