    What Our Tankless Techs Fix in Denver, CO

    Electric Tankless Element Repair in Denver, CO

    Electric units fail in their own way. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart models use two to four heating elements in separate chambers, so a single burned element leaves the heater behind at high flow, while a failed flow switch means no heat at all. We check each element's resistance, read voltage under load and clamp amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.

    After-Hours Tankless Repair in Denver, CO

    Some tankless failures will not wait for Monday. A leak at the exchanger or a gas odor at the heater means we come out after hours in Denver. If you smell gas, leave the house first and call the gas utility from outside, then call us, and our tech can close the gas cock at the unit and shut the water valves until the part arrives.

    Rinnai Error Code Repair in Denver

    Rinnai owners usually call about a code 10, which points to an air supply or exhaust blockage. Bird nests and dryer lint at the terminal come first, then pull the front cover to inspect the intake screen and the combustion fan. Vent runs longer than the manual allows can trigger it too. We measure.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Denver, CO

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. A plugged trap will lock the heater out. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Denver, CO

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, so a code 11 on one unit means something else on another. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, but the tests behind them differ. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Denver

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, and they hold temperature off thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. We pull the screen, read sensor values in the service menu and compare thermistor resistance to the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Repair in Denver, CO

    A tankless heater that dies on a cold morning in Denver is an emergency for a family of five. We take no-hot-water calls first, and the truck carries the parts that fail most: flame rods, igniters, flow sensors, thermistors and inlet filters. If a heat exchanger is leaking, we shut the water off at the service valves and order the part the same day. Control boards take longer.

    Commercial Tankless Repair in Denver, CO

    Hotels and gyms draw hot water from open to close, so their tankless racks scale up two or three times faster than a home unit. We service rack systems with manifold kits, checking each heater's flow sensor and fault log separately, and set a descaling schedule by run hours. Quarterly is common.
