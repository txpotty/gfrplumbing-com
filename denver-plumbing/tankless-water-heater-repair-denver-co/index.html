    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Denver, CO

    Rinnai is a popular tankless brand, and its units can face challenges such as scaling from hard water or issues with their flame rod. Our technicians in Denver, CO are skilled at Rinnai tankless water heater repair, focusing on descaling services, fan motor replacements and fixing issues with gas valve assemblies. We make sure your Rinnai system continues to provide reliable, on-demand hot water, following manufacturer specifications.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Denver, CO

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and confirm proper drainage. PVC venting is also inspected for slope and sagging.

    Ignition Failure Repair in Denver, CO

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet. Undersized gas lines are a common hidden cause when a tankless unit replaced a tank. We correct the issue so the unit lights every time.

    Pilot Light and Ignition Repair in Denver, CO

    For gas-fired tankless water heaters, a faulty ignition system can completely stop hot water production. Our team in Denver, CO diagnoses issues with igniters, flame sensors and gas valves. We confirm proper gas pressure and ventilation are maintained for safe and efficient operation. Timely repair of these components prevents dangerous gas problems and restores your unit's ability to heat water on demand.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Denver

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace faulty parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.

    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in Denver, CO

    Descaling service usually costs $175 to $350, and sensor or igniter repairs run $250 to $600. Heat exchanger replacement can cost $800 to $1,500 or more unless covered by warranty. A new tankless unit installed usually ranges from $3,000 to $5,500. We compare these costs against the age and condition of your unit.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Denver

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water faster. Pump failures, check valve problems or wrong schedule settings can cause long waits or lukewarm water. We test the pump, check valves and controls and set the schedule to match your routine. A properly tuned system saves both water and gas.
