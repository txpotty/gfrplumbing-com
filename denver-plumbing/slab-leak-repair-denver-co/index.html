    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Denver

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. A reroute bypasses it.     Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Denver

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. It finds rebar and cables. Combined with a listening device, radar finds leaks in any pipe type, even with traffic noise, since radar maps the route and the mic confirms the spot. We use both in Denver.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Denver, CO

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out.
