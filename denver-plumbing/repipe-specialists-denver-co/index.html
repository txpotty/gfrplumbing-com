    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Denver

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more involved. We coordinate with the HOA, schedule water shutoffs that may affect neighbors and protect adjoining units from dust and noise as much as we can. Building-wide jobs go in phases. Big buildings get repiped by stack or wing, which lets residents stay home.

    Type L Copper Repiping in Denver

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. Our copper repipes use Type L with proper hangers and isolators so the pipe does not wear against framing. All joints use lead-free solder. Because some water chemistries eat copper, we look at your leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Commercial Repiping in Denver, CO

    Commercial buildings in Denver, CO often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. We phase commercial repipes by floor, suite or riser so most of the building stays in service while we work. Night work is possible. We use Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX sized from fixture units and installed to commercial code.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Denver, CO

    Mountain homes in Colorado frequently rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, manganese, or other minerals. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Adding the loop now costs less than later.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Denver, CO

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 once access and restoration are included. The numbers add up quickly. After two or three leaks, a repipe often pays for itself, and we give you a side-by-side comparison so you can make that call with real numbers in front of you.

    Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Denver, CO

    A widespread pipe failure sometimes forces a repipe sooner than you planned. We make the house safe and usable first, with temporary repairs that get you water, then schedule the full repipe as soon as the crew and permit allow. We move fast.     Slab Leak Repiping in Denver

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines slower, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Denver

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through. Our estimator counts fixtures, checks access, finds the main and asks about any planned remodels before choosing a route. Then we size the lines. Bigger homes may need a 1-inch trunk so the shower does not drop when the dishwasher fills.
