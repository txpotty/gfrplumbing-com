    This branch takes more grease and food than any other line in most homes. The grease goes far down the pipe.     Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Denver, CO

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Gaskets fail too. We replace worn pop-up assemblies, broken tub shoes and leaking shower drain bodies, often working from inside the tub so the ceiling underneath stays intact. When access is needed, we keep the opening small.

    Laundry and Floor Drain Repair in Denver

    A washer dumps a lot of water fast. Small standpipes overflow. We correct standpipe height, replace cracked laundry box drains and add venting where it is missing, and for garage and utility floor drains we restore the trap seal so sewer odor stays in the pipe. Trap primers get repaired too.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Repair in Denver, CO

    Kitchen drains take grease, food and soap scum. No drain clogs more often. We clear the branch, replace corroded or cracked tailpieces, fix leaking slip nuts and correct trap heights so the arm has the right fall to the wall. We also check the disposal and dishwasher connections.

    Main Line Drain Repair in Denver

    A problem in the main line shows up at every drain in the house. The lowest fixture backs up first. Roots, cracks from ground movement and collapsed sections are the common causes, and we confirm which one on camera, then repair it with a spot dig, a trenchless liner or a section replacement depending on what the footage shows. You see the footage first.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Denver, CO

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. Most go unnoticed for months. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then replace the bad length with pipe that matches what is already there. Every repair is done to code.

    Storm Drain Repair in Denver, CO

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. Drought cycles are common here, so water-efficient fixtures and careful irrigation choices are part of most plumbing upgrades. Leaves and silt clog them. We clear the line with a jetter, camera it to look for crushed or separated sections, and repair broken pipe so the runoff reaches daylight instead of soaking the soil next to your foundation. We check the discharge end every time.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Denver

    A simple clog clearing starts around $150. Main line work costs more. Complex main line repairs can run past $3,000, depending on the drain involved, the extent of the damage and the access. We price it after we look.

    Age and corrosion lead the list. Soil movement is close behind. Tree roots, temperature swings, heavy loads over buried pipe, plus bad installation such as skipped primer or unsupported runs, can all break a drain early. Most failures have more than one cause.

    Fixing the cause instead of the clog. Pitch is the big one. After we correct a sag, replace a rough fitting or add a missing vent, the drain should run clean as long as grease and wipes stay out of it. Old metal pipe may need periodic jetting.

    We install PVC and ABS for most new drain work. Some jobs call for cast iron. Cast iron still makes sense in some multi-story walls where noise matters, and we tie new pipe into existing metal drains with listed transition fittings. Every part meets code.

    Slow drains, gurgling and sewer smell are the big three warnings. So is water under the sink. A drain that clogs again a few weeks after it was cleared, or a stain on the ceiling below a tub or shower, means the pipe or a fitting needs repair rather than another cleaning. Small leaks get worse.

    Call us if a plunger or a small hand snake has not cleared it. Repeat clogs too. Multiple slow drains, sewer smell or water coming up in a different fixture than the one you are using point to a blockage or defect past the reach of a home snake. That needs a camera or a bigger machine.

    Tightening a slip nut or replacing a plastic trap is fair game. Hand snakes are fine too. Anything inside a wall, under a floor or involving cast iron, or a repeat leak at the same joint, should go to a plumber. Skip the caustic cleaners.

    Most trap and fixture drain repairs take one to two hours. Wall work takes longer. Replacing drain pipe inside a wall or under a floor usually runs half a day or more with access setting the pace. The estimate includes the timeline.
