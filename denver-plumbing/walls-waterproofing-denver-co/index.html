    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Denver, CO

    Basement Wall Sealing in Denver, CO

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Long heating seasons keep boilers and water heaters busy, so annual maintenance pays off in fewer midwinter breakdowns. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Denver, CO

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Denver

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Denver, CO

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Homes from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may still have polybutylene supply lines, which are known to fail at fittings. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. Old mortar joints get repointed first.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Denver, CO

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation. Sometimes the fix is a downspout extension and regrading the soil so it slopes away from the house, six inches of drop over the first ten feet, and that costs a few hundred dollars. We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. Every option comes written out with its price.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Denver, CO

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while replacing drywall, cabinets and flooring again and again costs far more, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill. Exterior block sealing depends on wall length. Your quote lists each option with its price.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Denver

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane foam, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Vertical cracks are usually a one-visit job.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Denver, CO

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and tie it into a sump pit so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.
