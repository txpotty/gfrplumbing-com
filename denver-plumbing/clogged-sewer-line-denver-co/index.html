    What Our Sewer Line Service Covers in Denver, CO

    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in Denver

    Most home sewer clogs come from three things: roots, grease and wipes. Many times they team up. Grease coats the pipe, wipes snag on the coating and roots growing through a joint hold the whole mass in place until the line stops. We clear it with a sewer machine, then camera the line to see which one started it.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Denver

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That is the difference. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale while the nozzle drives itself forward. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it. Results often last years.

    Commercial Clogged Sewer Line Service in Denver

    Commercial sewer lines in Denver carry far more than a house does. Kitchen grease is the big one. GFR Plumbing clears restaurant mains, apartment laterals and office drains with heavy equipment, and we can set up a jetting schedule to keep the line open between grease trap pumpings.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Denver, CO

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. It scrubs the whole wall. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, while a jetter removes the grease, scale and fine roots coating the pipe so the next clog has nothing to start on. We camera the pipe first. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Denver

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. The cable is not the fix. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and the cable only bores a narrow path through the middle that closes up again within weeks. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. It lasts much longer.

    Preventative Sewer Line Maintenance in Denver

    Lines that clog from roots once a year can be cleaned before they clog. It saves you the emergency. GFR Plumbing sets up scheduled cutting or jetting for homes in Denver with older clay or cast iron, usually every 12 to 24 months depending on how fast the roots come back. Each visit includes a quick camera check.

    Emergency Sewer Backup Service in Denver, CO

    Sewage coming up through a shower or floor drain needs attention right away. Turn off anything draining. Our emergency crews stop the backup, clear the line and contain the mess, and we can refer you to a restoration company if sewage reached carpet or drywall. Photos and notes go to your insurance.

    Tree Root Removal Services in Denver

    Older Denver homes with mature trees see more root clogs than anyone. Old joints let them in. GFR Plumbing cuts roots out with a sectional machine and a root saw sized to the pipe, then jets the line to wash out the cut roots. Root treatment can slow regrowth between visits.
