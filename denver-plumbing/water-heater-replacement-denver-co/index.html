    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Denver, CO

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Denver, CO homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Then we leak test.

    A standard tank replacement costs the least up front in Denver, CO.     Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Denver, CO

    A leaking tank can put 40 or 50 gallons on the floor faster than most people expect. Close the cold supply on top, turn off the gas or breaker, open a hot faucet to relieve pressure, and we will aim to have a plumber and tank there the same day. Do not wait it out.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Denver, CO

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Denver

    Homes from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may still have polybutylene supply lines, which are known to fail at fittings. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. Two-person crew, always.
