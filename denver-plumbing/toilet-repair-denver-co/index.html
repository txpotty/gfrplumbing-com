    Toilet Flush Repair in Denver, CO

    Flush Valve Seat Repair in Denver

    Scale on the flush valve seat is the reason a brand-new flapper sometimes keeps leaking, because the rubber cannot seal against a rough, crusted ring. Feel the seat with a finger.     Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Denver

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out.
