    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Denver, CO

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Around Denver, a yearly water heater flush helps clear the scale that collects at the bottom of the tank. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Denver

We make sure your propane system is installed correctly for maximum efficiency and safety, providing reliable hot water for your Denver residence.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Denver

    For many homes in Denver, gas tankless water heaters are a common choice due to their powerful heating and lower operating costs compared to electric models.     Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Denver, CO

    Tankless installation costs more upfront than a standard tank because of the unit price, gas line upgrades and venting changes. Over time, lower standby energy losses and a longer expected lifespan offset part of that cost. Tankless units often last 15 to 20 years with proper descaling, compared with about 8 to 12 years for many tanks in hard water areas. We give you a side-by-side quote so you can decide what fits your budget.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Denver

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units. A 40 gallon tank heater uses roughly 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need up to 199,000 BTU, so the gas line often needs to be upsized. Venting changes from a metal flue to PVC or stainless, and the unit moves to a wall location. We remove and dispose of your old tank and patch the old flue penetration.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Denver

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to deliver hot water to distant fixtures. A recirculation pump or dedicated return line keeps hot water close to faucets, reducing wait time and water waste. Some units have built-in pumps with smart scheduling. We design the system to fit your plumbing layout and usage patterns.
