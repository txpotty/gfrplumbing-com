    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Denver, CO

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for decades, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements.     Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Denver, CO

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A compact gray water pump handles it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer drops at once, with a lint filter to keep fibers off the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Denver

    Macerating systems like Saniflo add a bath fixture or two without breaking the concrete floor. The unit sits behind the toilet, grinds the waste and pumps it through a small discharge line, often three-quarter inch or one inch, up to the nearest drain, which makes it a good fit for a small basement half bath. Venting still applies.

    Ejector Pump Replacement in Denver

    Ejector pumps usually last 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Watch for constant running, a hum or grind, repeated alarms and odor at the pit, since any of them means trouble is close. The float usually goes first.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Denver, CO

    Upgrading an old ejector can often keep the existing pit. If the basin is sound, we can swap in a better pump, replace the old swing check with a new one, add a full-port shutoff and install a high water alarm, which covers most of what a new system offers for a fraction of the cost. Cracked basins get replaced.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Denver, CO

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Two pumps, one alarm.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Denver

    Residential ejector systems in Denver serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. Each system needs a sealed basin, a pump with a float, a check valve and a vent. Simple parts, exact install. Install mistakes cause more trouble than hardware, so the vent and lid seal get extra attention.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Denver, CO

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. That speeds up new installs, and their wide-angle float switches are less likely to hang up on the basin wall than older tethered floats, which is one of the most common reasons a pump fails to start. Switch failures cause many calls.
