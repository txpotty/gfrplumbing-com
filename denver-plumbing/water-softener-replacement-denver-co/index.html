    Metered Softener Replacement in Denver

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first month.

    Fleck Water Softener Replacement in Denver, CO

    Fleck is another leading brand known for durable water softener components. When your Fleck system needs replacing in Denver, CO, our plumbers are proficient with these units.     Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Denver

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Denver. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are well known for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water.
