    Softener Systems We Replace in Denver, CO

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Denver, CO

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. We check those first and test hardness before and after the unit, so you know whether it is softening at all. Discontinued valves usually mean replacement.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Denver, CO

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Colorado homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Denver, CO

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Denver

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. Metered valves like the Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 count gallons and regenerate only when the resin is close to spent. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Denver, CO

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. It depends on the valve. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Denver

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. We replace timer units with metered valves that regenerate on gallons used. Salt use usually drops. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Denver, CO

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. Put the unit in bypass first. Most stuck or leaking systems get replaced in a day or two, sooner if the right size is in stock.

    Long-Term Softener Costs in Denver, CO

    Long-term cost on a softener is mostly salt and water. Compared with a timer unit, a metered valve set efficiently often uses a third less salt, which matters over a fifteen-year life. That is how we compare models.
