    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Denver

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Denver. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. GFR Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Denver, CO

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Before leaving a home for a winter trip, keep the heat at a safe setting and consider shutting off the main water valve. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.
