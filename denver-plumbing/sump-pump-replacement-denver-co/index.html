    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Denver

    A standard sump pump replacement usually costs $500 to $1,200 installed. Battery backup systems usually add $800 to $1,800. Sewage ejector pumps generally cost $1,200 to $2,500.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by GFR Plumbing can also help lengthen its lifespan.

    If storms knock out power in your area or you have finished living space below grade, a backup is a smart investment. A battery unit with a high-water alarm typically runs six to ten hours of intermittent pumping on a full charge.

    We install a variety of trusted sump pump brands known for their reliability and performance, including Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps and Little Giant. Our plumbers can help you pick the best brand and model for your home's requirements and budget.

    We install Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne pumps. Cast iron models are our pick for durability.

    While it may seem straightforward, professional sump pump replacement is highly recommended. It involves electrical connections, proper plumbing for the discharge line, and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding and comply with local Denver codes.

    Most sump pump replacements take one to two hours. Battery backups or new discharge lines take longer.

    Sump pumps generally have a lifespan of 7 to 10 years. Even if they appear to be working, their efficiency can decrease over time. Regular inspection and testing are important, and replacement is advisable as they approach the end of their expected service life.
