    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Broomfield

    The cost of well pump replacement in Broomfield, CO typically ranges from $1,500 to $3,500, though it can vary.     Most new well pumps come with a manufacturer's warranty, typically ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. GFR Plumbing also backs our installation work with our own labor warranty for complete peace of mind.

    Well pump replacement is a complex job that involves high voltage electricity, heavy equipment and possibly deep well access. It requires specialized tools and experience for proper installation, electrical safety and compliance with local plumbing codes. It is strongly recommended to hire a licensed professional to avoid injury, property damage or costly mistakes.

    Shallow well pumps, or jet pumps, are designed for wells where the water level is usually 25 feet or less from the pump, using suction to draw water. Deep well pumps, or submersible pumps, are placed directly in the water near the bottom of the well and push water up, suited for depths over 25 feet. Choosing the correct type is critical for efficiency and performance.

    Common signs of a failing well pump include no water, sputtering faucets, reduced water pressure, discolored water or unusually high electricity bills. If your pump runs constantly or cycles on and off too frequently, these are also signs that replacement might be necessary.
