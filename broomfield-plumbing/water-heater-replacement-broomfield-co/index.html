    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Broomfield

    Most standard gas or electric tank replacements cost $1,400 to $3,000 including the heater, labor and typical code upgrades. Attic installs, larger tanks or relocations can raise the cost to $3,000 to $4,500.

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith water heaters. Each offers dependable performance and a range of warranties.

    Upgrading provides more reliable hot water, improved energy efficiency and lower utility bills, better performance and a reduced risk of leaks or breakdowns. Newer models often have advanced features and longer warranties, improving comfort and safety in your home.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but usually have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Manufacturer tank warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line adjustments.

    Leaks at the base, rusty water, rumbling noises, inconsistent hot water and an age over 10 years are typical signs. A leaking tank should be replaced right away.

    While it might seem tempting, water heater replacement involves gas lines, high voltage electricity, water pressure and heavy equipment.
