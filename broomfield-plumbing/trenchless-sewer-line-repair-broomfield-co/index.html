    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Broomfield, CO

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Colorado homeowners.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Broomfield, CO

    Tree and shrub roots seek out moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause recurring backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every couple of months.

    Sectional Point Repair in Broomfield

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Broomfield

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for extensive trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Broomfield that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Pipe Bursting in Broomfield, CO

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is best for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It typically requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Broomfield

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot get into. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.
