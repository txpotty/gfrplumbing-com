    Why Choose GFR Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Broomfield, CO?

    Skipped pump-outs usually announce themselves at the floor drain first, with gray water pooling around the grate after the dish machine drains. Then comes the smell. That sour, rotten odor is grease breaking down and releasing hydrogen sulfide gas. The same gas corrodes concrete, which shortens the life of an in-ground interceptor.

    • Odor Control, A pumped trap stops the rotten-egg odor that decaying grease gives off.
    • System Longevity, Less grease in the line means fewer jetting calls and a longer life for the drain pipe.
    • Extended Lifespan, Less hydrogen sulfide in the tank means slower corrosion of concrete and baffles.
