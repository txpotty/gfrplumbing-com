    Why Polybutylene Fails in Broomfield

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Mountain homes in Colorado frequently rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, manganese, or natural minerals. Heat makes it worse. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Broomfield

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. For slab houses, new PEX-A runs through the attic and drops down interior walls, so none of it sits under the floor. It also tolerates freezing better. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter open, so pressure at the far shower stays strong.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Broomfield, CO

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. Every fixture, appliance and the water heater gets new pipe from the main, and most houses are finished in two to four days with water on each evening.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Broomfield

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Check the heater first. It also shows up under sinks, at the main and through garage walls, and we follow it into the attic to see how much of the house it feeds.

    The Cost of Polybutylene Replacement in Broomfield, CO

    Price depends mostly on house size, the number of bathrooms, attic access and whether you pick PEX or copper. PEX is the cheaper option. One burst can cost more in drywall, flooring and cabinets than the full replacement, not counting your deductible. Every estimate breaks out labor, pipe, permit and drywall.

    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Broomfield

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. Watch the water meter. With every fixture off, a spinning leak indicator on the meter means water is going somewhere, and we use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to find it without opening walls at random. Finding it lets us make a temporary repair and plan the full replacement.
