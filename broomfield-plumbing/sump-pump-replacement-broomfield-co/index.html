    Commercial Sump Pump Service in Broomfield

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller.     Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Broomfield, CO

    Pit filling, pump not running? Call (720) 727-9398 and we will send someone. Leave the lid off, get cords off the wet floor, and run a wet vac to keep the water level below the top of the pit while you wait. We carry common pumps, so the swap usually happens on the spot.     Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Broomfield

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. We replace those pumps and controls to meet code. Two alternating pumps on one controller mean one can fail without flooding the pit. Insurers sometimes ask for proof.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Broomfield

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. We pull the old unit, scoop the gravel and silt out of the basin, and set the new pump on a flat brick so it does not suck up debris. The check valve gets replaced at the same time. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Broomfield

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. The usual failure is a burned-out motor or a float that hangs up on the cord. We replace it with the right Wayne or another cast iron unit. Takes about two hours.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Broomfield

    Sudden summer thunderstorms bring flash flooding and hail, and heavy runoff can overwhelm storm drains and sewer lines. A primary pump on household current does nothing once the breaker panel goes dark. We add a 12-volt backup pump on its own float, mounted a few inches above the primary, with a deep-cycle battery and a charger that tests itself. It also covers a stuck primary switch. A high-water alarm lets you know the backup has taken over.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Broomfield, CO

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. If you have a Zoeller now, we can usually match the discharge outlet and switch style so the swap goes quickly. Pump not keeping up? The M98 steps up to half horsepower.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Broomfield, CO

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.
