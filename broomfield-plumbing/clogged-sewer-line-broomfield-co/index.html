    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Sewer Line in Broomfield

    The cost to clear a clogged sewer line in Broomfield, CO can range from $250 to $700, depending on the severity of the clog, the method required (e.g., snaking vs. hydro-jetting), and whether camera inspection is needed. Complex issues like extensive root intrusion or pipe damage will be on the higher end, as they may need more intensive work.

    Most main line clearings take one to two hours. Severe root intrusion or lines without a cleanout can take longer. Camera inspection adds about 30 to 60 minutes.

    Cutting roots clears the line but roots will grow back if the joints remain open. Root treatments can slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement seals the joints for good. We can show you the extent of root intrusion on camera.

    Multiple drains backing up at once, gurgling toilets when the washer drains and sewage odors in the house are classic signs. Water rising in the shower when you flush a toilet is another strong clue. Wet spots or sewage smell near the cleanout outdoors can also signal a clog.

    Homeowners are typically responsible for the lateral from the house to the property line or city tap. If the clog is in the city main, the municipality handles it. Our camera and locator help determine where the blockage is.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid flushing anything but human waste and toilet paper. Be careful about pouring grease down kitchen drains, use drain screens, and consider periodic professional drain cleanings, especially if you have mature trees near your sewer lines in Broomfield.

    Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water streams to completely clean the interior of your sewer pipes, removing grease, scale, mineral buildup, and roots. It is used for stubborn, deep-seated clogs and as a preventative measure, especially effective for older pipes in Broomfield that accumulate significant buildup.

    If our camera inspection reveals that your clog is due to a broken, collapsed, or severely damaged sewer line, clearing the clog will only be a temporary fix.
