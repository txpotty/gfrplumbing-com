    Drainage System Integration in Broomfield, CO

    Effective walls waterproofing often requires a solid drainage system to divert water away from your foundation.     Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Broomfield, CO

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry for good.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Broomfield

    Even minor cracks in foundation walls can allow significant water intrusion. Our services include crack repair using epoxy or polyurethane injection to seal these entry points permanently. This method fills the entire depth of the crack, restoring strength and preventing water penetration. We address both vertical and horizontal cracks, knowing that horizontal cracks can indicate structural issues that need a thorough assessment before sealing. Addressing these early prevents further water damage and costly future repairs.
