    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Boulder

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Boulder can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Boulder, CO

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Boulder

    GFR Plumbing often installs Uponor PEX systems, a leading brand known for high-quality, durable PEX-A piping. Uponor's ProPEX expansion fittings create strong, reliable and leak-resistant connections, well suited for the demands of a home's plumbing. We recommend Uponor for its proven track record and longevity, giving Boulder homeowners peace of mind about their plumbing investment.
