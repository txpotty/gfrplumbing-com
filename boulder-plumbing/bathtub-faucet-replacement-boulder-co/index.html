    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Boulder, CO

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Boulder, CO

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. Unlike a pressure-balance valve, which only reacts to pressure changes, a thermostatic valve senses the actual water temperature and adjusts the mix, which matters for kids and older adults who cannot react fast to a hot spike. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Boulder, CO

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. A new valve makes sense for discontinued parts, a cracked body or an old faucet with no scald protection. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.

    Tub Spout Replacement in Boulder

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts clamp to copper with a set screw, and threaded ones screw onto a pipe nipple. We check yours before buying. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Boulder

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister each use their own cartridges and trim threads, and a new trim kit from the same maker as the valve usually bolts right on without touching the plumbing. Mixing brands rarely works. We find the logo or the casting mark first.
