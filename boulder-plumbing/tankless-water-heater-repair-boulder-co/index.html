    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Boulder, CO

    Rinnai units are common, and scale causes most of their service calls. The LC code is the unit's own warning that the exchanger needs a flush. We also see dirty flame rods behind a code 11 or 12 and tired combustion fans behind a code 61, and each part gets tested against Rinnai's published values. Parts come from the factory list.

    Same-Day Tankless Repair in Boulder

    Water dripping from inside the cabinet, or a fault you cannot reset, should be seen that day. We bring sensors, igniters, flame rods and filters, so a good share of visits end with hot water.     Condensing Tankless Repair in Boulder, CO

    Condensing tankless heaters recover heat from the flue gas and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. A plugged trap will lock the heater out. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Boulder, CO

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, so a code 11 on one unit means something else on another. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, yet the checks behind each one are not the same. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.

    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in Boulder, CO

    Descaling usually runs $175 to $350, while a sensor or igniter repair tends to run $250 to $600. A heat exchanger out of warranty can cost $800 to $1,500 in parts and labor. A new unit costs a lot more. Installed, a new gas tankless typically runs $3,000 to $5,500, so on a unit past 15 years with a failed exchanger we usually lean toward replacing.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Boulder

    Pressure at full fire matters on a gas tankless burner, not just at the moment it lights. We put a manometer on the inlet test port and run every hot tap in the house, because pressure that drops below the manufacturer's minimum under load causes flame loss and cold slugs. Then we check the exhaust. Codes come off when the numbers are right.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Boulder

We connect a circulation pump to the service valves, circulate descaling solution through the exchanger for close to an hour, then flush and clean the inlet screen.
