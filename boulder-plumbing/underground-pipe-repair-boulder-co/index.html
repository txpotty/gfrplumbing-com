    Pipe Bursting Services in Boulder, CO

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is very effective for complete pipe replacement, creating a new, durable pipe of the same or larger diameter. It's a robust solution for severely damaged or collapsed sewer and water lines, offering a long-term fix in Boulder, CO.

    Main Water Line Repair in Boulder

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house and is under constant pressure. When it leaks, you may notice a spinning meter dial with all fixtures off, low pressure or a wet spot that never dries. We locate the leak, expose the pipe and splice in a new section with approved fittings. If the line is old galvanized or polybutylene, we will recommend a full replacement before more breaks happen.

    Underground Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Boulder, CO
