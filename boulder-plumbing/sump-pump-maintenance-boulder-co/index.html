    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Boulder, CO

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Boulder, CO.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Boulder

    Sudden summer thunderstorms bring flash flooding and hail, and heavy runoff can back up storm drains and sewer lines. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Boulder

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a scheduled basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Boulder, CO

    Routine maintenance usually costs a fraction of replacing a burned-out pump and cleaning up a flooded basement. A new installed sump pump often runs $400 to $1,200 depending on horsepower and backup options. Most pumps last seven to ten years with care. We will tell you plainly whether yours has more years left or is ready to retire.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Boulder

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last years longer.

    GFR Plumbing is proficient in maintaining all major sump pump brands, including popular models from Zoeller and Wayne.     Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Boulder, CO

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get hung up.
