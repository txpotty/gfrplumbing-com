    Commercial Water Heater Service in Boulder, CO

We check draft and combustion, test water temperature at the far end of the building and flush the tank, then tell you whether repair or replacement makes more sense. Tankless units get a descaling flush. Recirculation pumps on long runs get tested as part of the visit.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Boulder, CO

    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in Boulder, CO

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property.     Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Boulder

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines slower, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. You get a price on each option.
