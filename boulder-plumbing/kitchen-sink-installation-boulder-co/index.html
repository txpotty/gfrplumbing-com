    Top-mount Sink Installation in Boulder

    Top-mount, or drop-in, sinks are a common and straightforward option for many Colorado homes. While simpler to install, proper sealing and alignment are still crucial to prevent water damage and ensure stability. Our plumbers in Boulder install top-mount sinks, applying durable sealant around the rim to create a watertight barrier. We make sure the sink is securely fastened and all plumbing connections, including the faucet and drain, are properly tightened and tested for leaks.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Boulder

    Drop-in sinks rest on the countertop with a rim, making them a good choice for laminate and tile. We check the cutout size, seal the rim, and tighten mounting clips evenly. This is often the fastest and most affordable kitchen sink upgrade. It also works well in rentals and older homes.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Boulder

    Undermount sinks need strong clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone, and support it while the adhesive sets. Then we install strainers, traps and supply lines. A proper undermount install prevents gaps and leaks along the counter edge.
