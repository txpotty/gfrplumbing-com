    How We Install Showers in Boulder, CO

    Shower Pan Installation in Boulder

    Shower pans fail quietly. Pinholes and loose drain clamps let water into the subfloor slowly, which is why tiled pans sit flooded for a full day before tile. Prefab pans get a level check. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Boulder

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. Valve, head, handheld and drain go in first, then the pan is sloped a quarter inch per foot to the drain. Then we test. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Boulder

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator within the maker's distance limits, plumb its supply and drain and run the steam line to the head inside the enclosure. The circuit is an electrician's job. The enclosure needs a sealed door and a sloped ceiling.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Boulder, CO

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. They suit guest baths well. Most installs finish in a day.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Boulder, CO

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. Blocking goes in for grab bars, the valve goes where a seated person can reach it and a handheld goes on a slide bar. No high curbs. We follow ADA guidelines when you ask.

    Shower Stall Installation in Boulder

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. We set fiberglass and acrylic bases in a bed of mortar or setting compound so the floor of the unit does not flex underfoot, since flex is what cracks a base within a couple of years. The drain connects from below. We check the flow at the head once the trim is on.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Boulder

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Acrylic and steel tubs get mortar or foam underneath, while cast iron rests on a level ledger, which keeps the caulk line intact. Then the valve. Spout and head go at the heights you choose, and the overflow gasket gets a water test.

    Emergency Shower Replacement in Boulder

    A cracked pan or a failed valve can force a replacement fast. When water is getting under the floor or into a ceiling below, we pull the damaged unit, dry what we can and install a standard stall so the bathroom is usable again within a day or two. Custom work can wait. The framing gets inspected first.
