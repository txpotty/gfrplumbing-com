    Gas Water Heater and Tankless Line Upgrades in Boulder, CO

    Tankless water heaters often need up to 199,000 BTU, far more than the 40,000 BTU a typical tank heater uses. Many existing 1/2 inch lines cannot supply that demand, which causes error codes and lukewarm water. We calculate the full house load and upsize the line to 3/4 inch or larger as needed. This upgrade is one of the most important steps in a successful tankless conversion.

    A rotten egg or sulfur smell, a hissing sound near a line or appliance, and dead vegetation over a buried line are warning signs. If you smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility immediately.

    We use proven, industry-standard brands for gas line materials and fittings for durability and safety. This includes manufacturers like Gastite and OmegaFlex for CSST, and high-quality black iron pipe and fittings from reputable suppliers. All materials are chosen to meet or exceed local plumbing codes.

    We mainly install black iron pipe and corrugated stainless steel tubing, or CSST. Black iron is a traditional, rigid option, while CSST offers flexibility and faster installation. Both are safe and code-compliant when installed by licensed professionals, and the choice usually depends on the application and property layout.

    Gas lines usually do not need frequent inspections once installed correctly, but it is good practice to have them checked during routine plumbing inspections or if you notice unusual smells or sounds. Many newer homes across Colorado use PEX with manifold systems, which handle freezing better than rigid pipe but still require protection.
