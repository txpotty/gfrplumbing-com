    Most homeowner's insurance policies cover water damage from sudden and accidental events, like a burst pipe or water heater leak.     Our mold prevention strategy involves rapid water extraction, thorough drying with commercial-grade equipment, and applying EPA-approved antimicrobial agents to affected surfaces.
