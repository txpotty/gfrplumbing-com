    Root Intrusion Detection in Boulder, CO

    Tree roots are a common problem for sewer lines, especially with the mature trees found in many Boulder neighborhoods. Roots seek out moisture and can infiltrate even tiny cracks in pipes, growing and eventually causing severe blockages or pipe damage. Our high-resolution cameras clearly show root masses, allowing us to recommend fitting solutions like hydro-jetting or pipe repair. We use professional locators to mark the precise spot above ground.

    Detecting Pipe Damage and Corrosion in Boulder, CO

    Sewer lines can suffer from many forms of damage, including cracks, breaks, corrosion and sagging sections due to shifting soil. Our camera inspections provide a detailed visual assessment of the pipe's interior, identifying these structural issues. We can determine the severity and exact location of damage, helping you decide between spot repair, relining or full pipe replacement. Early detection is key to preventing catastrophic failures.

    Belly and Offset Detection in Boulder, CO

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds water and waste. Offsets happen when pipe sections shift out of alignment, often because of soil movement. Expansive clay soils are a well-known problem in parts of Colorado, swelling with moisture and lifting slabs, basement floors, and buried pipes. The camera shows these defects clearly, and we mark their location for targeted repair.
