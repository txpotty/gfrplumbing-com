    Gas Line and Repipe Inspections in Boulder, CO

    Safety comes first with gas lines, and our maintenance includes thorough inspections for leaks, corrosion, and proper valve operation.     Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Boulder

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get replaced. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility right away.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Boulder, CO

    Sudden summer thunderstorms bring flash flooding and hail, and heavy runoff can back up storm drains and sewer lines. A working sump pump is essential for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when unexpected storms hit Boulder.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Boulder

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is especially important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and possible breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.
