    What a Plumbing Maintenance Visit Covers in Boulder, CO

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspection in Boulder, CO

    Room by room, fixture by fixture. We check under every sink for drips, test toilet fill valves and flappers, run every faucet and shower, look at the washer hoses and read the house water pressure at a hose bib. Washers and flappers get replaced that day. Bigger items go on the report.

    Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Boulder

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life. Sediment drain, anode check, burner or element inspection and a relief valve test cover both tank and tankless. Cold showers are avoidable. We spot the signs early.

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in Boulder, CO

    A camera shows what the sewer looks like inside. Roots, sags, cracks and grease show up on camera, especially in old clay and cast iron. The recording is yours. We lay out options if something needs work.

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Boulder

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. Visible piping and connections get leak tested, shutoffs get turned, and old flex connectors get replaced. Repairs follow local code. If you smell gas, get outside before calling the gas company.

    Drain Camera Checks During Maintenance in Boulder

    Sewer problems hide underground. A camera run from the cleanout shows root intrusion, grease buildup, sags and offset joints long before they cause a backup into the lowest drain in the house. Expansive clay soils are a well-known problem in parts of Colorado, swelling with moisture and heaving slabs, basement floors, and buried pipes. You see the footage. Catching a cracked joint early can mean a spot repair instead of a full replacement.

    Softener Upkeep in Boulder, CO

    Softeners fail quietly. A softener that quit regenerating still sends water, just hard water, and you only notice when scale appears. We check the bypass and drain line. We measure hardness after.

    Getting Ready for the Hard Months in Boulder, CO

    Weak spots show up in bad weather. Hard freezes are a normal part of winter in Colorado, and pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, and garages are especially vulnerable. We find exposed pipes, unprotected outdoor faucets and lines that need draining before the tough season. Pipe sleeves cost little. Water damage costs plenty.

    Water Softener Service in Boulder

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. We test the water afterward. A failed softener sends hard water through the house without warning.
