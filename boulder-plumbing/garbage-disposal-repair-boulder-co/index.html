    Noisy Disposal Repair in Boulder

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. Loose mounting rings rattle too. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Boulder

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. The red reset button on the bottom is the first stop, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. Cabinets are damp places. In that damp cabinet, wire nuts corrode and cord connections loosen, so we replace them instead of retightening.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Boulder

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    Preventive Care and Longevity in Boulder, CO

    Run cold water before, during and after grinding, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Grease poured in with hot water coats the chamber and the pipe, while cold water keeps it solid so the impellers can break it up. Stringy peels cause most jams. Celery, artichoke leaves and corn husks belong in the trash.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Boulder, CO

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Three of the four are fixable. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Boulder

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Next we pull the object with pliers. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Boulder, CO

    Fine grinding with slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Coffee grounds and rice cause most of it. The trap comes off, the tube and arm get cleared with a properly sized cable, and we confirm the arm drops about a quarter inch per foot.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Boulder

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A steady whine is different. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Most smells start at the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.
