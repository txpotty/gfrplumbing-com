    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Boulder

    A double check valve assembly, or DCVA, uses two spring-loaded checks in series with four test cocks.     Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Boulder, CO

    Pressure vacuum breakers are the low-cost answer for lawn irrigation in Boulder, CO. They let air in when supply pressure drops, which breaks the siphon, but they only protect against back-siphonage, so they must sit above every downstream outlet and cannot be used where back pressure is possible. Clearance and drainage get checked as well.
