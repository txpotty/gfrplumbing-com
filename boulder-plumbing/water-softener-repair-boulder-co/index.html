    Resin Bed Problems in Boulder

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. New resin restores full capacity.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Boulder, CO

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. GFR Plumbing gives honest advice based on both. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. We lay out both options.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Boulder

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. GFR Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. Capacity comes back right away.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Boulder

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Boulder, CO

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. GFR Plumbing repairs these units in Boulder, CO. The media wears out in a few years.

Hard water and chlorine wear out the seals, spacers and pistons. Leaks at the valve or water running to the drain are common signs. A matching rebuild kit fixes it.
