    Resin Bed Replacement in Boulder

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a common issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. GFR Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, carefully removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in Boulder.

    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Boulder, CO

    Leaks around your water softener can be caused by cracked tanks, loose fittings, faulty O-rings, or pressure issues. Even a small leak can lead to water damage and wasted water. Our team uses specialized tools to quickly locate the source of any leak, whether it's a slow drip or a significant pool. We then perform careful repairs, from tightening connections and replacing seals to patching cracks or replacing damaged components, protecting your home from water intrusion.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Boulder

    If you're noticing scale buildup on faucets, cloudy dishes, dry skin and hair, or clothes that feel stiff after washing, your water softener probably needs attention. GFR Plumbing offers complete diagnostic services to identify the root cause of these hard water symptoms.     Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Boulder

    A common issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to inefficient regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and ensuring the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.

    Control Valve Repair in Boulder

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, directing the flow of water and initiating regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water or waste salt. GFR Plumbing technicians are experts at diagnosing and repairing common control valve issues, whether mechanical or electronic, ensuring precise timing and efficient operation. We stock common replacement parts to speed up your repair in Boulder.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Boulder, CO

    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. Our team can safely break up and remove salt bridges, clean the brine tank, and advise on best practices to prevent future occurrences. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, frequent in older systems.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Boulder, CO

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. We also recommend salt types that bridge less in warm or humid spaces.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Boulder, CO

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed usually costs $1,500 to $3,500. If the tank and valve are sound, repair generally wins. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense.
