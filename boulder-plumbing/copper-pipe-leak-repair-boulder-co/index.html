    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Boulder

    Copper pipes commonly run under concrete slabs in Boulder homes. A slab leak can be particularly challenging to detect, and shifting soil can put extra stress on those pipes. Expansive clay soils are a well-known problem in parts of Colorado, swelling with moisture and lifting slabs, basement floors, and buried pipes. We use acoustic and thermal imaging technology to locate these hidden leaks without invasive demolition.     Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Boulder, CO

    Hot water lines see more corrosion than cold lines because heat speeds up chemical reactions. Recirculation pumps that run constantly can also wear copper from the inside.     Underground Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Boulder

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Boulder

    Pinhole leaks are tiny holes that form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new Type L copper or a PEX transition. While the wall is open, we inspect nearby pipe for discoloration or thinning.

    Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Boulder

    Corrosion is a natural enemy of copper pipes and is made worse by certain water chemistries. Mountain homes in Colorado frequently rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, manganese, or natural minerals. This can lead to thinning pipe walls and eventual leaks. Our team assesses the extent of corrosion, which might show up as blue-green stains on pipes or fixtures. For localized corrosion, we perform spot repairs, but if corrosion is widespread, we may recommend re-piping sections to prevent recurring issues. We make sure all new connections are properly sealed and protected.
