    PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Boulder

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, and we cut out the bad fitting and replace it with a crimp, clamp or expansion connection rated for the pipe. Most take under an hour.

    Commercial Pipe Repair in Boulder, CO

    Offices, restaurants and apartment buildings lose money when the water is off. Timing matters. We repair larger diameter copper, CPVC and steel supply lines for Boulder businesses, planning water-off windows around business hours to keep things running. Only the affected branch goes down.

    PEX and Copper Pipe Repair in Boulder, CO

    PEX and copper fail in different ways. Copper gets pinholes. Copper thins from the inside and develops pinholes, while PEX usually fails at fittings or where it was kinked during installation, so the truck carries tools and fittings for both. Each repair is pressure tested.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Boulder

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Rusty water is a sign. Low pressure, discolored water and leaks at threaded joints tell us the pipe is closing off from the inside, so we swap the failing pipe for PEX or copper with proper fittings between metals. Pressure comes right back.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Boulder

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water chemistry causes most. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with new copper or a PEX transition. One usually means more are coming.

    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Boulder, CO

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Each fails its own way. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, so we replace failed fittings and damaged sections with parts matched to each material. Brittle CPVC needs a gentle hand.
