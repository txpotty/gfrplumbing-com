    Dependable Sump Pump Replacement Services in Boulder, CO

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Boulder

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle large water volumes. Our plumbers will assess your water intrusion patterns and recommend a pump with greater horsepower and a higher GPM (gallons per minute) rating. This prevents the pump from cycling too frequently or failing under stress, common issues in some Boulder properties.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Boulder, CO

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself might be cracked, damaged or too small. GFR Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Boulder, CO so your entire system works properly. A properly sized and intact pit is essential for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Boulder, CO

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide professional pedestal sump pump replacement in Boulder, CO, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a major concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Boulder

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Boulder

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Boulder for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, appropriately sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and maximize efficiency. That is crucial for handling heavy rainfall.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Boulder

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation stops sewer gas odors.

    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Boulder

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide redundancy. We schedule work to avoid interrupting operations.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Boulder, CO

    Some properties use pumps in yard drainage pits or below-grade window wells to handle storm runoff. These pumps may sit idle for months, then fail when they are needed most. We replace them with corrosion-resistant models and clear debris from the pit. We can route discharge away from foundations and onto approved areas. Regular testing before storm season is a good habit.
