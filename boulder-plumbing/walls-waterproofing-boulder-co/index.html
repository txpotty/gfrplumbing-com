    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Boulder

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most dependable way to stop a basement leak, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall. Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines slower, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Boulder, CO

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Boulder, CO

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Housing in Colorado ranges from older homes with galvanized and cast iron pipe to fast-growing suburbs built with copper and PEX. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. Old mortar joints get repointed first.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Boulder, CO

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Long heating seasons keep boilers and water heaters busy, so annual maintenance pays off in fewer midwinter breakdowns. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Boulder

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, a membrane over cement board or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Boulder, CO

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. It just stops puddling on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and makes sense when a patio or driveway sits against the foundation.
