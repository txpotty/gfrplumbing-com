    Drainage System Integration in Boulder, CO

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a robust drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Boulder, CO properties, so water is efficiently managed and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Boulder

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines harder, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Boulder, CO

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are particularly prone to water intrusion. Housing in Colorado ranges from older homes with galvanized and cast iron pipe to fast-growing suburbs built with plastic and PEX. Many older foundations may lack adequate exterior drainage or proper sealing. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions.     Foundation Crack Repair in Boulder

    Even small cracks in your foundation walls can allow water to seep into your basement or crawl space. Our walls waterproofing service includes foundation crack repair using proven epoxy or polyurethane injection techniques. These materials fill and seal cracks, preventing future water entry and often strengthening the foundation. Addressing cracks early in Boulder homes is crucial to avoid larger, more costly structural issues.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Boulder, CO

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to confirm the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test proves the pan is watertight before tile.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Boulder, CO

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Boulder

    A slow drip from a shower valve, supply stub or drain fitting can soak a wall for months before you notice. We locate the leak with moisture meters, thermal imaging and pressure tests. The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area surfaces.
