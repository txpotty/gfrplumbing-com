    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Boulder, CO

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Boulder

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more involved. Our office works with your HOA on approvals and shutoff times, and we take steps to protect the units next door. Building-wide jobs go in phases. For larger buildings, we repipe one stack or wing at a time so residents can stay in place.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Boulder

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.

    PEX Repiping in Boulder, CO

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. It resists scale and chlorine, handles a freeze better than copper and costs less to install, which is why most of our repipes use it. We use PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe open. Flow stays strong at the last shower.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Boulder, CO

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. The pipe gets brittle from the inside, and fittings crack, so a home that has had one polybutylene leak is usually at risk everywhere else in the system. Full replacement is the fix. Many insurers ask about it.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Boulder

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Both still turn up in older homes. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.

    Drain Line Repiping in Boulder

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. We camera the lines first. New PVC or ABS, sloped and vented properly, replaces the bad sections and drains better than the old pipe.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Boulder, CO

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. For a typical house, copper often runs 20 to 40 percent above PEX, though some buyers and homeowners like the look and feel of metal pipe and are willing to pay for it. Our quotes for either include permit, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Boulder

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. New PEX or copper stops the rust, restores pressure and ends leaks at the threads, which is where galvanized fails first. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.
