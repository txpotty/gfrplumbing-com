    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Aurora

    Gravity grease traps are a common and effective solution for many commercial kitchens in Aurora. These passive systems use the natural separation of FOG from water due to density differences. Our plumbers are experts in installing gravity grease traps, ensuring proper sizing, correct inlet and outlet piping, and convenient access for future cleaning and maintenance to get the most from their efficiency.

    Residential Grease Interceptor Setup in Aurora, CO

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Aurora

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.
