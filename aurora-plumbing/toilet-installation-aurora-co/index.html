    Types of Toilet Installation in Aurora, CO

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Aurora, CO

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. The floor underneath stays open. Cleaning gets easier, and the bowl height can be set anywhere within the carrier range, useful for a tall family. Service happens through the flush plate.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Aurora

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. An early set means the tile goes around the flange, leaving it low, and that calls for an extender ring later. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Aurora, CO

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Think about who will use it before you buy. Shorter adults and kids sometimes find the taller seat leaves their feet dangling, while anyone with bad knees or a hip replacement usually finds it much easier to sit and stand. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Aurora

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Aurora

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. A T-valve splits water off the fill line. Powered seats with warm water and a dryer need a GFCI outlet within a few feet, and if one is not there, we line up a licensed electrician before we mount anything. Non-electric seats take about thirty minutes.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Aurora, CO

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. The water connection and mounting are ours, and when an outlet has to be added we schedule a licensed electrician instead of doing that wiring. Then we test every button.

    Customer-Supplied Toilet Installation in Aurora, CO

    Bought the toilet yourself? First, look at the rough-in printed on the carton. We unbox it and look over the porcelain for shipping cracks before we pull your old one, since a hairline crack in the bowl or tank means it needs to go back to the store, not onto your floor. We bring the seal and stainless bolts.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Aurora

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. They recover instantly between flushes. We install manual and sensor-operated valves on floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, and we set the supply stop so the valve delivers its full rated volume without splashing or running on. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.
