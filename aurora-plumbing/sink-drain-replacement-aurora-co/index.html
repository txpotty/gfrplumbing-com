    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Aurora

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Most glass and stone vessel bowls have no overflow hole, so they need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, and a slotted drain in one of those bowls leaks out of the slots right into the counter hole. Mismatched drains are common. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Aurora

    Traps leak at the joints first. Slip nut washers flatten and harden with age, and a trap opened often for clogs usually has stripped threads. Trap kits are cheap. Labor is most of the bill.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Aurora

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. Old strainer putty dries out and the locknut rusts, so water runs down the tailpiece and looks like a trap leak. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Pop-Up Drain Replacement in Aurora

    Lift-rod pop-ups are the most common bath drain. We pull the old body, clean the flange seat down to bare porcelain, reset the new flange in putty or silicone, and adjust the clevis so the lift rod opens and closes the stopper with one easy motion. We match the faucet finish. Overflow-style drains are standard.
