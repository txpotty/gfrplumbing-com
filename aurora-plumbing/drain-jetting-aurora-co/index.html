    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Jetting in Aurora

    The cost of drain jetting in Aurora, CO can vary a good deal, typically ranging from $250 to $800, depending on the severity and location of the clog, the pipe size, and the length of the line.     We strongly advise against using a home pressure washer for drain jetting. These machines lack the power, specialized nozzles, and safety features of professional equipment. Improper use can damage your pipes, cause severe injury, or push clogs deeper. Always rely on trained professionals for drain jetting.

    While drain jetting focuses on clearing existing pipes, we work with reliable brands for replacement parts if needed, such as Charlotte Pipe for PVC, Cast Iron Soil Pipe Institute (CISPI) standards for cast iron, and several manufacturers for cleanout assemblies. Our focus is on the integrity of your existing drain system.

    When performed by trained professionals like GFR Plumbing, drain jetting is generally safe for most common pipe materials, including PVC, cast iron, and clay. However, extremely old, corroded, or damaged pipes may not withstand the high pressure. We always perform a camera inspection first if there's a concern about pipe condition.

    Yes, drain jetting is very effective at removing tree roots that have gotten into sewer lines. We use root-cutting nozzles that break up the roots with high-pressure water, flushing them out of the system. This provides a more thorough and lasting solution than snaking for root intrusions.

    Consumer jetter attachments for pressure washers exist, but they can cause leaks or injuries if misused. A pro has the right equipment and training.
