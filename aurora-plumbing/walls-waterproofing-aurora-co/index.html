    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Aurora, CO

    Basement Wall Sealing in Aurora, CO

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Higher elevations lower the boiling point of water, which affects water heater settings and relief valve performance in mountain homes. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Aurora

    Homes from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may still have polybutylene supply lines, which are prone to fail at fittings. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with steady hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and add drain tile wherever water collects at the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Some basements never need a sump.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Aurora, CO

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer with a line to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. The wall behind them can take a moisture-resistant board and sealer.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Aurora

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Higher elevations lower the boiling point of water, which affects water heater settings and relief valve performance in mountain homes. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Aurora

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Drainage System Integration in Aurora, CO

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Exterior footing drains, interior drain tile and surface French drains all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a gutter dumping at the corner. Downspouts get extended at least four feet out.

    A shower wall job usually takes two to four days from demolition to finished membrane, counting the drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing often wraps up in a day. Bigger foundation jobs take several days, sometimes more if it rains.
