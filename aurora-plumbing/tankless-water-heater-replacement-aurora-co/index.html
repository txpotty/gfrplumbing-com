    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Aurora

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We confirm they are sized right for the new unit. Old service valves often get replaced too.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Aurora, CO

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Before leaving a home for a winter trip, keep the heat at a safe setting and consider shutting off the main water valve. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Aurora, CO

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. The catch is gas, since most tankless units need a 3/4-inch line or bigger plus new venting. We check the meter size and pipe run before quoting, and we cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer needed. Plan on a full day.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Aurora

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger. We check meter capacity and pipe length against the manufacturer's sizing chart, then confirm pressure with the unit running. Starved units throw error codes.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Aurora, CO

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. Expect a bigger gas line, a new vent run and a wall-mounted spot for the heater. We cap or remove the old flue.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Aurora

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. Newer Rinnai models usually fit the old mounting pattern. Less drywall work that way. We match the vent type or convert to PVC on a condensing model.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Aurora

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. We match the new unit to your gas line, vent type and mounting spot whenever we can. Most swaps take four to six hours.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Aurora

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Otherwise one unit does all the work. Descaling gets scheduled by usage.
