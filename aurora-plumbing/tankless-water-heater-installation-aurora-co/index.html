    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Aurora, CO

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Aurora, CO homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage. Some need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, and a 100-amp panel often cannot carry that on top of the air conditioner and range. We check the panel first.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Aurora

    Sizing a home unit comes down to two numbers: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. With a shower at about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen tap a bit under, two showers and a sink together need around 5 or 6 gallons per minute at the needed rise. It shortens the list quickly.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Aurora

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job because the old tank spot is not always the best place. A garage or outside wall near the gas meter can shorten the gas run and simplify venting, but it may lengthen the hot water run to the bathrooms. Both distances matter. Long hot runs may call for recirculation.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Aurora, CO

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and parts are easy to get.     Commercial hot water loads in Aurora are heavier and more constant than residential. A restaurant can run a dish machine, hand sinks and a mop sink together, and the rack must hold temperature through the rush. Units share a header manifold. One can be serviced while the rest run.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Aurora

    Conversions in Aurora tend to run a full workday. We drain and remove the tank, upsize and test the gas, run a new vent through a wall or roof, then mount, plumb and fire the unit with service valves. You shower that night. The inspector comes after.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Aurora, CO

    Upfront, a tankless install in Aurora, CO costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. Over the long haul, the math narrows.
