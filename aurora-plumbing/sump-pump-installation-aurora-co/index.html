    Sump Pump Replacement in Aurora, CO

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Aurora, CO

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Clearance is usually under two feet. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Aurora, CO

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Aurora, CO

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Aurora, CO

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Parts are easy to find. We drill a 3/16 inch weep hole in the discharge to stop air lock.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Aurora

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. Lids close flat over them. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Aurora

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Aurora, CO

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary runs on house power and handles the everyday inflow, while the battery unit waits above it on a separate float. Finished basements are the usual reason. We install them in homes where a flood would ruin flooring and drywall.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Aurora

    Most Aurora, CO sump pump jobs land between $600 and $1,800. That covers the pump, the check valve and labor. Digging a pit adds cost. Every line item is on the quote.

    Our trucks carry Zoeller, Liberty, Wayne and Flotec pumps. We choose based on lift, inflow and budget.

    Plan on seven to ten years for a well-kept pump. A pump that cycles every few minutes through a wet spring wears out faster than one that runs a few times a month, and cheap switches fail first. We can check yours.

    Doing it yourself is possible but risky. Getting the pit depth, the check valve orientation, the discharge route and the outlet wiring right takes experience, and one mistake shows up during the next storm. This is daily work for us.

    Grinding, rattling or a pump that runs with no water in the pit are all warning signs. Rust on the housing is another. If the basement flooded anyway, it is time.
