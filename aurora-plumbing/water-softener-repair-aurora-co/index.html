    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Aurora

    A common issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to inefficient regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and ensuring the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering targeted repairs or replacements.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Aurora, CO

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for leaks.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Aurora

    If you're noticing scale buildup on faucets, cloudy dishes, dry skin and hair, or clothes that feel stiff after washing, your water softener likely needs attention.     Resin Bed Replacement in Aurora

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a common issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. GFR Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, properly removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in Aurora.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Aurora, CO

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed usually costs $1,500 to $3,500. If the tank and valve are sound, repair generally wins. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Aurora, CO

    While less common than traditional softeners, no-salt water conditioners also require maintenance and occasional repair. These systems work by conditioning water to prevent scale buildup without removing minerals. If you notice scale returning or reduced water pressure, your no-salt system may need attention. GFR Plumbing is experienced in diagnosing and repairing issues with these unique units in Aurora, CO, ensuring they continue to protect your plumbing and appliances effectively.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Aurora, CO

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and initiating regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water, regenerate too often, or leak. We diagnose issues like stuck pistons, worn out seals, motor failures, or programming errors. Our plumbers carry a range of replacement parts for popular brands like Fleck, Clack, and Autotrol, ensuring a quick and effective repair to restore proper operation.
