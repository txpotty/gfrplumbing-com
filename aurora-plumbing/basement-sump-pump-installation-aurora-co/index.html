    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Aurora

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. A battery backup keeps a second pump running without house power, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We test it under load.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Aurora

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. The motor stays dry and you can reach it without pulling the pump, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Aurora homes. They are louder, though.
