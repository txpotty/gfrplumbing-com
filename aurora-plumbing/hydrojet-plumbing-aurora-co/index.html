    Understanding Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Aurora, CO

    While snaking can punch a hole through a clog, hydrojetting in Aurora, CO provides a complete pipe cleaning. Snaking is effective for simple, localized clogs, but it often leaves behind residual buildup that can lead to recurring problems. Hydrojetting, by contrast, scours the entire interior of the pipe, removing debris, grease, and scale, offering a more long-term solution to your drainage issues.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Aurora

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under floors and slabs. Snaking clears a path, but the grease quickly builds back. Jetting with a rotating nozzle peels that layer off and flushes it downstream. Homeowners who cook often and restaurants benefit most from regular kitchen line jetting.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Aurora

    Tree roots are a common problem for sewer lines in Aurora, particularly with older homes and mature landscaping. Roots seek out moisture and can enter even tiny cracks, growing into dense masses that cause severe blockages and damage. Our hydrojet plumbing service uses specialized root-cutting nozzles to slice through these stubborn intrusions and wash them away, leaving your sewer line clear. This method is often more effective than traditional augers for complete root removal.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Aurora, CO

    Snaking a main line usually costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 including camera inspection. Commercial lines and long runs can cost $800 to $2,000 or more. Jetting costs more up front, but it often prevents repeat calls that add up over a year. We help you decide which method suits your line and budget.
