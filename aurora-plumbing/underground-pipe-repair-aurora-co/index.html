    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Aurora

    Simple buried pipe repairs usually run $500 to $2,500. Sewer replacements cost more. Extensive trenchless sewer replacements can be $5,000 to $15,000 or more, depending on where the pipe runs, how bad the damage is and the method used to fix it. The price comes after we look.

    Shallow irrigation leaks are sometimes a fair DIY job. Main lines are not. Main water lines and sewer laterals require locating, utility marks and code-compliant fittings rated for burial, so those should go to a licensed plumber. Any dig needs 811 first.

    Acoustic ground microphones pick up the sound of escaping water. Tracer gas helps too. Hydrogen-nitrogen gas pushed into the line rises through the soil at the leak, and electronic locators trace the exact pipe route so we know where to listen. The mark is usually very close.

    A spot repair is usually a few hours. Trenchless jobs take about a day. A full sewer replacement with excavation can take two to five days, with footage, depth and inspections setting the pace. We put the schedule in writing.

    Check the meter with every fixture off. Watch the dial. If it moves, water is going somewhere, with soggy ground or a hiss near the meter often pointing to the main line. A locate visit settles it.

    Not for main lines or sewer laterals. It goes wrong easily. Digging near gas and electric lines, working in an unshored trench and using fittings not rated for burial all lead to injuries, repeat leaks or contaminated drinking water. Leave it to a pro.

    Many utilities offer a one-time leak adjustment. It is worth asking. Most want proof that the leak was repaired promptly, so we hand you paperwork showing what was fixed and when. Policies vary by utility.

    For water lines we use PEX or Type K copper. Each is approved for direct bury. Sewer and drain work uses Schedule 40 PVC, and trenchless jobs use fused HDPE, all of which resist corrosion and handle ground movement better than the old materials they replace. We follow your city's code.
