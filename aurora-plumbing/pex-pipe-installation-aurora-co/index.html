    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Aurora, CO

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.
