    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in Aurora

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit. Attic units need working drain pans and drain lines to prevent ceiling damage if the tank leaks. We check the pan, drain line, venting and access platform. Garage units should be raised and protected where required for gas appliances. We confirm your setup meets local code.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Aurora

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for any leaks.

    Emergency Water Heater Inspection in Aurora, CO

    If your water heater is acting up and you're unsure why, our emergency inspection service in Aurora, CO can diagnose the problem fast. While not a full maintenance visit, we can identify issues like strange noises, sudden temperature drops, or leaks that point to an immediate need for repair. We'll then provide an straight assessment and options for repair or replacement.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Aurora, CO

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge where installed.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Aurora

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than you might think. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is crucial for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.
