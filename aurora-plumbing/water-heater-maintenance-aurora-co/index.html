    What a Water Heater Tune-Up Covers in Aurora, CO

    Anode Rod Checks in Aurora

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. We pull it and look. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Commercial Tank and Tankless Service in Aurora, CO

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Aurora, CO

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. Leaky valves get replaced. Expansion tanks get an air charge check.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Aurora

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Depending on your water chemistry, a rod that should last five years might be spent in three, so we pull it on every visit instead of assuming it is fine because the tank is still young. Spent rods get replaced. A healthy rod keeps the tank from rusting through.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Aurora, CO

    Small drips become floods. Connections, the relief valve, the drain valve and the seams get checked, along with the pan and floor. Rust stains are a warning. Catching it early saves the floor.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Aurora

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. We flush until it runs clear. Bad tanks need a few rounds.
