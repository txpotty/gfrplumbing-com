    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Aurora

    Reverse osmosis systems remove dissolved solids, which can be high in some water supplies. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet. Some systems also connect to refrigerators or ice makers. RO pairs well with a softener for complete water treatment. We change filters on a schedule so the system keeps working well.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Aurora

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. GFR Plumbing carefully installs various models of salt-based softeners, making sure of proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Aurora home.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Aurora, CO

    Pelican Water Systems offer a variety of water treatment solutions, including both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners. If you've chosen a Pelican system for your Aurora, CO home, GFR Plumbing can provide expert installation. Our plumbers make sure of proper sizing, secure connections and correct startup procedures to maximize the effectiveness and lifespan of your Pelican water softener or conditioner.
