    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Aurora

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Aurora. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. GFR Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Aurora, CO

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Aurora, CO

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Aurora, CO, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Aurora

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.
