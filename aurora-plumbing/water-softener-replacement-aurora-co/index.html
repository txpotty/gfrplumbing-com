    Softener Loop Installation in Aurora, CO

    Many homes have a softener loop near the main water line that makes for easy installation. When a home lacks one, we can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. We also route a drain line to a laundry box, floor drain or approved location. A good loop keeps outdoor hose bibs on hard water to save salt. This makes future service simpler.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Aurora

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore steady brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the right salt type for your system.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Aurora

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water around the clock. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but prevent hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Aurora, CO

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They require no drain and no salt. We explain the differences plainly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Aurora

    The cost of water softener replacement in Aurora, CO varies based on the type of system, its capacity and installation complexity.     Spots on dishes, scale on faucets, dry skin and increased soap use can point to a failing softener. A softener that no longer uses salt or keeps flooding the brine tank may also need replacement.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had heavy mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can look into other causes.

    While it may seem like a straightforward task, water softener replacement involves working with water lines, drain connections and sometimes electrical components. Improper installation can lead to leaks, inefficient operation or even water damage. Hiring a professional ensures the job is done correctly, safely and to code.

    We install systems with Fleck, Clack and Kinetico valves, among others. These brands have good parts availability and reliable performance.

    Salt-free conditioners reduce scale buildup but do not remove hardness minerals. A true softener removes them, which is more effective for soap and fixtures.

    Many systems include 5 to 10 years on the valve and longer coverage on tanks. We also provide a warranty on our labor.
