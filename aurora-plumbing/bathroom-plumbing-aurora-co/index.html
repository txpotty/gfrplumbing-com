    Low water pressure can come from several issues, including mineral buildup in aerators or showerheads, a failing pressure regulator, leaks in the water line, or issues with your water heater. Our plumbers can diagnose the exact cause.

    Most drips come from a worn cartridge or seal. Hard water scale can damage cartridges and cause leaks sooner. Replacing the cartridge typically solves the problem.

    The lifespan of bathroom plumbing fixtures varies, with toilets lasting 20 to 50 years, faucets 15 to 20 years, and shower valves 20 to 30 years. However, factors like water quality, frequency of use, and maintenance can affect these timelines. Many larger systems in Colorado draw on mountain snowmelt that runs fairly soft, while plains communities and groundwater sources often deliver harder water.     Most repairs take one to two hours. Toilet replacement takes about two hours, and shower valve replacement typically takes half a day.

    Fixture repairs and like-for-like replacements usually do not need a permit. Moving drains, adding fixtures or remodeling often does. We take care of permits when required.

    Replacing a standard shower head is often a simple DIY task. However, if you are experiencing low water pressure, leaks from the wall, or plan to install a complex multi-function shower system, it is best to call a professional for proper installation and to prevent leaks.

    Regular maintenance is key. Avoid flushing anything but toilet paper, use drain strainers to catch hair in sinks and showers, and fix minor leaks promptly. Consider a water softener if you have hard water, to reduce mineral buildup in fixtures and pipes.
