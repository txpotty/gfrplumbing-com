    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Aurora

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear. We make sure these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Aurora residence.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Aurora, CO

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Aurora, CO.

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails.
