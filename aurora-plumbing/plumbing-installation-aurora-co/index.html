    Gas Line Installation for New Appliances in Aurora, CO

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Installation in Aurora

    Fire lines and irrigation systems account for most of the backflow assemblies we install. A certified tester checks each assembly on install day and the results go to the water provider.     Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Aurora

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. One moves groundwater, the other moves sewage, and they are not interchangeable. We size before we sell. The pump gets matched to the fill rate, wired to a dedicated circuit and piped out through a check valve.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Aurora, CO

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. Our licensed plumbers size each run from the appliance BTU ratings and the developed length, then choose black iron or CSST to suit the route. Code requires CSST to be bonded. Skip the bond and a nearby lightning strike can arc through the tubing, which is one more reason gas work is not a weekend project.

    Gas Line Installation in Aurora, CO

    A new range or an outdoor kitchen all start with the same math: total BTU load and the length of the run. From there we size the pipe, choose between black iron and CSST, and dig the trench for any buried section. Every joint gets a soap test. After that, the line sits on a pressure gauge until the inspector signs off.

    PEX Repiping in Aurora

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. It runs quieter than copper, with less water hammer banging in the walls. It also handles a freeze better. Hard freezes are a normal part of winter in Colorado, and pipes in exterior walls, crawlspaces, and garages are particularly vulnerable. Fittings are matched to the tubing manufacturer.

    Water Softener Installation in Aurora, CO

    Mountain homes in Colorado frequently rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, manganese, or natural minerals. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. We test hardness in grains per gallon at the kitchen tap, then size a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner to your household use. The bypass valve matters. It lets you shut the unit off for service without losing water to the house.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Aurora

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. Repiping in PEX stops the cycle. We run either a manifold with home runs to each fixture, which balances pressure, or a trunk-and-branch layout when the walls make home runs impractical. Access holes are kept small and patched, or left square and clean for your drywall contractor.
