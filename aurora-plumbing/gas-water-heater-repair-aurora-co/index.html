    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Aurora

    Strange noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can add to sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, improving efficiency and quiet operation.

    Emergency Gas Water Heater Repair in Aurora

    When your gas water heater suddenly stops working, especially on a busy morning, it is an emergency. GFR Plumbing offers prompt emergency repair services for gas water heaters in Aurora.     Pilot Light Repair in Aurora

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most frequent gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Aurora, CO

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is essential for your family's safety.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Aurora, CO

    The gas control valve controls temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something fails. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and verify temperature control afterward.

    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Aurora

    A worn burner assembly can result in poor heating or unusual noises. We closely inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear. We also check the venting system for proper draft and obstructions, which is essential for safe operation and preventing carbon monoxide buildup in your Aurora home. Proper venting is a key safety consideration.

    Tank Leaks & Pressure Problems in Aurora

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure. Around Aurora, a yearly water heater flush helps clear the sediment that collects at the bottom of the tank. While some leaks might require a replacement, minor leaks or pressure relief valve issues can often be repaired. Our team checks the extent of the damage, inspects the anode rod and addresses pressure imbalances to prevent further complications in your Aurora home.

    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Aurora, CO

    Issues with the gas valve or thermostat can lead to inconsistent water temperatures or a complete lack of hot water. Our team can test these key components for proper operation, checking for electrical continuity and gas flow. If a part is defective, we will replace it with a quality, manufacturer-approved component so your water heater heats evenly and safely.
