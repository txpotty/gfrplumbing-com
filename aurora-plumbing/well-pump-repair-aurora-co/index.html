    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Aurora

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a stubborn problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts careful diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Aurora home, ensuring a targeted and effective repair solution.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Aurora, CO

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Drought cycles are common here, so water-efficient fixtures and careful irrigation choices are part of most plumbing upgrades. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.

    Jet Pump Repair in Aurora, CO

    Jet pumps, typically installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Typical issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear. We troubleshoot problems like air in the lines, clogged foot valves, or faulty pressure switches. Our team in Aurora is skilled at restoring prime and replacing worn components to get your jet pump operating smoothly again, a frequent need where water levels rise and fall.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Aurora, CO

    Franklin Electric is a leading manufacturer of well pumps and motors, known for their durability. Our technicians are very familiar with Franklin Electric products, from submersible motors to control boxes. We carry common Franklin Electric replacement parts to ensure efficient repairs. Whether it's a motor rewind or a complete pump replacement, you can trust our expertise with Franklin Electric systems in Aurora.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Aurora, CO

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. GFR Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Aurora operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Aurora, CO

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps prematurely. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives steadier pressure at the tap.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Aurora

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.
