    Common Toilet Replacements in Aurora, CO

    Toilet Brands We Replace in Aurora

    Brand matters less than model, because every major maker sells a great toilet and a mediocre one at nearly the same price. Our picks come down to flush valve size and the MaP score. Parts availability counts too. Proprietary flappers and fill valves mean that ten years from now, a simple fix becomes a special order.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Aurora

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Parts are easy to find. We set it level with shims if the floor is out, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Aurora

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. You can pick battery or hardwired sensors. We do the work after hours if needed, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Aurora

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. That is when we swap them. The old unit comes off, the flange gets scraped clean and checked for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Aurora, CO

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for 800 or higher. A high score means fewer double flushes and fewer clogs, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Aurora

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. The drain stays the same. We often pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in Aurora

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. We check it every time. For a cracked flange, a stainless repair ring is often enough, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Aurora

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. You should replace it. The cracked unit comes out, and we look at the floor under it before setting the new one, since a slow crack leak often soaks the subfloor first.
