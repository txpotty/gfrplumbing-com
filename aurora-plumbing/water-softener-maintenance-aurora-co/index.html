    Dependable Water Softener Maintenance Services in Aurora, CO

    Salt-Free Conditioner Maintenance in Aurora

    Template assisted crystallization and other salt-free conditioners do not remove hardness but change how it behaves. They still need media replacement every few years and sediment prefilter changes much more often. We inspect the media tank, replace prefilters and check bypass valves. We also explain honestly how these systems differ from a true softener.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Aurora, CO

    The brine tank is essential for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can build up, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a frequent issue we address in Aurora homes.

    Hardness Test and Water Quality Check in Aurora

    As part of our maintenance, we conduct a water hardness test to verify your water softener is effectively reducing mineral content to proper levels. This ensures you are receiving the soft water benefits you expect and allows us to fine-tune your system if necessary. We aim for consistent water quality in every Aurora home we serve.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Aurora

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs far less than a new system.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in Aurora, CO

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can build up in your brine tank, hindering the softening process. Our maintenance includes thoroughly cleaning the brine tank, inspecting for any cracks or leaks and confirming the float valve operates correctly. This step is crucial for the efficient regeneration of your water softener in Aurora, CO.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Aurora

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, dictating regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, avoiding the need for a costly full system replacement in Aurora.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Maintenance in Aurora

    The cost of water softener maintenance in Aurora, CO usually ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the system's condition and the services required. Factors like cleaning a heavily fouled brine tank or replacing minor worn parts can influence the final price. We provide a detailed quote before starting any work.

    We recommend professional water softener maintenance at least once a year. For homes with very hard water or high water usage, a twice-yearly check-up might be worthwhile to support good performance and longevity of the unit.

    Maintenance usually includes inspecting the brine tank for salt bridges, cleaning the tank, checking and cleaning the resin bed, lubricating moving parts, sanitizing the system and running diagnostic checks on the control valve. We also test water hardness to verify effectiveness.

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank, preventing salt from dissolving into the water. This means the system can't regenerate properly, leading to hard water. Our maintenance service includes breaking up and removing salt bridges, bringing back your softener's function.

    Most visits take about an hour, including testing and a manual regeneration. Resin replacement can add another hour or a bit more.
