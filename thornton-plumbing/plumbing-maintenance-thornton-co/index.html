    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Thornton

    Tankless units need descaling. Hard water scales the heat exchanger until the unit runs lukewarm or shuts off mid-shower. We pump descaler through the service valves. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Water Heater Flush and Inspection in Thornton

    Popping or rumbling means sediment. Minerals settle on the tank bottom, trap heat and cause the popping you hear when it fires. Flushing clears it. We descale tankless units through their service valves. Regular service helps keep the warranty valid.

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Sudden summer thunderstorms bring flash flooding and hail, and heavy runoff can overwhelm storm drains and sewer lines.     Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Thornton

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life. Sediment drain, anode check, burner or element inspection and a relief valve test cover both tank and tankless. Cold showers are avoidable. A yearly tune-up catches the warning signs.

    Softener Upkeep in Thornton, CO

    Softeners fail quietly. We check the bypass and drain line. Then we test the treated water.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Thornton

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast.
