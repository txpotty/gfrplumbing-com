    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Thornton

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines harder, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Thornton

    Many homes in Thornton have crawl spaces, which are very prone to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting prevents ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Thornton

    Housing in Colorado ranges from older homes with galvanized and cast iron pipe to fast-growing suburbs built with copper and PEX. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks. We seal cracks with injection products, apply interior or exterior waterproofing and install drainage where needed. A sump pump may be added if groundwater collects.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Thornton

    Even small cracks in your foundation walls can allow water to seep into your basement or crawl space. Our walls waterproofing service includes foundation crack repair using proven epoxy or polyurethane injection techniques.     Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Thornton, CO

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to confirm the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test confirms the pan is watertight before tile.

    Drainage System Integration in Thornton, CO

Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Thornton, CO properties, so water is managed well and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.
