    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings.     Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Thornton, CO

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Many insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to insure and sell.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Thornton

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes, a full home PEX repipe can drastically improve your plumbing system. We remove outdated galvanized, copper or polybutylene pipes and replace them with durable PEX, upgrading your entire water delivery system. This prevents future leaks, improves water pressure and ends the rust-colored water often seen with older piping in Thornton.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Thornton

    When your main water line from the street to your home needs replacement, PEX is an excellent option. Its flexibility allows for easier trenchless or minimal-dig installations, limiting disruption to your yard in Thornton. PEX main lines handle ground movement and resist corrosion better than rigid pipe. Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines harder, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Thornton, CO

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Thornton

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Many larger systems in Colorado draw on mountain snowmelt that runs fairly soft, while plains communities and groundwater sources often deliver harder water. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.
