    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Thornton, CO

    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are crucial for preventing leaks. Over time, these rubber components can degrade, crack, or become brittle due to age and exposure to water, especially where the water is hard. We specialize in replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Thornton

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet much quieter.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Thornton, CO
