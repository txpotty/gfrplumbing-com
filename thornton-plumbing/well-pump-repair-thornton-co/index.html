    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive typically shows a code that guides diagnosis.     Submersible Well Pump Repair in Thornton

    Submersible pumps are common in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms usually include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use dedicated equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, protecting the integrity of your well system.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Thornton

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Thornton, CO

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Sudden summer thunderstorms bring flash flooding and hail, and heavy runoff can overwhelm storm drains and sewer lines. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.
