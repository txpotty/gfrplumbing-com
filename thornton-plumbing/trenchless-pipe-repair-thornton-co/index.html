    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. That calls for a sectional liner.     Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Thornton

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. Then the liner goes in. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Same-Day Sewer Assessment in Thornton, CO

    Sewer lines that fail suddenly can often be cleared and scoped the same day. Then we schedule the fix. When lining fits, the repair gets booked fast, and until then we keep it flowing and tell you what not to send down the drain.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Thornton

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Thornton yard.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Thornton

    Older Thornton homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. They wear out. Roots get into clay joints and cast iron rusts from inside, and a liner seals over both with jointless pipe that should outlast the original. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Pipe Bursting in Thornton, CO

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.
