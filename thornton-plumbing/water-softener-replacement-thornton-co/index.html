    Metered Softener Replacement in Thornton

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first month.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Thornton

    Restaurants, salons and small businesses rely on soft water to protect dishwashers, espresso machines and water heaters. We replace commercial softeners sized to flow rate and daily usage.     Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Thornton

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Thornton.
