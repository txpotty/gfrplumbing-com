    What We Fix Under the Sink in Thornton, CO

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Thornton, CO

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. In a warm cabinet that core can fail after five to ten years and burst with no warning, and the multi-turn stop above it often will not close when you need it. Lines and stops get replaced together. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Thornton, CO

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Code calls for a trap seal between 2 and 4 inches, and a trap that leaks, sags or was cut down by a past owner can lose that seal and let the smell in. We replace bad traps with a matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Thornton

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Thornton

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. From below we turn the flywheel with a 1/4 inch hex key, hit the reset button and look for a bent impeller. Leaks are a different story. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Thornton

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down all day, and nobody checks the trap until water is on the carpet. A strainer basket goes in after we clear it. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Thornton, CO

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. Either the clevis slipped on the lift rod or hair has wrapped the stopper so it will not seat. Cleaning fixes half of them. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Thornton, CO

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A burst supply line can dump gallons a minute, and we answer at night and on weekends with a stocked truck. Close the angle stops while you wait. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Thornton, CO

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. Pop-up linkage parts sit in a damp cabinet all day, and the pivot nut is the leak we find most often on bathroom sinks. Pedestal and wall-hung sinks get the same checks. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Local Sink Repair Near Me in Thornton, CO

    A local plumber knows the houses. Older neighborhoods tend to have galvanized stubs and chrome traps, while newer subdivisions have PEX and plastic traps with compression stops, so we bring parts for both. That cuts out a supply-house run.

    Some sink problems look local but are not. If the sink gurgles, the vent may be blocked, and if it backs up when the washer drains, the shared line is clogged. We check before we quote.
