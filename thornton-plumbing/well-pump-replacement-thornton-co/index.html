    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Thornton

    Well pump replacement typically costs $1,800 to $4,500 depending on well depth and pump size. Deep wells or constant pressure systems can cost $4,000 to $8,000.

    We install Grundfos, Goulds and Franklin Electric pumps. These brands are reliable and widely supported.

    Permit requirements vary by county. We follow local requirements for all well work.

    Well pump replacement is a complex job that involves high voltage electricity, heavy equipment and possibly deep well access. It calls for specialized tools and experience for proper installation, electrical safety and compliance with local plumbing codes. It is strongly recommended to hire a licensed professional to avoid injury, property damage or costly mistakes.

    It is often a good idea to consider replacing your pressure tank at the same time as your well pump, especially if both are of similar age. A new pressure tank can improve the efficiency and lifespan of your new pump by reducing cycling. It also cuts labor costs to do both jobs at once.

    Shallow well pumps, or jet pumps, are designed for wells where the water level is usually 25 feet or less from the pump, using suction to draw water. Deep well pumps, or submersible pumps, are placed directly in the water near the bottom of the well and push water up, suited for depths over 25 feet. Choosing the correct type is critical for efficiency and performance.

    In many areas, permits may be required for significant plumbing work like well pump replacement, especially if it involves changing the well system's electrical or structural components. GFR Plumbing will help you work through any permitting in Thornton, CO so your installation is fully compliant with local regulations.

    If your pressure tank is old or waterlogged, replacing it at the same time can protect the new pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most frequent reasons submersible motors burn out early.
