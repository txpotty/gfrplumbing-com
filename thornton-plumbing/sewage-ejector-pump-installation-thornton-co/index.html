    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Thornton

    The cost for sewage ejector pump installation in Thornton usually ranges from $2,500 to $5,000. This price can vary based on the pump type, basin size, complexity of the plumbing and electrical work and whether an existing system needs to be removed. We provide a detailed quote after assessing your project.

    A sump pump is designed to remove groundwater from a basement or crawl space, preventing flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is specifically engineered to handle wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in areas below the main sewer line. They are not interchangeable and serve separate purposes.

    We recommend having your sewage ejector pump inspected annually, especially if it is in a high-use area. Regular inspections can identify potential issues early, extend the life of your pump and prevent unexpected failures and costly backups. This is particularly important for older units.

    Regular maintenance for a sewage ejector pump involves occasionally checking the basin for debris, confirming the check valve works correctly and testing the float switch. It is also wise to avoid flushing non-biodegradable items. GFR Plumbing offers maintenance services to keep your pump running efficiently and prevent surprise breakdowns.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 1 to 5 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Avoid flushing wipes, paper towels, feminine products, grease and cat litter. These items clog pumps and cause early failure.

    A sump pump removes groundwater or seepage, while an ejector pump handles sewage and wastewater from fixtures. Ejector pumps are designed to pass solids and need a sealed, vented basin.

    New ejector installations usually require a plumbing permit and inspection. Straight pump replacements may not, and we confirm local requirements for each job.
