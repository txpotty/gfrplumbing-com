    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Thornton

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Thornton that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Thornton

    The cost of trenchless sewer line repair in Thornton, CO can vary a lot, typically ranging from $4,000 to $15,000 or more, depending on the length of the pipe, the method used and the complexity of the job. Factors like pipe diameter and accessibility also play a role, so a professional assessment is key for an accurate estimate.

    While trenchless methods are very versatile, they are not suitable for all situations. For instance, if a section of the pipe has completely collapsed or if there is severe misalignment that prevents the insertion of new pipe or liner, traditional excavation might be necessary. A camera inspection will determine feasibility.

    A properly installed liner has no joints for roots to enter, so root intrusion is essentially eliminated in the lined section.

    Lines that are cracked, root-infested or corroded but still hold their shape are typically good candidates for lining. Collapsed or badly offset lines may need pipe bursting or a spot excavation.

    For pipe bursting, we typically use high-density polyethylene pipe, known for its strength, flexibility and resistance to corrosion and roots. For CIPP relining, a felt liner saturated with epoxy resin, such as products from Perma-Liner or NuFlow, is used to create a new, seamless pipe within the existing pipe.

    Most trenchless sewer line repairs can be completed in one to three days, much faster than traditional excavation methods, which can take a week or more. The exact timeline depends on the length of the pipe, the severity of the damage and the specific trenchless technique used.

    Rocky mountain ground makes trenching for sewer and water lines harder, and lines must be buried below the local frost depth. Soils that shift with changes in moisture can put significant stress on underground pipes, leading to cracks, breaks and misalignments. Trenchless methods, especially pipe bursting with flexible HDPE pipe, are highly effective at reducing future damage caused by soil movement.

    Trenchless repair is very versatile and effective for issues like cracks, fractures, root intrusion and minor collapses.
