    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Maintenance in Greeley

    A standard tank water heater flush and inspection in Greeley usually costs $100 to $250. Tankless descaling typically runs $150 to $300, and anode rod replacement adds about $75 to $200 including the part.

    Most tank flushes and inspections take about 45 to 90 minutes. Tankless descaling usually takes 1 to 2 hours.

    With hard water, flushing once a year is recommended. Homes with very hard water or no softener may benefit from flushing every six months.

    While some basic tasks like flushing the tank might seem DIY-friendly, professional maintenance makes sure all key components, such as the anode rod, pressure relief valve, and heating elements, are properly inspected. Incorrect DIY methods can damage your unit or cancel warranties.

    The anode rod is a metal rod inside your water heater that sacrifices itself to keep the steel tank from corroding. It attracts corrosive elements in the water. Regular inspection and replacement of a depleted anode rod are crucial for extending the lifespan of your water heater, especially where water quality varies.
