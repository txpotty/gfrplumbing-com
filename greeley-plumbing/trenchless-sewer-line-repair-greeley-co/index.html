    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Greeley, CO

    Sewer lines in Greeley, CO can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Basements are common in Colorado, and shifting soil can crack floor drains, sewer lines, and basement walls over time. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Greeley, CO

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is especially useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.

    Pipe Bursting in Greeley, CO

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is best for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It typically requires an entry pit and an exit pit.
