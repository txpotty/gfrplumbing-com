    Walk-In Shower Installation in Greeley

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. We set that slope. Grab bar blocking goes in while the walls are open, so bars can be added later without hunting for studs.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Greeley, CO

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. Blocking goes in for grab bars, the valve goes where a seated person can reach it and a handheld goes on a slide bar. No high curbs. We follow ADA guidelines when you ask.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Greeley, CO

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. On a slab, we cut concrete. The new shower is safer to step into.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Greeley

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area.
