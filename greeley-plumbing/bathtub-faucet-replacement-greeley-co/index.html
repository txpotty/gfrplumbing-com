    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Greeley, CO

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Tub Spout Replacement in Greeley

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. We check yours before buying. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Greeley

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.
