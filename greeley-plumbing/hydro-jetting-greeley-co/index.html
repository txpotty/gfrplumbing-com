    High-Pressure Water Jetting for Drain Cleaning in Greeley

    High-pressure water jetting, or hydro jetting, is a non-invasive method that uses strongly pressurized water to clear and clean pipes. Our equipment in Greeley can generate water pressures up to 4,000 PSI, powerful enough to break apart stubborn blockages and scour pipe walls clean without chemicals. This method is effective for many pipe materials, from PVC to cast iron, and is an eco-friendly choice for deep drain cleaning.

    Scale Removal in Cast Iron Pipes in Greeley

    Cast iron drains in older homes build up rust tubercles and mineral scale, and hard water speeds that process. Jetting with the right nozzle can strip much of this buildup and restore flow. We keep pressure in a safe range for aged iron and watch the camera for thin spots. This cleaning is also the standard prep step before pipe lining. Clean pipe walls help a liner bond properly.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydro Jetting in Greeley

    Tree roots are a common problem in Greeley, CO, especially where mature landscaping grows over older sewer lines. Roots can work into tiny cracks in pipes and grow, causing severe blockages.     Drain snaking uses a cable with an auger that punches a hole through a blockage to restore some flow. Hydro jetting offers a more thorough and longer-lasting clean.

    While hydro jetting is very powerful, it is usually safe for most sound plumbing systems, including PVC, cast iron, and concrete pipes.
