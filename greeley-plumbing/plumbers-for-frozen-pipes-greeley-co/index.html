    When a faucet only trickles or nothing comes out after a cold night, ice is blocking the line. We open the faucet and find the frozen section. Metal pipe thaws fast with electric current, and plastic lines get slower, gentler heat. Patience pays here.

    Urgent Frozen Pipe Thawing in Greeley

    Time matters once a line freezes, because the longer ice sits, the more pressure builds behind it. Open the faucet on that line now. Most frozen lines flow again within an hour of arrival, and every one gets a pressure test.

    Burst Pipe Repair in Greeley, CO

    Water expands as it freezes, and that pressure can split copper, crack PVC fittings and pop PEX off a crimp ring. The split section comes out. Where one split appears, others often follow along the same run, so we pressure test the full branch and replace any section that bulged or thinned instead of patching only the obvious leak.

    Frozen Water Line Diagnosis in Greeley, CO

    Finding the frozen spot is the hard part. A thermal camera speeds it up. By checking which fixtures work and which do not, then scanning walls and ceilings with the camera, we can usually narrow the ice to a few feet of pipe and thaw it without cutting into the wall.
