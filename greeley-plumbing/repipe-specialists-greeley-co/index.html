    Slab Leak Repiping in Greeley

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Expansive clay soils are a well-known problem in parts of Colorado, swelling with moisture and lifting slabs, basement floors, and buried pipes. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    Copper Repiping in Greeley, CO

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Copper takes a bit longer. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a day longer than PEX, mostly because every turn needs a fitting.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Greeley

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. New PEX or copper stops the rust, restores pressure and ends leaks at the threads, which is where galvanized fails first. We disconnect and abandon the old pipe where removing it would mean tearing out finished walls.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Greeley

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. This works well for a first leak. If the house has had several leaks, we will walk through why a full repipe usually costs less over time.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Greeley, CO

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. The pipe gets brittle from the inside, and fittings crack, so a home that has had one polybutylene leak is usually at risk everywhere else in the system. We replace the full system with PEX or copper. Many insurers ask about it.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in Greeley

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it. First we stop the leak and get water back on with a temporary fix, then we schedule the repipe without pressure. That gives you time. Insurance may cover the water damage from the burst even if it does not cover the repipe itself, so we document what we find with photos.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Greeley

Bigger homes may need a 1-inch trunk so the shower does not drop when the dishwasher fills.
