    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Greeley

    Residential expansion tank installs usually cost $250 to $550 with the tank and labor. Add an attic install, a bigger tank or a new PRV and it can reach $600 to $1,000.

    Our installs come with a labor warranty, and the tank carries the manufacturer's coverage. We explain both at the time of service, including how long the tank is covered and what to do if it ever goes waterlogged before the warranty runs out. Keep the paperwork.

    Heated water expands. On a closed system it has nowhere to go, so pressure climbs every cycle and wears out heater components and relief valves, sometimes causing leaks, while the expansion tank gives that water an air cushion to push against instead. The air side compresses.

    A dripping T and P valve on the water heater is the most common symptom. Pressure that swings up and down is another, and a waterlogged tank sounds dull all the way up when you tap it and feels heavy, which means the diaphragm has failed. Then it needs replacing.

    Water spitting from the air valve is the clearest sign. Other clues are rust on the tank, banging pipes or pressure that swings, since all of those show up when the tank stops absorbing expansion and pressure starts climbing again. Tap it to check.
