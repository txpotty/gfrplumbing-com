    A thermocouple is a small copper-sheathed rod that sits in the pilot flame and makes a tiny electric current. That current holds a magnet in the gas valve open. No flame, no current. When the pilot goes out, the magnet lets go and the valve shuts off gas to both the pilot and the burner, which is the safety that keeps raw gas from filling a closet. Weak thermocouples cause more pilot outages than anything else.
